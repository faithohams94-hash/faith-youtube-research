# Rise & Ruin: Visual Style Guide (illustrated)

The main video style is an **illustrated 2D animated documentary**. It's informed by the GoDocs frame study (`../channel-research/godocs/analysis.md`) and made distinct by the **Rise/Ruin colour shift**, the **red-eye-bar leader** and a **real data layer** of maps and charts.

The stickman cast (`characters/`) is kept for Shorts diagrams and simple chart gags only.

## 1. The look
- Clean black ink outlines, flat cel shading on characters, soft painterly textured backgrounds.
- Expressive adult-cartoon characters with simple features. Clearly cartoon, never photorealistic.
- Cinematic framing with shallow depth of field: the subject is sharp and the background softly blurred.
- One idea per frame, and no text in the artwork (text is added in CapCut).

## 2. Colour system: the channel's signature
| Palette | When | Colours |
|---|---|---|
| **RISE** | Growth, wealth, history, "before" | Warm golden daylight: sand, ochre, sky blue, turquoise |
| **RUIN** | Crisis, debt, collapse, "after" | Dusk or storm: deep navy `#0E1B24`, slate grey, muted teal, **one danger red** `#E10600` |

Every video moves **from RISE to RUIN** (or back, for recovery stories). Viewers feel the story turn through colour.
Overlay text always uses the brand style: white on black boxes with one yellow `#FFE600` word, set in Anton or Inter Bold.

## 3. Scene grammar (from the frame study)
| Shot | What it does | Example from the study → our version |
|---|---|---|
| ESTABLISHING | The "where" | A desert skyline → Lagos towers beside Makoko's stilt houses |
| SYMBOL | One object carries the idea | Dry tap and empty glass in front of oil pumps → a fuel pump nozzle dripping nothing in front of Nigerian oil rigs |
| CONTRAST | Foreground vs background paradox | A thirsty man while another celebrates in a money rain → a pensioner counting coins while a minister's motorcade passes |
| CHARACTER | Emotion and reaction | A man with binoculars scanning the desert → an IMF lender peering at a country through binoculars |
| CROSS-SECTION | Explains a hidden mechanism | Cracked earth and an underground cave → layers of debt stacked like rock strata under a city |
| SCALE | A big number made physical | — → a mountain of cash dwarfing a capital city |
| MOOD PLATE | Transitions and pauses | Storm clouds → the same, in the RUIN palette |
| MAP or CHART | Credibility | — (they lack it) → real maps and charts in the brand palette |

## 4. Rules
1. Never draw a realistic likeness of a real person. All leaders use the **[LEADER]** design with the **red eye bar**.
2. Traditional clothing must be accurate and dignified. No mocking stereotypes or exaggerated ethnic features.
3. No gore, violence or injury. Unrest is shown as crowds with blank placards.
4. Stay clearly cartoon, so no AI disclosure is needed and there's no misleading realism.
5. Don't recreate another channel's specific frames or characters. Use the grammar, not their pictures.

## 6. Animation in CapCut (low cost, looks premium)
- Every still gets slow motion: a 105–115% push-in or a slow pan over 4–8 seconds.
- **Parallax:** separate the foreground character from the background and move them at different speeds.
- Add particles or overlays (dust, rain, falling cash, smoke) and a subtle camera shake on impact lines.
- Use 6–8 Veo clips per video for the hero moments only.
- Cut on the narration beat, about one new shot every 4–7 seconds.
