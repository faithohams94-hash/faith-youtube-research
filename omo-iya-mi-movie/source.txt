#H1 Overview

#H3 Logline
A jealous elder sister engineers the destruction of her younger sister's marriage, and then marries the husband herself. She does not know that the younger sister has been hiding a fortune, a medical secret and a grandmother's last condition. Every lie she tells is recorded somewhere, and every person she silences leaves a witness behind.

#H3 Synopsis
Adunni Ogundele-Adebisi has a loving marriage to Tunde Adebisi, owner of the largest auto company in Abuja. After five childless years, she has told the world and her husband that the fault is hers. The truth, which only she and a doctor know, is that Tunde has a low sperm count. She took the blame to protect his pride.

Her older sister, Morenike, unmarried and consumed by envy, learns the real result through a bribed nurse. She decides to take the marriage apart and replace Adunni at Tunde's side. She plants a handsome stranger, Bayo, as Tunde's personal assistant. She hides a camera in the house. The plan backfires when Tunde watches the raw footage and sees that his wife is innocent. Bayo is jailed, and when he threatens to expose Morenike, she pays corrupt policemen to silence him. He dies in custody.

Morenike then uses the one weapon that cannot be disproved. In front of Tunde and his mother, she says that Adunni cannot give him a child. Tunde throws Adunni out into the rain. Adunni, who could save herself with one sentence of truth, chooses silence. She drives to a mansion, a workshop and a house that her late grandmother secretly left her. Within three years she builds a luxury aso-oke empire. Morenike marries Tunde.

The marriage poisons itself. A blackmailing policeman drains Morenike. A lover-contractor, Michael, swindles Tunde and then swindles Morenike. A hospital reveals to Tunde that he was the one who could not father a child. A prisoner whom Bayo befriended spends his freedom hunting the truth. When Tunde's collapsing company begs a rescue loan from Adunni's company, he does not know whose signature he is asking for.

#H3 Format and Tone
Feature-length drama written for YouTube release. Target runtime 95 to 105 minutes. Dialogue is in English with short Yoruba phrases (translated in the script). The tone is Nollywood melodrama with a thriller engine: big emotions, heavy silences, cliffhangers before every ad break, and reversals that arrive just after the audience feels certain.

#H3 Setting
Abuja, Nigeria, present day, covering roughly five years. Principal locations: the Adebisi mansion in Maitama; Adebisi Motors headquarters; the Ogundele family mansion and Agba Aso-Oke Industries; a divisional police station in Gwagwalada; the FCT Police Command; a private hospital; a car-parts shop in Utako.

#H3 Directing Rule for Shocks
Each time the audience believes it knows what happens next, give it something else. The script does this by building a clear expectation, letting it settle, and then breaking it with a fact that has been quietly planted earlier. No twist arrives without a plant. The Shock Map in the next section lists them.

#H1 The Shock Map

#P Each entry lists what the audience expects, what actually happens, and where the reveal was planted. Use it as a checklist when directing and editing.

#B SHOCK 1 (Cold Open). Expect: the boardroom flash-forward gives away the ending, so the story is a simple revenge tale. Get: the flash-forward shows only the middle of the story. The real ending involves a murder trial that nobody sees coming. Plant: the open does not show Morenike's fate.
#B SHOCK 2 (Scene 15). Expect: Tunde believes the doctored clip and doubts Adunni. Get: Tunde demands the raw footage, sees the truth, and destroys Bayo instead. The plan backfires on the schemer.
#B SHOCK 3 (Scenes 17 and 18). Expect: Morenike's accomplice will blackmail her later. Get: she has him killed within days, and the order is a single quiet sentence.
#B SHOCK 4 (Scene 22). Expect: Adunni, thrown out of her home, is poor and alone. Get: she owns a mansion with staff, a workshop and a house. Plant: the phone call to Baba Sule in Scene 5.
#B SHOCK 5 (Scene 23). Expect: Adunni cannot have children. Get: Adunni is fertile. Tunde is the one who cannot. She took the blame. Plant: the envelope in her jewellery box, Scene 4.
#B SHOCK 6 (Scene 21). Expect: Adunni will tell the truth to save herself. Get: she stays silent to protect Tunde's dignity.
#B SHOCK 7 (Scenes 25, 26 and 32). Expect: Morenike has won. Get: the policeman who killed Bayo now owns her, and then her lover robs her too.
#B SHOCK 8 (Scene 28). Expect: Morenike will be exposed by the murder inquiry. Get: she is nearly exposed first by a hospital, because the nurse she bribed years ago was fired and the doctor mentions it aloud.
#B SHOCK 9 (Scene 31). Expect: Adunni will forgive Tunde and rescue him for free. Get: she grants the loan on a sixty-day trap clause.
#B SHOCK 10 (Scene 36). Expect: Tunde catches Morenike stealing and the story ends in a fraud arrest. Get: the police arrive for murder, and Tunde learns for the first time that Bayo is dead.
#B SHOCK 11 (Scene 40). Expect: Adunni lives alone with her grief. Get: she has a husband, Dr. Dawson, and triplets.
#B SHOCK 12 (Scene 43). Expect: Morenike's lawyer will find a loophole. Get: Michael turns state witness, and Bayo's own phone speaks from the grave.
#B SHOCK 13 (Scene 46, post-credit). Expect: the story is closed. Get: the grandmother left a second sealed letter, addressed to Morenike.

#H1 Screenplay

#H2 Cold Open

#SC 1 | EXT. ADEBISI MANSION, MAITAMA, ABUJA | NIGHT, HEAVY RAIN
Black screen. Thunder. A heavy iron gate slams.
ADUNNI stands outside in a soaked lace wrapper, one cloth bag in her hand. Behind the bars, the mansion glows warm and gold.
TUNDE stands on the porch steps, his face wet with rain he is refusing to wipe.

@TUNDE (voice cracking)
Take your things and go.

@ADUNNI
Five years, Tunde. Five years, and you listen to one night?

@TUNDE
Do not say another word.

Adunni grips the gate bars. Behind Tunde, CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI stands in the doorway with her arms folded, unmoving.

@ADUNNI (quietly)
You will find out one day who did this. And when you do, I want you to remember I never begged.

A pair of headlights cut through the rain. A black SUV stops at the curb.

#TR SMASH CUT TO:

#SC 2 | INT. AGBA ASO-OKE INDUSTRIES, BOARDROOM | DAY (THREE YEARS LATER)
A glass boardroom on the top floor. Indigo silk samples line the walls. A framed portrait of an old woman hangs at the head of the table.
TUNDE sits at the long table in a worn suit, hands shaking. Beside him, two chiefs in expensive agbada sweat in the air-conditioning.
Heels on marble, approaching. The door handle turns.
Tunde looks up. We do not see who he sees. We see only his face go empty.

@TUNDE (whispering)
Adunni.

A document slides into frame across the table. We read one line: ASSET FORECLOSURE NOTICE. The Adebisi mansion.

@ADUNNI (O.S., calm)
Good morning, Mr. Adebisi. This house is mine now.

#TR CUT TO BLACK.

#TITLE ỌMỌ ÌYÁ MI. My Mother's Child.

#H2 Act One: The Perfect Marriage

#SC 3 | INT. ADEBISI MANSION, KITCHEN | MORNING (THREE YEARS EARLIER)
Bright, spacious, warm. ADUNNI stirs a pot while the cook watches helplessly. TUNDE comes in adjusting his cufflinks and kisses the top of her head.

@TUNDE
You are cooking again. We have staff for this.

@ADUNNI
A wife should know her husband's food.

@TUNDE
Aya mi. My wife. You are the queen of this house.

She laughs and flicks soup at his sleeve. He pretends outrage. They are happy, and the camera lets us enjoy it.
CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI appears at the door with her handbag over her arm. The laughter thins.

@CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI
Five years. My friends carry grandchildren to church. I carry only my prayers.

@TUNDE
Mama.

@CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI
I say nothing against your wife. I only say the clock does not stop.

Adunni keeps stirring. Her hand is steady. Her eyes are not.

#SC 4 | INT. ADEBISI MANSION, MASTER BEDROOM | NIGHT
Tunde sleeps. ADUNNI sits at the dressing table. From the bottom of her jewellery box she lifts a sealed hospital envelope, soft with age. She does not open it. She holds it to her chest, then puts it back and covers it with gold.
Across the corridor, a door with an Ankara cloth hung over the handle. A nursery that has never been used. She touches the cloth and walks past.

#TW PLANT: the envelope (Shock 5). Do not show its contents.

#SC 5 | EXT. ADEBISI MANSION, GARDEN | DAY
ADUNNI walks among the hibiscus with a phone to her ear.

@ADUNNI (into phone)
Baba Sule, pay the weavers first. Always the weavers first.

@BABA SULE (V.O.)
Ma, the bank is asking whose name is on the deed.

@ADUNNI
Nobody's name is to be spoken. Mama said so. Not until the day comes.

A shadow in the hedge. MORENIKE has arrived early and has heard the last words. She steps out smiling.

@MORENIKE
Which deed?

@ADUNNI
My tailor. He is behind on rent.

@MORENIKE
You and your charity cases.

She kisses Adunni's cheek. Adunni does not see her sister's smile freeze and then recover.

#TW PLANT: the deed, the weavers, the name nobody speaks (Shock 4).

#SC 6 | INT. MORENIKE'S FLAT | NIGHT
A cramped one-bedroom flat, a ceiling fan that wobbles. MORENIKE in a faded work blouse eats cold rice alone. A knock. She opens the door to MICHAEL, who leans in the frame with a bag of suya and a smile that is too smooth.

@MICHAEL
Why are you eating cold rice at midnight?

@MORENIKE
Because the world has plenty to give and none of it is for me.

@MICHAEL
Then take it, baby. Take it. Who is stopping you?

She looks at him for a long moment. When he goes to the bathroom, she takes a folded photocopy from inside a Bible and holds it to the lamp. We see only a hospital logo and a name: T. ADEBISI. A single line is highlighted yellow. We do not read it.

#TW PLANT: the photocopy (Shocks 5 and 8).

#SC 7 | EXT. ADEBISI MANSION, DRIVEWAY | DAY
Balloons. Staff in matching uniforms. ADUNNI is led out blindfolded by TUNDE. A white SUV sits under a red ribbon. The number plate reads ADUNNI 1.

@TUNDE
Open your eyes.

She gasps. Both hands over her mouth. She hugs him and spins him around while the staff cheer.
MORENIKE stands by the gate and applauds one beat too late. In her handbag, her phone buzzes. The screen reads LANDLORD. She declines the call and keeps clapping.

#SC 8 | INT. MORENIKE'S FLAT | NIGHT
Dark. MORENIKE sits on the floor with the photocopy in her lap and a candle between her knees.

@MORENIKE (to herself)
A man with this cannot give his mother a grandchild. And a woman who cannot give a child can be sent away.

Her thumb finds a number. She hesitates once, then presses call.

@MORENIKE (into phone)
Bayo. Do you still want that job?

#SC 9 | INT. BUKA CANTEEN | NIGHT
A cheap canteen, plastic chairs, the sound of a TV. BAYO eats beans with a spoon bent from use. MORENIKE slides a CV and a paper bag of folded clothes across the table. Inside the bag is a navy suit.

@BAYO
Madam, why are you doing this for me?

@MORENIKE
Tunde Adebisi needs a personal assistant. You will be very good at it.

@BAYO
And what do I do for you?

@MORENIKE (smiling)
Be kind to my sister. Very kind.

Bayo laughs, thinking he understands. He does not.

#SC 10 | INT. ADEBISI MANSION, KITCHEN | DAY
MORENIKE sits at the kitchen island. ADUNNI chops vegetables.

@MORENIKE
There is a boy. Bayo. His father is dead. He has five siblings and a mother who cannot walk.

@ADUNNI
Ah. God.

@MORENIKE
Tunde needs an assistant. Just a word from you.

@ADUNNI (soft)
I will speak to him tonight.

Cut to the bedroom. Tunde, in bed, yields in two sentences to his wife's pleading voice.

@TUNDE
For you, Adunni. Anything for you.

#SC 11 | INT. ADEBISI MOTORS, HEADQUARTERS | DAY (MONTAGE)
BAYO in his navy suit: fast, polite, always smiling. He stands when TUNDE enters. He knows every client's name. He brings coffee. He brings good news.
Whenever ADUNNI visits her husband's office, BAYO is there. A door held. A joke told. A sweet bought from the canteen "just because."
TUNDE watches, grateful. ADUNNI laughs. Neither notices that Bayo's eyes never leave her.

#SC 12 | INT. ADEBISI MANSION, LIVING ROOM | DAY
MORENIKE visits with a gift of kola nuts.

@MORENIKE
Adunni, that fabric I asked you about, the green one with the gold thread. Is it upstairs?

@ADUNNI
In the wardrobe. Wait here.

Adunni goes up. The moment her footsteps fade, Morenike pulls a small camera from her bag. She sets it into a crystal vase on the console, angled at the doorway. A red light blinks once and goes still. She is sitting calmly when Adunni returns.

#TW PLANT: the camera.

#SC 13 | INT. ADEBISI MANSION, FOYER AND KITCHEN | DAY
A knock. ADUNNI opens the door. BAYO stands holding a folder.

@BAYO
Madam, Oga asked me to drop these documents.

@ADUNNI
Come in. Wait, let me give you something.

She hands him a glass of water. He drinks too slowly.

@BAYO
Madam, you are too kind. Many people would not do this.

@ADUNNI
Are you hungry? I have food.

She serves a plate. As she sets it down, he catches her wrist.

@BAYO (soft)
Do you know you are very beautiful?

She freezes.

@ADUNNI
What are you doing? Let go of my hand.

@BAYO
Madam, you are lonely in this house. I can see it.

@ADUNNI
What are you doing? What do you think you are doing?

She pulls free and throws the plate at his feet. It shatters.

@ADUNNI (shaking)
Get out of my house.

He backs off, smiling, as though he has lost nothing. The vase on the console shows a tiny red light.

#SC 14 | INT. MORENIKE'S FLAT | NIGHT
MORENIKE watches the footage on a laptop, headphones on. She pauses on the frame where Adunni stands frozen, her wrist in Bayo's hand. She trims the clip: twelve seconds, no sound.
On screen it looks like consent. Morenike exports the file with a small satisfied click.

#SC 15 | INT. ADEBISI MOTORS, TUNDE'S OFFICE | DAY
TUNDE stands at his desk. MORENIKE sits across from him, hands folded, eyes wet.

@MORENIKE
I did not want to be the one to show you this.

She plays the clip on her phone. TUNDE watches. His jaw sets. He watches it again. He sets the phone down very gently.

@TUNDE
Play it again.

@MORENIKE
Tunde, why torture yourself?

@TUNDE
Where is the full video?

@MORENIKE
It is... this is all I could...

@TUNDE
Our foyer has its own CCTV. It records sound. I installed that system myself.

His phone is already at his ear.

@TUNDE (into phone)
Send me yesterday's footage from the foyer. All of it. Now.

The file arrives. He plays it with the sound up. We hear Adunni's voice: "Let go of my hand." We hear the plate shatter. We hear Bayo laugh.
Morenike stares at the screen. She had trusted the silent clip. She never thought about the dome in the ceiling.
Tunde rises. His chair falls behind him.

@TUNDE
That dog.

@MORENIKE (too fast)
Tunde, maybe we should think before...

@TUNDE
Think? He put his hands on my wife!

He storms out. Morenike remains in the chair. For the first time, her face drops. Plan broken.
