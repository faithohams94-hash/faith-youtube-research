#H1 Overview

#H3 Logline
On the night of Adunni's birthday, a jealous elder sister watches her younger sister receive the biggest car in Abuja and decides to destroy her marriage. She succeeds, and then marries the husband herself. But every lie she tells is recorded somewhere, and years later the sister she threw into the rain owns his mansions.

#H3 Synopsis
Adunni Ogundele-Adebisi is married to Tunde Adebisi, the owner of the biggest auto company in Abuja. Their marriage is loving, but after five years there is still no child. Years ago Adunni quietly told the doctor, and then the whole family, that the fault was hers. The truth is that Tunde has a low sperm count. She took the blame to protect his pride, and she never told him otherwise.

At Adunni's birthday party, Tunde surprises her with the newest car in Abuja. Her older sister, Morenike, unmarried and consumed by envy, watches from the lawn and decides that night to take the marriage apart and take Tunde for herself. She plants a charming stranger, Bayo, as Tunde's personal assistant, and hides a camera in the house. Bayo makes a move on Adunni. Morenike edits the footage so it looks like Adunni did not resist, and shows it to Tunde. Tunde jails Bayo. When Bayo threatens to expose Morenike, she pays corrupt policemen to silence him, and he dies in custody.

Morenike then uses the weapon Adunni handed her. In front of Tunde and his mother, she says that Adunni cannot give a child. Tunde throws Adunni out into the rain. Adunni drives to a mansion, a workshop and a house her late grandmother secretly left her. Her grandmother raised both sisters, and she left everything to Adunni because she had seen Morenike's nature when Morenike was a child. Within a few years Adunni builds a luxury aso-oke empire. Morenike marries Tunde.

The marriage poisons itself. A blackmailing policeman drains Morenike. Her lover, Michael, swindles Tunde and then swindles Morenike. A prisoner Bayo befriended spends his freedom hunting the truth, and an investigator finds Bayo's secret phone. Tunde's collapsing company takes a rescue loan from Adunni's company, not knowing whose it is. When he cannot repay, Adunni buys out his creditors and becomes the owner of his mansions. While packing, Tunde finds a cassette in Morenike's suitcase. It shows him everything he got wrong.

#H3 Format and Tone
Feature-length drama written for YouTube release. Target runtime 95 to 105 minutes. Dialogue is in English with short Yoruba phrases (translated in the script). The tone is Nollywood melodrama with a thriller engine: big emotions, heavy silences, cliffhangers before every ad break, and reversals that arrive just after the audience feels certain.

#H3 Setting
Abuja, Nigeria, present day, covering roughly five years. The Location Bible in this document describes every principal location in detail.

#H3 Directing Rule for Shocks
Each time the audience believes it knows what happens next, give it something else. The script does this by building a clear expectation, letting it settle, and then breaking it with a fact that has been quietly planted earlier. No twist arrives without a plant. The Shock Map in the next section lists them.

#H1 The Shock Map

#P Each entry lists what the audience expects, what actually happens, and where the reveal was planted. Scenes are named, not numbered, so the list stays correct if scenes move.

#B SHOCK 1 (the birthday party). Expect: the biggest night of Adunni's life is a celebration. Get: it is the night her sister decides to destroy her. The camera finds Morenike's reflection in the car door, smiling and then not smiling.
#B SHOCK 2 (the edited clip). Expect: Tunde sees the whole truth. Get: he sees twelve silent seconds in which Adunni appears to freeze, and the lie takes hold. Plant: Morenike tucks the original cassette into her suitcase lining.
#B SHOCK 3 (the toilet block). Expect: Morenike's accomplice will blackmail her later. Get: she has him killed within days, and the order is a single quiet sentence.
#B SHOCK 4 (the hidden mansion). Expect: Adunni, thrown out of her home, is poor and alone. Get: she owns a mansion with staff, a workshop and a house. Plant: the phone call to Baba Sule in the garden.
#B SHOCK 5 (the flashback in the hall). Expect: Adunni cannot have children. Get: Adunni is fertile. Tunde is the one who cannot. She took the blame. Plant: the sealed envelope in her jewellery box.
#B SHOCK 6 (the blackmail and the swindle). Expect: Morenike has won. Get: the policeman who killed Bayo now owns her, and then her lover robs her too.
#B SHOCK 7 (the loan). Expect: Adunni will forgive Tunde and rescue him for free. Get: she grants the loan on a sixty-day clause.
#B SHOCK 8 (Bayo's old room). Expect: the evidence is a recording Morenike can destroy. Get: Bayo hid a phone in a wall of his room before he died, and it recorded her.
#B SHOCK 9 (the arrest). Expect: Tunde catches Morenike stealing and the story ends in a fraud arrest. Get: the police arrive for murder, and Tunde learns for the first time that Bayo is dead.
#B SHOCK 10 (the boardroom). Expect: Tunde knows who is lending him money. Get: he has been begging his own ex-wife, and she has bought out every creditor he has.
#B SHOCK 11 (the suitcase). Expect: the story is over once Morenike is jailed. Get: Tunde finds the original cassette and learns he threw out an innocent wife. Payoff: the tucked-away cassette from the edit scene.
#B SHOCK 12 (the TV in the shop). Expect: Adunni lives alone with her grief. Get: she has a husband, Dr. Dawson, and triplets, and Tunde realises in one second that it was never her.
#B SHOCK 13 (the courtroom). Expect: Morenike's lawyer will find a loophole. Get: Michael turns state witness, and Bayo's own phone speaks from the grave.
#B SHOCK 14 (the post-credit scene). Expect: the story is closed. Get: the grandmother left a second sealed letter, addressed to Morenike.

#H1 How to Use This Script for Visuals

#P This edition is written for people and tools that will turn each scene into video or still images. Every scene is built from the same layers, so nothing has to be guessed.

#B SETTING blocks (grey panel): the physical world of the scene. Where it is, the time of day, the light, the colors, the textures, the weather, the sounds in the background and the mood.
#B ACTION lines: what the camera sees, in order. Each line describes movement, props, facial expression and body language, not just plot.
#B DIALOGUE with delivery notes: who speaks, and in the parenthesis, how. Delivery covers pace, volume, pitch, breath and the expression on the speaker's face.
#B REACTION blocks (indented, italic): what happens in the seconds after a line or an action. Faces falling, hands stopping, the room changing. These are the beats most tools skip, so they are written out.
#B DIRECTOR'S NOTES: plants and payoffs for the twists. These are for the editor and director, not the camera.

#H3 Visual Continuity Rules
#B Light follows the story. The Adebisi mansion is warm gold while the marriage is happy, then turns cool blue and grey after the eviction. Adunni's new world is deep indigo with warm lantern light. Morenike's world is sodium orange and harsh fluorescent.
#B Each main character keeps one signature color. Adunni: champagne and indigo. Morenike: mustard and gold. Tunde: charcoal and white, then oil-stained grey. Chief Mrs. Adebisi: deep red and coral.
#B Rain only appears twice: the eviction and the last scene. It is the film's rhyme.
#B Keep the same face for each actor and the same wardrobe for each timeline stage. The Character Bible lists the stages.

#H2 Principal Characters

#CHAR ADUNNI OGUNDELE-ADEBISI
role: Protagonist. Wife of Tunde, younger sister of Morenike, secret heiress, later founder of Agba Aso-Oke Industries.
age: 30 at the start. 35 at the end.
look: Slim, graceful, dark-brown skin, soft round face with a gap-toothed smile, long neck, hair in neat braids or under a gele. Wardrobe stages: (1) the marriage, pastel lace, champagne gowns, simple gold studs; (2) the eviction, a soaked lace wrapper and bare feet; (3) the empire, sharp deep-indigo aso-oke suits, a wine gele, a single heavy ring; (4) the new family, soft cream knitwear and a simple headscarf.
body: She walks with a straight spine and small, quick steps when happy, and slows to a glide when hurt. She touches people when she speaks: a hand on an arm, a squeeze of a wrist. She laughs with her whole shoulders and covers her mouth with both hands when moved. When she is wounded she goes very still, her lower lip trembles once, and she swallows before speaking. Her eyes fill without a sound. In the empire years her stillness becomes a weapon: a level gaze, hands folded, a very slight lift of the chin.
voice: Soft mezzo, warm and unhurried, with a gentle Yoruba lilt. She rises at the end of a sentence when teasing, and drops lower and slower when she is hurt. She never shouts. She says "Ah, Tunde" and "Mama" with affection. Her laughter is breathy and short. In the boardroom her voice becomes flat and calm, each word placed like a coin.
nature: Warm, humble, proud in a quiet way. She will give up her reputation for love, and then be unmovable once trust is gone. She never uses the word revenge.
prompt: Portrait of a 30-year-old Nigerian woman, dark brown skin, soft oval face, small gap between her front teeth, slender neck, hair in neat box braids, wearing a champagne lace gown and simple gold stud earrings, gentle warm smile with glistening eyes, fairy lights glowing behind her at a garden party, hands lightly clasped at her chest.
vprompt: Warm soft Nigerian female voice, early thirties, gentle Yoruba-accented English, unhurried pace, slight musical lilt, breathy laugh, drops lower and slower when hurt, never shouts, calm and flat when commanding.

#CHAR MORENIKE OGUNDELE
role: Antagonist. Elder sister of Adunni. Unmarried. Later Tunde's second wife and then a convicted murderer.
age: 37 at the start. 42 at the end.

body: She stands very upright with her shoulders back and her handbag held against her stomach like a shield. Her smile appears fast and disappears fast, like a light switched on and off. Her eyes stay still when her mouth smiles. She claps late. She tilts her head to listen and never interrupts. When she lies, she blinks slowly and touches her collarbone. When she is angry, she goes quieter, not louder. In prison she becomes stone: hands folded, spine straight, no blink.
voice: Crisp alto, controlled and polite, with careful diction. In public she is sweet: "my dear", "Sister", "Mama", with a bright rising tone. In private she is flat and dry, almost bored. She uses long pauses to make others fill the silence. She never raises her voice, even when ordering a killing. Her laugh is a single dry breath.
nature: Intelligent, patient, charming in public. She believes she is owed a life.
