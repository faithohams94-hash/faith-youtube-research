01 · Script · retire these lines

If every channel says it, we don't.

"Here's what nobody tells you about…"In our brother script at 1:43. In our divorce script at 1:27 and 16:16. Even our banner says it.
"But here's the part that changes you."Generic. The viewer has heard it a thousand times.
"You realize something…"Announces a feeling instead of showing it.

Instead: let an object carry the turn

"In your wallet, behind your license, there's a check stub folded so small it's gone soft at the edges. Thirty-eight thousand dollars."

That line is already in our brother script. It's the hook. It just needs to come first.

03 · Thumbnail

Big face. Proof of money. Daylight.

Their winner: the boss's angry face fills a third of the frame, the hero is calm, it's bright daylight, and the property deed is the proof. Ours: eight small faces at night.

1

Faces big and clear

1 to 3 faces max. The antagonist's reaction is the hook.

2

Show proof of money

A deed, a "$0 mortgage" letter, a bank app, a car key.

3

3 versions, A/B tested

The part most channels miss

Six moves that monetize.

01

Repeat a winner within 14 days

When a video beats our average 10×, make two more on the same angle. Every case study did this.

02

Volume wins

They have 82, 102 and 186 videos. We have 5. 3 a week gets us to ~50 by month 4.

03

A series, not one-offs

Beanie Survives: 110K subscribers from 26 videos, built as episodes. "Ep. 13" got 80K.

04

Read the numbers at 48 hours

Check click-through rate and first-minute retention in YouTube Studio. Weak click rate → swap the thumbnail.

05

Shorts as trailers

3 vertical cuts per video to pull new viewers in. Shorts views don't count toward the 4,000 hours, so the long video stays the core.

06

Stay "authentic"

Since July 2025 YouTube won't monetize mass-produced, repetitive AI content. Original scripts, one character and real editing keep us safe.
