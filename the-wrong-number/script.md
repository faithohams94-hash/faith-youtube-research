# THE WRONG NUMBER
## "She Texted a Dead Number… and a Stranger Answered"

*A Nigerian drama in 52 scenes. About 50 minutes. All dialogue, no narrator. Text messages are spoken as voice-over and shown on screen. The only text that isn't spoken is short visual notes for the AI scenes.*

---

## CAST

- **CHLOE ADEYEMI** (33 → 43). Beautiful and gifted, a trained interior designer. Married to Kunle with two children, living in Abuja. She has learned to say "sorry" before she even knows what she did.
- **DR. KUNLE ADEYEMI** (40). Medical Director of Adeyemi Specialist Hospital, Maitama. Charming in public and controlling at home. He believes a husband owns his house, his car and his wife.
- **EMMA WILLIAMS** (35 → 45). Department manager at Silverline, an AI film studio in Lagos. Single, witty and blunt. She has just bought a new SIM card, and nobody told her the number used to belong to someone else.
- **ADA** (7). Chloe's daughter, who notices everything. **TOBI** (4), her son.
- **MUSA**. The family driver.
- **RITA**. Kunle's secretary at the hospital.
- **NKEM**. Kunle's girlfriend.
- **MAMA KUNLE**. Kunle's mother.
- **HALIMA**. Chloe's old university friend.
- **BISI**. Emma's colleague and best friend.
- **BARRISTER ZAINAB BELLO**. Chloe's lawyer.
- **TUNDE BALOGUN**. An architect, gentle and patient.
- **MR. OKORO**. Kunle's lawyer.
- **JUDGE**, **GUESTS**, **CLIENT**, **SECURITY GUARD**, **MC**.

## RECURRING DETAILS TO KEEP CONSISTENT

- **2:19 PM on a Friday.** The minute the first wrong text was answered, and every Friday after it.
- **"Jollof or fried rice?"** A question that once terrified her and finally sets her free.
- **The dead number.** Kunle's temporary SIM, which he threw away. It was recycled and sold to Emma in Lagos.
- **"Friday Stranger".** The name Chloe saves Emma under.
- **Rice.** He pours it on her in Scene 4. At the end, she cooks whatever she wants.
- **"Still here."** The two words that keep the friendship alive.

## ACT ONE — THE RICE

## SCENE 1 — THE FOOD MUST NOT BE LATE
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION, ASOKORO, ABUJA — KITCHEN — FRIDAY, 12:41 PM**
*(~0:00–0:58)*

*A huge marble kitchen. Three pots are on the fire. CHLOE ADEYEMI (33), beautiful and sweating, stirs one pot while holding her phone to her ear with her shoulder. TOBI (4) cries from the sitting room. ADA (7), in her school uniform, pulls her wrapper.*

**KUNLE (ON PHONE):** Chloe. It's 12:41.

**CHLOE:** I know, darling. The stew is almost—

**KUNLE (ON PHONE):** I didn't ask about the stew. I said my food should be on my desk by one o'clock. I have the board meeting at half past one. Do you want me to collapse in front of my board?

**CHLOE:** No, darling. It will be there. I promise.

**KUNLE (ON PHONE):** Your promises are like NEPA light, Chloe. They come and go.

*He hangs up. Chloe stares at the phone.*

**ADA:** Mummy, Tobi poured Milo on the rug.

**CHLOE:** *(closing her eyes)* Okay. Okay. Mummy is coming.

**ADA:** Why can't Aunty Blessing help us? She used to help us.

**CHLOE:** Because Daddy said nobody touches his children except their mother. *(beat)* Go and wash Tobi's hands, my love.

*She grabs a food flask, the cooler and her phone, and dials.*

## SCENE 2 — "PLEASE MADAM, DON'T TOUCH THE CAR"
**INT. KITCHEN / EXT. FUEL STATION, WUSE — INTERCUT — 12:48 PM**
*(~0:58–2:01)*

*MUSA, the family driver, stands beside the black Lexus at a fuel queue that stretches out of sight.*

**CHLOE:** Musa, where are you? I need you to take me to Oga's office now now. His food must be there by one.

**MUSA (ON PHONE):** Ah, madam! I'm at Wuse. Oga sent me money this morning to buy fuel for the week. The queue is long like Third Mainland Bridge. I'll be there in thirty minutes, madam.

**CHLOE:** Thirty minutes? Musa, I don't have thirty minutes. He'll kill me.

**MUSA (ON PHONE):** Madam, please wait for me. Please.

*Chloe looks at the wall, where the spare key to Kunle's other car, the white Range Rover, hangs on a hook.*

**CHLOE:** I'll drive myself.

**MUSA (ON PHONE):** *(panicking)* Madam, NO! Please, madam, don't touch Oga's car! You know how Oga is about that car! Madam, I'm begging you, wait for me—

*Chloe hangs up. Her hand shakes as she takes the key off the hook.*

**ADA:** *(at the door)* Mummy, are you allowed to drive Daddy's car?

**CHLOE:** *(forcing a smile)* Today, Mummy is allowed. Look after your brother. Mummy will be back in one hour.

## SCENE 3 — THE SPEECH
**INT. ADEYEMI SPECIALIST HOSPITAL, MAITAMA — BOARDROOM / RECEPTION — 1:06 PM**
*(~2:01–2:45)*

*BOARDROOM. Twelve doctors and board members listen while DR. KUNLE ADEYEMI (40), handsome and in a perfect suit, gives a speech. Applause is building.*

**KUNLE:** …and this is why our hospital is different. We don't only treat bodies. We treat families. Compassion. Patience. Respect. That is the Adeyemi standard. *(beat)* A man who cannot be gentle with the weak has no business wearing a white coat.

*Applause.*

*RECEPTION. Chloe hurries in with the cooler, out of breath. RITA, the secretary, looks up.*

**RITA:** Good afternoon, ma. Mrs. Adeyemi?

**CHLOE:** Good afternoon. Please, I've brought my husband's lunch. Is he—

**RITA:** He's in the board meeting, ma. Please sit down. I'll tell Dr. Kunle the moment he comes out.

*Chloe sits on a leather sofa and holds the cooler on her lap like a baby. A TV on the wall plays a cooking show. The host laughs: "And remember, ladies, a happy kitchen is a happy home!" Chloe watches it without blinking.*

## SCENE 4 — "WHO BROUGHT YOU HERE?"
**INT. KUNLE'S OFFICE — 1:31 PM**
*(~2:45–3:47)*

*A big office with diplomas on the wall. Kunle sits down, loosening his tie. Rita knocks and enters.*

**RITA:** Good afternoon, sir. Your wife is in reception.

**KUNLE:** *(surprised, then smiling)* My wife? Call her inside.

*Chloe enters with the cooler and a nervous smile.*

**CHLOE:** I brought your food, darling. It's still hot.

**KUNLE:** *(not touching it)* What are you doing here?

**CHLOE:** You said your food should be on your desk by one—

**KUNLE:** Who brought you here?

**CHLOE:** …I came.

**KUNLE:** Where is Musa?

**CHLOE:** He went to buy fuel. You sent him the money this morning. He said thirty minutes, and I knew you were hungry, so—

*Kunle stands slowly. He walks to the window and looks down at the car park. His white Range Rover is parked crookedly across two spaces.*

**KUNLE:** *(very quietly)* You touched my car.

**CHLOE:** Darling, I only—

**KUNLE:** What did I tell you about my car, Chloe? *(beat)* What did I TELL you?

**CHLOE:** *(whispering)* Not to drive it.

*Rita stands frozen by the door. Kunle opens the cooler and lifts out the steaming food flask. Then he pours the jollof rice over Chloe's head. It runs down her hair, her face and her dress. Rita gasps and covers her mouth.*

**KUNLE:** *(calmly)* Now you're hot too. *(beat)* Get out of my office.

*Chloe stands there, rice falling from her hair onto the carpet. She can't breathe.*

**KUNLE:** Rita, call the cleaners. *(to Chloe)* And give Rita my car key. Musa will bring it home.

*Chloe puts the key on the desk with a shaking hand and walks out past Rita, who can't look at her.*

## SCENE 5 — THE CAR PARK
**EXT. HOSPITAL CAR PARK — MOMENTS LATER**
*(~3:47–4:24)*

*Chloe stands beside the Range Rover with rice still in her hair. She can't open the door because she gave the key back. She just stands there. Footsteps run up behind her. It's RITA, holding a packet of tissues.*

**RITA:** *(breathless)* Madam. Madam, please, wait.

**CHLOE:** *(not turning around)* Please. Don't look at me.

**RITA:** *(handing her the tissues, her voice shaking)* I'm sorry, ma. I'm so sorry. I've worked for him for six years. I never knew—

**CHLOE:** *(wiping her face)* Nobody knows. That's how he likes it.

*Musa's old Corolla screeches into the car park. Musa jumps out, sweating, and sees her hair.*

**MUSA:** *(his hand on his head)* Ah! Madam! Ah, God! *(beat, quietly)* Madam, I begged you. I begged you not to touch that car.

**CHLOE:** *(a broken laugh)* It wasn't the car, Musa. *(beat)* It's never the car.

*She gets into Musa's car. Rita watches it drive away, then looks up at Kunle's office window. He is standing there, watching too.*

## SCENE 6 — "WHY IS THERE RICE IN YOUR HAIR?"
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — BATHROOM / SITTING ROOM — AFTERNOON → NIGHT**
*(~4:24–5:10)*

*Chloe kneels over the bathtub, washing rice out of her hair and sobbing without a sound. Ada stands at the door.*

**ADA:** Mummy, why is there rice in your hair?

**CHLOE:** *(quickly wiping her face)* Mummy fell in the kitchen, baby.

**ADA:** You didn't go to the kitchen. You went to Daddy's office.

*Chloe has no answer.*

*NIGHT. The front door opens. Kunle walks in, calm and smiling, and kisses Tobi's head. Chloe stands by the dining table with dinner served.*

**KUNLE:** *(sitting)* The food you poured on yourself today was a waste. Do you know how much rice costs now?

**CHLOE:** *(whispering)* I'm sorry.

**KUNLE:** Listen to me carefully. You will never, ever come to my office in my car again. Do you understand? My staff saw you drive in like a market woman.

**CHLOE:** Yes, darling.

**KUNLE:** Good. *(tasting the soup)* Too much salt.

## SCENE 7 — "I WILL TELL YOUR MOTHER"
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — MASTER BEDROOM — NIGHT — TWO WEEKS LATER**
*(~5:10–6:41)*

*Sounds through the door: a crash, a cry, then silence. Inside, Chloe is on the floor beside the bed holding her arm, with her lip bleeding. Kunle stands over her, breathing hard.*

**KUNLE:** I asked you a simple question. Where did the twenty thousand naira go?

**CHLOE:** *(crying)* Ada's school trip. I told you. I showed you the receipt.

**KUNLE:** You don't spend MY money without asking me!

*Chloe pulls herself up against the bed and grabs her phone.*

**CHLOE:** I'm calling your mother.

**KUNLE:** *(freezing)* What?

**CHLOE:** I'm calling Mama. I'll tell her everything you do to me. The rice. The slaps. Tonight. Everything.

*She dials. "MAMA KUNLE" appears on the screen. Kunle snatches the phone and ends the call.*

**KUNLE:** *(low, dangerous)* You call my mother, and I'll send you back to your father's house in Ibadan tonight. Without the children. You'll never see Ada and Tobi again.

*Silence. Chloe stares at him. Then something changes in his face. He sinks to his knees in front of her.*

**KUNLE:** *(suddenly soft, almost crying)* Chloe. Chloe, I'm sorry. It's the hospital. The board, the pressure. I don't know what came over me. *(He takes her hands.)* I'll never do it again. I swear on my father's grave. Please. Please, don't tell Mama.

**CHLOE:** *(barely audible)* You said that last time.

**KUNLE:** This time I mean it. *(He kisses her hands.)* I'm going to treat you like a queen. Watch me.

*Her phone, on the floor, starts ringing. "MAMA KUNLE." The call she cut has rung back. Kunle and Chloe both freeze. He picks it up, presses it into her hand and whispers.*

**KUNLE:** *(whispering)* Answer it. Smile.

**CHLOE:** *(answering, voice trembling)* Good evening, Mama.

**MAMA KUNLE (ON PHONE):** Chloe? You called me just now. Is everything alright, my daughter?

*Kunle kneels in front of her with his hands joined like a man praying, mouthing "please."*

**CHLOE:** *(after a long, long pause)* It was a pocket dial, Mama. Everything is fine.

**MAMA KUNLE (ON PHONE):** *(a strange pause)* …Are you sure, Chloe? You sound like you've been crying.

**CHLOE:** *(closing her eyes)* I'm sure, Mama. Goodnight.

*She hangs up. Kunle exhales and kisses her hands again and again.*

**KUNLE:** Thank you. Thank you, my queen. You'll never regret it.

*Chloe stares at the wall over his shoulder. She already does.*

## SCENE 8 — THE GOOD HUSBAND
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — DINING ROOM — NEXT MORNING**
*(~6:41–7:21)*

*The table is laid with fresh flowers, bread and egg. Kunle is pouring tea, which he has never done in nine years. Chloe enters, with her arm wrapped. The children stare.*

**KUNLE:** Good morning, my queen. Sit. I made breakfast.

**ADA:** *(whispering to Tobi)* Daddy can cook?

**KUNLE:** *(laughing)* Daddy can do many things. *(He slides a paper to Chloe.)* Before I forget. My line has had no network for one week. MTN people are useless. So I bought a new SIM. Here's the number. Text me there for now.

**CHLOE:** *(reading)* 0814 333 2197.

**KUNLE:** Save it as "Darling New."

**CHLOE:** *(quietly saving it)* Darling New.

*He kisses her forehead and leaves for work. Chloe looks at the flowers, the bread and her wrapped arm.*

**ADA:** Mummy, is Daddy sick?

**CHLOE:** Why, baby?

**ADA:** Because he's being nice.

## SCENE 9 — THE LINE THAT CAME BACK
**INT. KUNLE'S RANGE ROVER — NIGHT — PARKED OUTSIDE A LOUNGE IN WUSE 2**
*(~7:21–8:04)*

*Kunle sits in the driver's seat. NKEM (26), glamorous, is in the passenger seat, taking selfies. His phone buzzes with an SMS: "Dear customer, your line 0803 ••• ••12 has been restored. Thank you for choosing us."*

**KUNLE:** *(smiling)* My old line is back.

**NKEM:** So what happens to the new one? The one your wife texts every hour?

**KUNLE:** *(pulling the new SIM out of his second phone)* I'll tell her to stop using it. Or maybe I won't. *(He laughs.)* A dead number is a very useful thing, Nkem. When I'm with you, I'm "unreachable."

**NKEM:** You're wicked.

**KUNLE:** I'm organised. *(He lowers the window and flicks the tiny SIM into the gutter.)* There. Wherever that number goes now, it's not my problem.

**NKEM:** And if she texts it?

**KUNLE:** Then she's talking to nobody. *(beat)* She's used to that.

## SCENE 10 — NOBODY TOUCHES MY CHILDREN
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — BEDROOM / STAIRCASE — FRIDAY, 7:10 AM — THREE WEEKS LATER**
*(~8:04–9:08)*

*Chloe lies in bed shivering with a fever and a wet towel on her forehead. Kunle, dressed for work, stands at the door.*

**KUNLE:** Chloe. It's ten past seven. Where is my breakfast?

**CHLOE:** *(weak)* I'm sorry, darling. I have a fever. I couldn't stand up.

**KUNLE:** You couldn't stand up, but you could lie down. *(sighing loudly)* Every day there's something. Listen. I'll eat lunch at the hospital. Bring it before one. And this time Musa drives you. If I see you behind the wheel of my car, God help you.

**CHLOE:** Please, can I call Aunty Blessing to help with the children today? Just today?

**KUNLE:** No. Nobody raises my children except their mother. *(He picks up his bag.)* Oh, and that new number, the 0814 one. It's dead. Use my old line from now on.

**CHLOE:** *(coughing, eyes closed)* Okay.

**ADA (O.S.):** Mummy! Tobi is crying! He wants you!

**KUNLE:** You heard me? The old line.

**CHLOE:** *(already getting up for Tobi)* Yes, yes. I heard you.

*He leaves. Chloe, dizzy, stumbles toward her son's cries. The instruction is already gone from her head.*

## SCENE 11 — "JOLLOF OR FRIED RICE?"
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — KITCHEN — FRIDAY, 2:14 PM**
*(~9:08–9:56)*

*Chloe is still feverish and sweating, with Tobi on her hip and Ada doing homework at the counter. She opens the fridge. One bowl of jollof rice. One bowl of fried rice. She freezes.*

**CHLOE:** *(whispering)* Jollof or fried? Jollof or fried? What did he say this morning?

*She dials Kunle. It rings and rings. No answer.*

**CHLOE:** Please pick up. Please.

*She hangs up, opens her messages and taps "Darling New."*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Jollof rice or fried rice? Sorry, I forgot which one you wanted.

*Send. 2:17 PM. She puts Tobi down and holds the edge of the counter. 2:19 PM. A reply.*

**UNKNOWN (TEXT):** Wrong receiver, but jollof rice with turkey sounds better 😂

*Chloe stares at the screen.*

**CHLOE:** *(confused)* Wrong receiver?

**ADA:** What is it, Mummy?

**CHLOE:** I don't know. Maybe it's a fake number. *(beat)* Maybe Daddy's line has a scammer on it.

*She switches to Kunle's old contact and types fast.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Jollof or fried rice?

*A reply comes in seconds.*

**KUNLE (TEXT):** Fried. Musa is waiting outside. Don't be late.

*Chloe exhales. She looks back at the stranger's message, "jollof rice with turkey sounds better 😂", and for one second, very small, she almost smiles.*

**ADA:** Mummy, you're smiling.

**CHLOE:** *(the smile vanishing)* No, baby. Mummy has a fever. Pack your books.

## SCENE 12 — THE DRUG REP
**INT. HOSPITAL — CORRIDOR OUTSIDE KUNLE'S OFFICE — 2:52 PM**
*(~9:56–10:38)*

*Chloe arrives with the cooler, pale with fever, with Musa a step behind her. Rita looks up from her desk, startled and nervous.*

**RITA:** *(standing too quickly)* Madam! Good afternoon. You're early. Please, sit, I'll—

*Kunle's office door opens. NKEM walks out, laughing and adjusting her dress, with a branded tote bag on her shoulder. She stops when she sees Chloe. They look at each other.*

**NKEM:** *(smiling sweetly)* Good afternoon, ma.

**CHLOE:** *(slowly)* Good afternoon. *(beat)* And you are?

**RITA:** *(jumping in)* She's… a drug rep, ma. Pharmaceutical. She came about the new antibiotics.

**NKEM:** *(tapping her bag)* Antibiotics. Yes.

*Nkem walks past, her perfume trailing behind her. Chloe watches her go all the way to the lift, then looks at Rita, who can't meet her eyes.*

**CHLOE:** *(quietly)* He didn't pick up my call at 2:14.

**RITA:** *(barely audible)* He was… in a meeting, ma.

*Kunle appears in the doorway with his tie slightly loose. He sees the cooler and smiles.*

**KUNLE:** My queen! And with Musa this time. You see? You can learn. *(He takes the cooler.)* Fried rice?

**CHLOE:** *(looking at the lift doors)* Fried rice.

**KUNLE:** Good girl. Go home and rest. You look terrible.

*He goes back in and closes the door. Chloe stands in the corridor. The perfume is still in the air.*

## SCENE 13 — RECYCLED
**INT. SILVERLINE AI STUDIOS, VICTORIA ISLAND, LAGOS — BREAK ROOM — SAME FRIDAY, 2:20 PM**
*(~10:38–11:24)*

*A busy creative studio full of screens. EMMA WILLIAMS (35), sharp and stylish, eats suya from paper and laughs at her phone. BISI, her colleague, leans in.*

**BISI:** What's funny?

**EMMA:** Somebody just asked me whether to cook jollof or fried rice.

**BISI:** And you answered?

**EMMA:** Jollof with turkey. Obviously. I'm not a monster.

**BISI:** Wait, isn't that the new SIM you bought last week?

**EMMA:** Yes. 0814 333 2197. The girl at the shop said it was a "fresh line."

**BISI:** *(laughing)* Fresh line? In Lagos? Emma, that number has a history. Recycled numbers carry other people's wahala.

**EMMA:** *(putting the phone away)* Then I'll return the wahala to sender. It's just rice, Bisi.

**BISI:** *(raising an eyebrow)* It's never just rice.

## SCENE 14 — "FRIDAY STRANGER"
**INT. CHLOE'S BEDROOM, ABUJA / INT. EMMA'S APARTMENT, LAGOS — INTERCUT — THE NEXT FRIDAY, 2:19 PM**
*(~11:24–12:05)*

*Chloe folds laundry, fever gone. She scrolls back to the stranger's message. She remembers serving the fried rice last Friday. She hesitates, then types.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** For your information, fried rice was the correct answer.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Ah. The rice woman returns. Correct according to who?

**CHLOE (TEXT):** My husband.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Then your husband simply has bad taste 😂

**CHLOE (TEXT):** You don't know him.

**EMMA (TEXT):** True. But anybody who rejects good jollof is already suspicious.

*Chloe laughs out loud, a real laugh she hasn't heard from herself in years. Ada runs in.*

**ADA:** Mummy! You're laughing!

**CHLOE:** *(surprised at herself)* Yes. I suppose I am.

**ADA:** Who made you laugh?

**CHLOE:** *(looking at the phone)* A stranger.

*Later, alone, Chloe edits the contact. She deletes "Darling New" and types: "FRIDAY STRANGER." Save.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Same time next Friday?

**EMMA (TEXT):** Only if you bring better rice opinions.

## SCENE 15 — "WHO DID YOU USE TO BE?"
**MONTAGE — LAGOS AND ABUJA — FRIDAYS OVER TWO MONTHS**
*(~12:05–13:00)*

**EMMA (TEXT):** Serious question. Is pineapple on pizza a crime?

**CHLOE (TEXT):** My daughter eats it.

**EMMA (TEXT):** She's young. Rehabilitation is possible.

*Another Friday.*

**EMMA (TEXT):** How was your week, Rice Woman?

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Don't call me that 😂

**EMMA (TEXT):** Then tell me your name.

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Chloe.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Emma. Nice to meet you, Chloe of the disputed rice.

*Another Friday, late at night. Chloe sits on the bedroom floor while Kunle snores.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Emma, do you think people can forget who they used to be?

**EMMA (TEXT):** Yes. But I don't think the old person disappears completely.

**CHLOE (TEXT):** How do you find her again?

**EMMA (TEXT):** Start with one thing that used to make you happy.

*Chloe looks up at the top shelf of the wardrobe. She stands on a stool and pulls down a dusty folder: "CHLOE ADEYEMI — INTERIOR DESIGN PORTFOLIO." She opens it. Sketches of beautiful rooms. Colour swatches. Her own handwriting.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** I think I remember something.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Then hold on to it. Don't let anybody put it back on that shelf.

## SCENE 16 — THE VOICE
**INT. CHLOE'S CAR (PARKED IN THE GARAGE), ABUJA / INT. EMMA'S BALCONY, LAGOS — FRIDAY, 9:40 PM**
*(~13:00–13:50)*

*Chloe sits in the back seat of the family Lexus in the dark garage. It's the only place in the house without ears.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Can I call you? Just once. I want to know what you sound like.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Only if you promise not to judge my Lagos accent.

*Chloe's thumb hovers. Then she presses call. It rings once.*

**EMMA:** *(warm, a little nervous)* Hello? Rice Woman?

**CHLOE:** *(a shaky laugh)* You sound exactly like your texts.

**EMMA:** Is that good or bad?

**CHLOE:** *(quietly)* It's good. *(beat)* You sound like somebody who isn't angry.

**EMMA:** *(softly)* Chloe… why are you whispering?

**CHLOE:** Because I'm in the garage. *(beat)* It's the only room in my house where nobody listens.

*Silence in Lagos. Then headlights sweep across the garage wall. The gate is opening. Kunle's car.*

**CHLOE:** *(panicking)* He's home. I have to go.

**EMMA:** Chloe—

*Click. Chloe wipes her face, checks herself in the mirror, practises a smile and steps out of the car just as Kunle's headlights hit her.*

**KUNLE:** *(from his window)* What are you doing in the garage at this hour?

**CHLOE:** *(smiling)* Looking for Tobi's toy, darling.

## SCENE 17 — "MY WIFE WOULD FORGET HER OWN HEAD"
**INT. ADEYEMI MANSION — DINING ROOM — NIGHT — SIX MONTHS LATER**
*(~13:50–15:30)*

*A dinner party. Kunle hosts four business GUESTS. Chloe has cooked everything and stands serving.*

**GUEST:** Kunle, this egusi is a masterpiece. Your wife should open a restaurant!

**KUNLE:** *(laughing)* Please, don't encourage her. If I leave Chloe alone, she'll forget her own head. I have to supervise everything in this house. Even the salt.

*The guests laugh. Chloe smiles along.*

**GUEST:** And she used to be a designer, abi? I remember the wedding brochure.

**CHLOE:** Yes, I trained in interior design. I've been thinking of taking small—

**KUNLE:** *(cutting in smoothly)* She's been thinking of making my life difficult. *(laughter)* Chloe, darling, bring the dessert.

*LATER. The guests are gone. Kunle loosens his tie.*

**KUNLE:** "Small projects"? You announced that in front of MY guests?

**CHLOE:** I was only answering the question.

**KUNLE:** You always have an explanation. *(He leaves.)*

*Chloe sits on the edge of the bed in the dark and opens FRIDAY STRANGER.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Emma, can somebody make you feel useless without ever saying the word "useless"?

**EMMA (TEXT):** Yes. How someone treats you matters as much as the words they use.

**CHLOE (TEXT):** And what if everybody thinks he's wonderful?

**EMMA (TEXT):** They're judging the man they see at dinner. You're living with the man you know at night.

*A long pause. Chloe's thumb shakes.*

**CHLOE (TEXT):** I think I'm afraid of my husband.

*In Lagos, Emma puts down her plate.*

**EMMA (TEXT):** Chloe. Are you and the children safe right now?

**CHLOE (TEXT):** Yes. He's asleep.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Then you don't have to decide anything tonight. But you shouldn't have to live afraid. Is there one person in Abuja you trust?

**CHLOE (TEXT):** I used to have friends.

**EMMA (TEXT):** Maybe one of them is still waiting to hear from you.

## SCENE 18 — HALIMA
**INT. CAFÉ, WUSE 2, ABUJA — DAY**
*(~15:30–16:20)*

*Chloe sits across from HALIMA, her old university friend, with sunglasses on indoors.*

**HALIMA:** Three years, Chloe. Three years, and suddenly you call me at 7 a.m. and say "please, can we see"? *(beat)* Take off the glasses.

**CHLOE:** Because I thought you'd ask me why I stayed.

**HALIMA:** I'm not asking why you stayed. I'm asking how I can help you. *(beat)* Who told you to call me?

**CHLOE:** *(a small smile)* A stranger who answered a wrong number.

## SCENE 19 — "WHO GAVE YOU PERMISSION?"
