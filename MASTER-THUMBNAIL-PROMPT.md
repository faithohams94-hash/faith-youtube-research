# Master Thumbnail Prompt (Quiet Equity niche)

There are two parts.
- PART A: paste it into a chat with your title (and script if you have it) to get 5 thumbnail concepts and prompts.
- PART B: a fill-in template for writing a single thumbnail prompt by hand.

---

## PART A: Title → 5 thumbnails

```
You are the thumbnail designer for a faceless YouTube channel that tells cinematic second-person money stories. Create 5 high-CTR thumbnail concepts for the video below.

VIDEO TITLE: [PASTE TITLE]
SCRIPT OR KEY MOMENTS (optional): [PASTE SCRIPT OR 3–5 KEY SCENES]

LOCKED PROTAGONIST (describe him fully in every prompt)
A young man with an oversized head, messy curly jet-black hair, thick black rectangular glasses, heavy half-lidded tired eyes with faint dark under-eye lines, thick slightly furrowed black eyebrows, pale off-white skin and a small flat frowning mouth. He is ALWAYS deadpan, giving a calm, knowing side-eye straight at the viewer, and is always the darkest, sharpest shape in the frame.
Signature look (matches the channel logo, brand/protagonist-reference.webp): black hoodie with the hood up. Attach that image as a character reference whenever your generator allows it.
Outfit by wealth tier: plain black hoodie with the hood up (broke) → black crewneck sweater (middle) → dark tailored jacket over a black tee (rich).

THE THUMBNAIL FORMULA (every concept must follow it)
1. ANCHOR: the protagonist is chest-up, takes up 35–45% of the frame on the left or center, and faces the viewer.
2. CALM VS CHAOS: behind him, a busy, detailed scene shows the premise at a glance. Background characters are hyper-expressive (shocked, furious, crying, laughing, bowing, envious) while he stays perfectly still. That contrast is the hook.
3. STORY PROPS: 1–2 objects that tell the story instantly (a bill, a phone with a green check mark, keys, a gold card, a menu, a wristband or price tag).
4. CONTRAST DEVICE (use at least one): rich versus poor split-screen, warm gold versus cold blue lighting, a cutaway cross-section, or before and after.
5. COLOR: bright, saturated, high-contrast background; his dark clothing pops; ONE red accent (an arrow, an angry face, a tie, a carpet); green for success or money; gold for wealth.
6. TEXT (optional, used in about half of the concepts): 1–3 words OR a number jump ("$0 → $50M", "SECRET FLOOR", "THEY BOW NOW"). Heavy condensed all-caps sans-serif, cream or white with a thick black outline and a drop shadow, placed top-left or top-center, never over his face, never repeating the title. Optionally add one small hand-drawn red arrow pointing to the key prop.
7. SAFE ZONE: keep the bottom-right corner clear for the duration badge, and keep the image readable at mobile size.

EMOTION TRIGGERS (give each concept a different one)
- Status reversal (the people who looked down now bow)
- Injustice or outrage (absurd prices, line-cutting)
- A secret or exclusivity (a hidden floor, a gold button, a door with no sign)
- Quiet revenge or vindication
- Before and after or wealth voyeurism
- Envy or social comparison

STYLE (put it inside every prompt)
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720, flat 2D cartoon webcomic illustration, bold clean black outlines, flat cel shading, semi-chibi characters with oversized heads, bright detailed background, high contrast, crisp and readable at small size.

OUTPUT FORMAT (for each of the 5)
## [#]. [Concept name]
- Visual concept: one sentence
- Text overlay: exact words and position, or "None"
- Emotion trigger: …
- Prompt: one fully standalone paragraph covering scene, protagonist description, background characters, props, lighting, text overlay, style and safe zone

After the 5 concepts, recommend the strongest one in a single line.
```

## PART B: Single thumbnail template

```
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720. The protagonist, a young man with an oversized head, messy curly jet-black hair, thick black rectangular glasses, heavy half-lidded tired eyes with faint dark under-eye lines, thick slightly furrowed black eyebrows, pale off-white skin and a small flat frowning mouth, wearing [OUTFIT], is chest-up on the [left/center], giving the viewer a calm deadpan side-eye, and is the darkest shape in the frame.
[PROP] in his hand or beside him.
Behind him: [BUSY PREMISE SCENE] with hyper-expressive [shocked/angry/envious/bowing] background characters.
Lighting: [warm gold / cold blue / split warm vs cold].
Text: "[1–3 WORDS or $X → $Y]" in heavy condensed all-caps cream letters with a thick black outline, at [top-left/top-center][, with a small red arrow pointing to PROP].
Flat 2D cartoon webcomic illustration, bold clean black outlines, flat cel shading, bright saturated high-contrast background with one red accent, bottom-right corner kept clear.
```
