# POV: You Still Live With Your Parents at 30: Thumbnails v2 (high-curiosity)

The rules behind these:
- ONE focal point and ONE big emotional face, readable on a phone at thumbnail size.
- A curiosity gap: the viewer sees a reaction or a secret but not the full story, so they have to click.
- Maximum 3 words of text that add to the title and never repeat it.
- Extreme light contrast: a dark frame with one glowing element.

Attach brand/protagonist-reference.png as a character reference for every prompt.

PROTAGONIST = a young man with an oversized head, messy curly jet-black hair, thick black rectangular glasses, heavy half-lidded tired eyes, thick slightly furrowed black eyebrows, pale off-white skin and a small flat mouth
