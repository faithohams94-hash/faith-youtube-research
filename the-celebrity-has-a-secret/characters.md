# THE CELEBRITY HAS A SECRET — CHARACTERS & IMAGE PROMPTS

Every prompt is written for photorealistic, cinematic AI images (Midjourney, Leonardo, Flux, Kling or similar). Generate each main character once from their prompt, save the best image as that character's **reference face**, and reuse that reference in every scene so the face stays the same throughout the film.

**Style line to add to every prompt:** *cinematic film still, photorealistic, shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, soft film grain, modern Lagos, Nigeria*

---

## MAIN CHARACTERS

### 1. SIENNA VALE (32)
**Who she is:** Nigeria's biggest singer and actress. Glamorous, quick-witted and famous for being "cold". Behind the wall, she's a survivor who escaped an abusive stepfather as a teenager, changed her name from Naomi Cole and built her career from a laundry job and Tuesday-night bar gigs. Fiercely protective of her best friend June.
**Image prompt (stage look, Scenes 1–7):** A stunning 32-year-old Black Nigerian woman with deep brown skin, high cheekbones and full lips painted deep red, long glossy platinum-blonde straight hair (lace wig) falling past her shoulders, striking pale grey contact lenses. She wears a fitted floor-length gold gown covered in crystals with a high slit, diamond drop earrings and gold crystal-studded stiletto heels. Standing alone in a white spotlight on a huge concert stage, stage smoke behind her, 40,000 phone lights glowing in the dark arena.
**Image prompt (everyday look):** The same woman, bleached-blonde hair in a messy bun, grey contact lenses, no makeup except lip balm, oversized grey hoodie and black leggings, gold hoop earrings, in a bright, luxurious cream-coloured Ikoyi home, soft morning light, guarded but tired expression.
**Image prompt (final scene):** The same woman, blonde hair tied up loosely, contact lenses removed so her real warm dark-brown eyes show, wearing an old soft T-shirt, sitting at a kitchen counter with a steaming bowl, laughing freely, warm lamp light.

### 2. NAOMI COLE (about 10, seen only in an old photograph)
**Who she is:** Sienna as a child, before she escaped. She appears only in the photograph Victor brings to the newsroom (Scene 31). Never shown in any flashback.
**Image prompt:** An old, slightly faded 2000s family photograph of a 10-year-old Black Nigerian girl with dark-brown eyes, dark hair in neat plaits with white ribbons, a simple yellow school uniform and an uncertain, careful half-smile, standing against a plain blue wall. Slightly creased photo paper, warm faded colours.

### 3. MARA (43)
**Who she is:** Sienna's manager: sharp, practical, always on schedule. She treats every problem as a publicity problem until she finally understands this one isn't. Becomes Sienna's protector.
**Image prompt:** A 43-year-old Black Nigerian woman with a short, neat natural tapered haircut, minimal elegant makeup, small gold studs, a tailored black trouser suit with a crisp white blouse, holding a tablet, standing in a modern glass office. Confident, no-nonsense expression with a hint of warmth.

### 4. TOBI (26)
**Who he is:** Sienna's personal assistant. Witty, fashionable, always carrying too many bags and "three emergency biscuits". His jokes hide a big heart, and his own mother once changed her name to escape his father.
**Image prompt:** A slim 26-year-old Black Nigerian man with a neat low-cut fade and a thin moustache, a playful bright smile, wearing a stylish patterned short-sleeve shirt, slim beige chinos and white trainers, a canvas tote bag on each shoulder, holding a packet of biscuits, in a glamorous backstage corridor.

### 5. JUNE (32)
**Who she is:** Sienna's oldest friend since school. Warm, funny and down-to-earth, a single mother living in a modest flat in Surulere. Her aunt gave teenage Sienna a safe place to stay. Her child is never shown or named.
**Image prompt:** A warm-faced 32-year-old Black Nigerian woman with a round face and kind eyes, her hair tied up in a colourful ankara head scarf, wearing a loose ankara house dress, standing in the doorway of a small, cosy, modest Lagos living room with a worn green sofa, laughing.

### 6. ELI OKAFOR (30)
**Who he is:** An ambitious entertainment reporter at the digital outlet Lagos Lens. He sets out to expose Sienna's "secret" and slowly realises the real story is the man trying to sell her past.
**Image prompt:** A 30-year-old Black Nigerian man with short twisted hair and a trimmed beard, an intelligent and restless expression, wearing a denim jacket over a black T-shirt, a press lanyard round his neck, sitting at a cluttered newsroom desk covered in sticky notes, lit by his computer screen.

### 7. KEMI (40)
**Who she is:** Eli's editor. Dry, unflappable and ethical. Her job is to stop him from becoming the story's villain.
**Image prompt:** A 40-year-old Black Nigerian woman with shoulder-length braids tied back, reading glasses on a beaded chain, a mustard-yellow blouse, holding a coffee mug, standing in a glass-walled editor's office in a modern Yaba newsroom with exposed brick. Calm, sceptical, slightly amused.

### 8. DANIEL (40s)
**Who he is:** Sienna's head of security. Big, calm and few words. He watches everything and promises he'll never leave the house.
**Image prompt:** A tall, broad-shouldered Black Nigerian man in his 40s with a clean-shaven head and a short beard, wearing a sharp black suit, white shirt and no tie, with a discreet earpiece, standing with hands clasped in front of a black SUV at night. Calm, watchful eyes.

### 9. VICTOR (58)
**Who he is:** Sienna's stepfather, the man she escaped. Respected in public and abusive at home. He finds her, sends threats and tries to sell her past to the press. **Never show him before Scene 31.**
**Image prompt:** A well-groomed 58-year-old Black Nigerian man with short greying hair and grey temples, a neatly trimmed grey moustache, wearing a pressed light-blue long-sleeve shirt and a gold wristwatch, sitting at a bare table in a plain interview room. A practised, pleasant church-elder smile that doesn't reach his eyes.

### 10. ADA NWOSU (48)
**Who she is:** A respected TV journalist who conducts the final interview on Sienna's terms: no childhood photo and no sad music.
**Image prompt:** An elegant 48-year-old Black Nigerian woman with short grey-streaked natural hair, small pearl earrings, a tailored deep-plum dress, sitting in an armchair in a softly lit TV interview studio. Calm, trustworthy, kind expression.

### 11. MAYA (15)
**Who she is:** A teenage girl in Ikorodu who watches Sienna's interview and finally writes down her dream: to become an architect.
**Image prompt:** A bright-eyed 15-year-old Black Nigerian girl with neat cornrows, wearing a simple oversized T-shirt, sitting cross-legged on a rug in a small modest living room, hugging her knees, her face lit by a TV screen, a school exercise book beside her.

### 12. RUTH (40s)
**Who she is:** Maya's mother, a nurse's aide. Tired and loving, always sewing a button back on.
**Image prompt:** A gentle Black Nigerian woman in her 40s with her hair in a simple low bun, wearing a pale-blue nurse's-aide uniform, sitting on a second-hand sofa sewing a button onto a white school shirt, warm lamp light, small modest flat.

### 13. BEN (40s)
**Who he is:** Maya's father, a security guard working twelve-hour shifts. Funny, patient and determined to help his daughter.
**Image prompt:** A strong, tired-looking Black Nigerian man in his 40s with a short beard and kind eyes, wearing a navy security-company uniform shirt, sitting on a small sofa with his work boots beside him, smiling warmly.
