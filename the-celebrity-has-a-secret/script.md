# THE CELEBRITY HAS A SECRET
## "Everyone Knows Her Name. Nobody Knows What It Cost Her."

*A Lagos drama in 46 scenes. About 50 minutes. All dialogue, no narrator. Every scene opens with a visual paragraph. Every line of dialogue is followed by an **after-action** note (lip-sync and body language) for your AI video prompts. Story by Mary Lade, adapted scene for scene. Nothing in the plot has been changed.*

---

## HOW TO READ THIS SCRIPT

- **Scene opening (italic paragraph).** What the camera sees before anyone speaks. Use it for your establishing shot.
- **Dialogue.** `**NAME:** words`. These are the only spoken words in the film.
- **After-action** (`↳` line under each line of dialogue). **Lip-sync** says how the mouth moves and the delivery. **Body** gives posture, hands, eyes and movement. Use it to prompt the shot of that line.
- **ON SCREEN** cards are text the audience reads: phone screens, headlines, messages.
- **Continuity:** Sienna's bleached-blonde hair and grey contacts stay the same until the final scene, where the contacts come out and her real brown eyes show. Her hair stays blonde.
- **Care rules (from the original script):** the abuse is never shown or re-enacted, and Victor is never shown or hinted at before Scene 31. June's child is never seen or named.

## SCENE 1 — PERFORMER TONIGHT
**INT. EKO GRAND ARENA, LAGOS — BACKSTAGE CORRIDOR — NIGHT**
*(~0:00–0:27)*

*Darkness. A heartbeat of bass thumps through the walls. Slowly, light rises on a long backstage corridor: black walls, cables taped to the floor and crew in headsets rushing past. The camera drifts to a closed white door. In the centre is a gold star, and under it a polished brass plate: "SIENNA VALE — PERFORMER TONIGHT." Somewhere far away, 40,000 people are chanting a name we can't make out yet. The camera pushes closer to the brass plate. A hand in a black headset glove knocks twice.*

**STAGE MANAGER (KOLA):** Miss Vale! Thirty minutes to stage. Thirty minutes!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: loud, crisp, mouth wide on "thirty". Body: leans toward the door, one hand pressing his headset to his ear, the other knuckle still against the wood, then hurries off down the corridor.*

**SIENNA (O.S., BEHIND THE DOOR):** Thank you, Kola.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: off screen, a calm, warm voice muffled by the door. Body: none visible. Hold on the gold star as the crowd's chant grows louder.*

**KOLA:** *(into his headset, walking away)* She's awake, she's calm, she's coming. Tell the band to breathe.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: fast, half-whispered into the mic, a small grin at the end. Body: walking quickly, glancing back over his shoulder at the door with a mix of nerves and pride.*

## SCENE 2 — ONLY HER LIPS
**INT. SIENNA'S DRESSING ROOM, EKO GRAND ARENA — CONTINUOUS**
*(~0:27–1:19)*

*A glowing mirror framed with round bulbs. Makeup brushes are laid out like surgical tools, and perfume bottles catch the light. Every shot here is from behind, from the side or in close-up, and her face is never fully seen. We see the back of her neck and a silk robe in champagne gold. A makeup artist's brush sweeps across a cheekbone, out of focus. In the mirror, only one thing is sharp: a pair of full lips being painted deep red, slowly, perfectly. A WIG STYLIST lowers a long, glossy, platinum-blonde wig onto her head, adjusts the lace at the temples and smooths it down. FUNKE, the makeup artist, steps back to admire her work. TOBI (26), her assistant, slim and fashionable with a tote bag on each shoulder, leans on the door frame, eating a biscuit.*

**FUNKE:** Don't press your lips together yet. Let it set.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: soft, concentrated, eyes on the mirror. Body: hovers the lip brush an inch away, her other hand gently holding Sienna's chin from the side, so Sienna's face stays hidden behind Funke's arm.*

**SIENNA:** If I don't press my lips together, I can't tell you you're a genius.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: only her painted lips visible in the mirror, barely moving, a teasing curl at one corner. Body: perfectly still in the chair, hands folded on her robe.*

**FUNKE:** *(laughing)* Then hum it.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: bright laugh, mouth open. Body: steps back, wipes the brush on a towel and shakes her head fondly.*

**WIG STYLIST (SADE):** Lace is invisible. Wind can blow, God can blow. This wig is not moving.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: proud, rhythmic Lagos delivery. Body: does a final smooth along the blonde hair, then lifts both hands away like a magician finishing a trick.*

**TOBI:** I have your in-ear monitors, your water, your throat spray and three emergency biscuits.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: crunchy, speaking through a bite, comic. Body: lifts each item out of the tote in turn, then holds up the biscuit packet guiltily.*

**SIENNA:** Three?
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: one word, lips just parting, eyebrows hidden. Body: slight tilt of the head toward the door, face still turned away from camera.*

**TOBI:** There were four. The sound check was emotional.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: deadpan, then a guilty half-smile. Body: brushes crumbs off his shirt and hides the packet behind his back.*

*Her lips curve into a smile in the mirror. A rack appears: a gold-and-crystal stage gown. Two dressers lift it. We see only the silk robe sliding off one shoulder, and then the gown shimmering into place. Hands do up a long zip. A stylist fastens diamond drops on each ear.*

**MARA (O.S.):** Twenty minutes, Sienna.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: off screen, firm, a manager's clipped voice. Body: in the doorway, MARA (43), in a tailored black suit with a tablet in her hand, appears only from the shoulders down.*

**SIENNA:** I heard Kola the first time, Mara.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: lips in the mirror, relaxed and amused. Body: rises from the chair; the gown catches the light; she turns away from the mirror so her face is never shown.*

**MARA:** I'm saying it with love.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: dry, small smile. Body: taps the tablet against her palm, then reaches out and adjusts the gown's shoulder with quick, practised fingers.*

## SCENE 3 — THE WALK
**INT. BACKSTAGE CORRIDOR → STAGE WINGS — MOMENTS LATER**
*(~1:19–1:51)*

*Low angle, floor level. A pair of gold, crystal-studded stiletto heels steps out of the dressing room onto black concrete. Click. Click. Click. Each step lands on the beat of the bass pumping through the walls. The camera tracks the shoes down the corridor. Crew members flatten against the walls to let her pass, and someone whispers "Wow." The roar of the crowd grows with every step. DANIEL, her security lead, big and calm in a black suit, walks one step behind her. At the end of the corridor, the stage wings glow with blue and gold light. Kola waits with a countdown on his tablet. The camera rises slowly up the gown and stops at her red lips. Her face is still hidden by the shadow of the curtain.*

**KOLA:** Sixty seconds. Band's in. Lights on your mark.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: urgent but steady, enunciating every word. Body: one finger raised, eyes flicking between his tablet and the stage.*

**DANIEL:** I'll be at stage left the whole time. Nobody comes up those stairs.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: low, calm, almost a murmur. Body: hands clasped in front of him, eyes scanning the crowd beyond the curtain, never on her.*

**SIENNA:** You say that every night.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: lips in close-up, a gentle smile. Body: lifts her chin, rolls her shoulders back, the gown shimmering.*

**DANIEL:** And every night it's true.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: small, sure smile. Body: a single nod, then takes his position at the foot of the stage stairs.*

**KOLA:** Five. Four. Three…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: whispering the numbers, then silently mouthing "two, one". Body: fingers folding down one by one, the other hand raised to signal the lighting desk.*

*Blackout. The crowd screams into the dark.*

## SCENE 4 — FOREVER IN LOVE
**INT. EKO GRAND ARENA — MAIN STAGE — CONTINUOUS**
*(~1:51–2:56)*

*Total darkness, then one white spotlight snaps on at centre stage. Smoke curls through the beam. Slowly, she walks into the light, and for the first time we see SIENNA VALE (32) in full: a stunning Black woman with long platinum-blonde hair, striking grey eyes and the gold gown blazing. The arena explodes. 40,000 phone lights rise like stars. Wide shots of the crowd show young women crying, a man holding up a sign that reads "MARRY ME SIENNA", and a girl on her father's shoulders screaming. Sienna lifts the microphone. Silence falls. A soft piano begins.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** *(soft, sweet, fairytale-princess voice)* You want me… now and forever…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, soft and airy, mouth shaping long vowels, eyes half-closed. Body: one hand on the mic, the other drifting slowly outward toward the crowd, palm up.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** I see you in the high… and you see me in my eyes…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, rising in pitch, a gentle vibrato on "eyes". Body: opens her eyes and looks out over the crowd, then touches two fingers to her heart.*

**CROWD:** *(screaming)* SIENNA! SIENNA! SIENNA!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: thousands of mouths shouting, faces wet with tears. Body: fans jumping, waving phones, a girl covering her mouth in disbelief.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** Holding you for life… oh… forever in love…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, building, chest voice on "forever", lips wide. Body: steps forward to the edge of the stage; the spotlight follows; her free arm sweeps across the arena.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** Ah… holding you forever… forever in love…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, the final high note held long, then softening to a whisper. Body: head tilted back, eyes closed, both hands holding the mic; on the last word, she smiles.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** When the lights go low and the whole world's calling… I only hear you, I only hear you…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, warm and low, intimate. Body: walks slowly along the stage edge, trailing her fingertips toward reaching hands without touching them.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** You found me in the dark… you knew my name before the stars…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sung, rising, a catch of emotion on "my name". Body: stops at centre stage, eyes closed, one hand pressed flat to her chest.*

**CROWD:** *(screaming)* WE LOVE YOU, SIENNA!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: thousands of mouths, a roaring chant. Body: a wave of phone lights swaying left to right across the arena.*

**SIENNA (SINGING):** *(bridge, soft, almost whispered)* And if they ask me who I am… I'll say I'm yours… I'll say I'm free…
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: breathy, slow, every syllable separate. Body: the band drops out; only piano; she kneels at the edge of the stage in the spotlight, the gown pooling around her.*

*The drums kick in. The full band explodes. Fireworks shoot up behind her. She rises from her knees as the whole arena sings the chorus back to her.*

**CROWD:** *(singing together)* Holding you forever… forever in love!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: a sea of singing mouths, out of time and joyful. Body: arms swaying, phone lights waving side to side, couples hugging.*

**SIENNA:** *(into the mic, laughing, breathless)* Lagos! You're louder than my band!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: shouting with joy, a big open smile. Body: points the mic at the crowd, bounces on her heels and spins once in the gown.*

**FAN IN FRONT ROW:** *(crying)* I love you, Sienna! You saved my life!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sobbing, shouting through tears. Body: a young woman gripping the barrier with both hands, shaking.*

*Sienna catches the fan's eye and presses her hand to her heart toward her. Close on Sienna's face: joy, and something else just behind it for half a second. Then the stage lights blind white.*

## SCENE 5 — "YOU WERE UNREAL"
**INT. BACKSTAGE CORRIDOR — AFTER THE SHOW — NIGHT**
*(~2:56–3:40)*

*Sienna walks off stage into the corridor, glowing with sweat and holding her heels in one hand. Instantly the crew mobs her: dancers in sequins, band members with guitars still strapped on, sound engineers, Tobi and Mara. Champagne pops somewhere. Everyone is hugging, shouting and jumping.*

**DANCER (LOLA):** Sienna! That last note! I forgot my own choreography!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: screaming happily, mouth wide. Body: throws her arms around Sienna, bouncing.*

**SIENNA:** I saw. You turned left when the whole Lagos turned right.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: laughing, teasing, out of breath. Body: hugs Lola back with one arm, her heels dangling from the other hand.*

**GUITARIST:** Forty thousand people singing your song, Sienna. Forty thousand!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: awestruck, slow on "forty thousand". Body: lifts both hands to his head, the guitar swinging on his back.*

**TOBI:** I cried twice. The second time was because I finished the biscuits.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: comic sniff, then a big grin. Body: wipes a fake tear, then hugs her tightly, rocking side to side.*

**MARA:** *(stepping in, unusually soft)* You were unreal tonight.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: quiet, sincere, a rare smile. Body: holds Sienna by both shoulders and looks at her properly for a moment.*

**SIENNA:** Careful, Mara. That sounded like a compliment.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: playful, eyebrows raised. Body: covers Mara's hand on her shoulder with her own.*

**MARA:** Enjoy it. Tomorrow I'm back to being your schedule.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: dry, then a short laugh. Body: lets go, checks her tablet again, but still smiling.*

## SCENE 6 — THE FLASHES
**EXT. EKO GRAND ARENA — VIP EXIT — NIGHT**
*(~3:40–4:11)*

*The steel exit doors swing open into chaos. A wall of white flashes. Paparazzi push against a rope line, shouting all at once, cameras held high. Fans scream behind them. A black Mercedes Maybach waits at the kerb with its back door open. Daniel and two guards form a moving wall around Sienna, now in a cream coat over the gown, sunglasses on at night. Every flash freezes her face for a split second.*

**PAPARAZZO 1:** Sienna! Sienna! Over here! Look this way!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: shouting, mouth stretched wide. Body: leaning over the rope with the camera held above his head, firing nonstop.*

**PAPARAZZO 2:** Who are you going home to tonight, Sienna? Who's the lucky man?
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sly, shouting the question. Body: shoves forward, a long lens almost touching Daniel's shoulder.*

**DANIEL:** Back. Give her space. Back, please.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: firm, even, never shouting. Body: one arm extended like a gate, the other hand guiding Sienna by the elbow without gripping.*

**SIENNA:** *(pausing at the car, a small wave)* Goodnight, Lagos. Go home safely.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: warm, measured smile under the sunglasses. Body: a short royal wave, then lowers herself into the car with one hand holding the door frame.*

*The door shuts. Through the tinted window, the flashes keep exploding like lightning as the car pulls away.*

## SCENE 7 — "LAGOS STOOD STILL"
**INT. NEWS STUDIO, CHANNELS 9 → INT. SIENNA'S CAR — NIGHT — INTERCUT**
*(~4:11–5:13)*

*A bright television news set with a glass desk and a giant screen showing the arena lit by phones. A polished NEWS ANCHOR, CHIOMA BELLO, sits upright, papers squared in front of her, and turns to camera two. Behind her, the screen cuts to footage: fans crying and fainting into the arms of medics, a teenage boy shaking and sobbing "She looked at me!", and a crowd outside the arena still singing at midnight, scenes that recall the biggest pop stars in history.*

**NEWS ANCHOR (CHIOMA):** Good evening. Lagos stood still tonight as superstar Sienna Vale performed to a sold-out crowd of more than forty thousand fans at the Eko Grand Arena.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: crisp newsreader diction, a controlled smile. Body: hands folded on the desk, a slight turn of the head to the second camera.*

**NEWS ANCHOR (CHIOMA):** Medics treated dozens of fans for exhaustion and emotion. Some had queued since five o'clock this morning.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: steady, slightly amazed at "five o'clock". Body: glances at her notes, then back up to camera.*

**CRYING FAN (ON NEWS FOOTAGE):** She's not human! That woman is not human! I've waited my whole life!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sobbing, shouting into the reporter's mic. Body: hands on her head, mascara running, a friend holding her up.*

*INT. SIENNA'S CAR — MOVING. The city lights of Lagos slide past the window: the Third Mainland Bridge glowing over black water. Sienna sits in the back seat, gown and coat, shoes off, feet tucked up, sunglasses pushed up into her blonde hair. She watches the same broadcast on her phone, the screen lighting her face. She's smiling, a real, private, girlish smile. At the wheel is UNCLE SULE (60s), her driver, in a grey cap, glancing at her in the rear-view mirror.*

**UNCLE SULE:** Madam, they said forty thousand. On the radio, the man said forty-five.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: amused, proud, the Hausa accent warm. Body: eyes smiling in the rear-view mirror, both hands steady on the wheel.*

**SIENNA:** The radio man always adds five thousand, Uncle Sule. He's very generous.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: laughing softly, relaxed. Body: leans her head against the window, phone still in her hand.*

**UNCLE SULE:** Eight years I have driven you. From small clubs with ten people to this. *(beat)* You're happy tonight. I can see it.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: gentle, fatherly, slow. Body: a quick look back over his shoulder, then eyes back on the road.*

**SIENNA:** *(looking at the phone, smiling)* Tonight, I'm happy.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: whispered, the smile reaching her eyes. Body: hugs her knees closer, watching the crowd sing on the little screen.*

*She turns the phone off and watches the city go by. The car glides into the dark. Hold on her reflection in the window: blonde, grey-eyed, smiling. Then the reflection's smile slowly fades, as if a thought has crossed it. Cut to black.*

## SCENE 8 — THE HAND
**EXT. LAGOS AWARDS VENUE, VICTORIA ISLAND — NIGHT**
*(~5:13–5:57)*

*A red carpet under a white marquee. Flashbulbs fire nonstop. A sleek black car glides up to the kerb, and the door opens. Sienna steps out in a sculpted gold gown, blonde hair swept to one side, grey eyes cool. Behind her, Tobi tumbles out of the car, wrestling with a small evening bag and a huge black umbrella that's opening by accident. Daniel walks a step behind. Photographers crowd the barrier, shouting over each other.*

**TOBI:** I have your phone, your lipstick and three emergency biscuits.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: hurried, breathless. Body: fights the umbrella closed, tucks the bag under his arm, nearly trips on the carpet edge.*

**SIENNA:** Three?
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: one dry word, the corner of her mouth twitching. Body: doesn't turn around, just tilts her head back toward him.*

**TOBI:** There were four. Traffic was emotional.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: deadpan, guilty. Body: brushes a crumb from his lapel.*

*Sienna almost smiles. The photographers roar.*

**PHOTOGRAPHER (DELE):** Sienna! Here! Give us something!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: shouting, aggressive grin. Body: leaning over the barrier, the camera already firing.*

**SIENNA:** I gave you an album last month.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: cool, crisp, eyebrows lifted. Body: a brief, practised pose, hand on hip, chin angled, then begins to walk on.*

*Suddenly, Dele steps through the barrier and grabs her wrist to turn her toward his lens.*

**SIENNA:** Let go.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: low, sharp, both words clipped. Body: freezes, eyes dropping to the hand on her wrist, her whole body going rigid.*

**DELE:** Just one picture. Don't be difficult.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: smirking, dismissive. Body: tightens his grip and pulls her a half-step toward him.*

**SIENNA:** Take your picture. Keep your hands off me.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: louder, controlled anger, every word clear. Body: twists her wrist free, steps back and holds her arm against her body.*

*Daniel moves between them in one smooth step and separates them. Nearby, a phone held up in the crowd records only this last moment.*

**DELE:** *(shouting as Daniel moves him back)* Your fans made you!
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: shouting, indignant. Body: stumbling back behind the barrier, pointing at her.*

**SIENNA:** Then respect what they made.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: quiet, steady, ice in the voice. Body: looks straight at him for one second, then turns and walks inside with her back straight.*

*INT. VENUE LIFT — MOMENTS LATER. Mirrored walls. The doors close and the noise vanishes. Sienna stands very still. Her wrist trembles. She presses it flat against her gown to hide it. Tobi watches her in the mirror.*

**TOBI:** *(softly)* Did he hurt you?
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: gentle, careful, nothing like his joking voice. Body: half-turns toward her, his hand lifting slightly as if to help, then stopping.*

**SIENNA:** Fix your collar.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: flat, quick, deflecting. Body: eyes fixed on the lift doors, chin up.*

*Tobi looks down. His collar is already straight. He looks back at her and says nothing.*

*CUT TO: a phone screen in someone's hand, replaying the cropped video in a loop: Sienna saying "Keep your hands off me!" The caption reads: "SIENNA HUMILIATES PHOTOGRAPHER 😱". Views climb in real time: 10K… 80K… 1.2M.*

## SCENE 9 — ICE
**INT. SIENNA'S HOME, IKOYI — KITCHEN — LATE THAT NIGHT**
*(~5:57–6:42)*

*Midnight. The huge kitchen is dark except for the cold light of the open freezer. Sienna, still in the gold gown with her heels kicked off on the marble, presses a bag of frozen peas against her wrist. Her phone, face up on the counter, is a constant stream of notifications. Tobi stands in the doorway in his coat, car keys in hand, unwilling to leave.*

**TOBI:** I can stay. I'll sleep on the sofa. I'm very small. You won't notice me.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: gentle, trying for a joke. Body: hovering in the doorway, keys jingling nervously.*

**SIENNA:** Go home, Tobi. Your mother will think I kidnapped you.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: tired, a faint smile. Body: doesn't turn around, keeps the frozen bag pressed to her wrist.*

**TOBI:** My mother thinks you're a saint. She has your poster in her kitchen.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: warm, a little proud. Body: steps one foot into the kitchen.*

**SIENNA:** *(glancing at the phone)* Then tell her not to read the internet tonight.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: dry, flat. Body: flips the phone face down with one finger.*

**TOBI:** *(quietly)* That man grabbed you, Sienna. You don't have to be funny about it.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: soft, serious, careful. Body: stops moving, his eyes on her wrist.*

**SIENNA:** Goodnight, Tobi.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: quiet, kind, final. Body: turns back to the freezer light.*

*He nods and leaves. The front door closes. Sienna stands alone. She lifts the frozen bag and looks at the red marks of fingers on her wrist. Her breath shakes. She presses the cold bag back against it and closes her eyes.*

## SCENE 10 — THE ICE QUEEN
**INT. SIENNA'S DRESSING ROOM, IKOYI — MORNING**
*(~6:42–7:37)*

**TOBI:** "Rich, rude and out of control."
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: reading aloud in a dramatic newsreader voice. Body: holds the tablet up like a scroll, one eyebrow raised.*

**SIENNA:** That's three reviews. Are they charging separately?
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: dry, mouth half full of toast. Body: doesn't look up, brushes crumbs off her robe.*

**SIENNA:** Release it. I'm not recording an apology for removing somebody's hand.

**TOBI:** This one says you hate ordinary people.

**SIENNA:** I haven't met all of them yet.

**SIENNA:** Blue. Silver makes me look like an expensive refrigerator.

**SIENNA:** They can know my work.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: low, final, every word separate. Body: stands up and turns to the mirror, ending the conversation.*

*Silence. Tobi quietly turns off his tablet. As the screen dims, one last notification slides across it: "WHAT IS SIENNA HIDING?"*

## SCENE 11 — "SHE MOVED HIS HAND"
**INT. MARA'S CAR, PARKED OUTSIDE SIENNA'S HOUSE — DAY**
*(~7:37–8:24)*

*Mara sits alone in her white SUV in the driveway, engine running, AC on. She pauses it on the hand. Her phone rings: "BLESSING — GISTHUB BLOG." She answers on speaker.*

**BLOGGER (BLESSING, ON PHONE):** Mara, darling! Just one quote. "Sienna regrets the outburst." Five words. Everybody's happy.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: sugary, fast, a fake friendliness. Body (intercut): a woman in a ring light, filming herself while she talks.*

**MARA:** I'm looking at the full video, Blessing. A grown man grabbed a woman's wrist on a red carpet.

## SCENE 12 — ONE QUESTION

**SIENNA:** But don't let somebody's cruelty write your whole future. The life you're living now may not be the only life you can have.

**ADA:** Thank you, Sienna.
*↳ After action. Lip-sync: warm, quiet. Body: reaches over and briefly touches Sienna's hand.*
