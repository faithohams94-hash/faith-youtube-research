## Adding the banner (Canva or CapCut)

1. Canvas **1280 × 720**. Place the art (or the two halves side by side, with a white diagonal stripe between them).
2. A dark bar across the top, about 20% of the height, 85% opacity.
3. Text in one line: **$4 LUNCH** in white · **vs.** small in white · **$2.4M OWNER** in bright yellow (#FFD400).
4. Font: a heavy condensed font such as **Anton** or **Bebas Neue**, about 100 px tall.
5. Thick black outline (about 8 px) and a hard drop shadow straight down.
6. No speech bubbles. Nothing in the bottom-right corner.
7. Shrink the preview to phone size: if you can't read the banner or see the container and the keys, make them bigger.

## One thing to decide

In the script, the building you buy is a **four-story red-brick** office building. This concept uses a **sleek modern glass tower**, which looks richer on a thumbnail but doesn't match the video. If you'd rather match the story, swap *"a sleek modern glass office tower"* for *"a handsome four-story red-brick office building"* in the prompts.
