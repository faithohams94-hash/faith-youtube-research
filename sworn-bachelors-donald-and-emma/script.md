# THE SWORN BACHELORS — PART ONE: DONALD × EMMA
## "The Billionaire Who Swore Never to Love"

*A Nigerian romance-suspense drama in 45 scenes. Dialogue-led, with about 10% narration.*

---

## THE BIG IDEA

Three boys from Ajegunle swore on a rooftop that they would never fall in love. Twenty-three years later, all three of them have, at the same time, and none of them will admit it. This story follows Donald. While he is hiding Emma from his brothers, Michael and Geoffrey are hiding something too, so nobody can question anybody without exposing himself.

## CAST

- **DONALD IRWIN** (37). Self-made billionaire and CEO of Everys Conglomerate (ports, telecoms, EveryPay fintech, Eko Atlantic real estate). Brilliant and generous, and controlling to the bone. He has to be in charge of everything because, once, he was in charge of nothing.
- **EMMA REARDON** (27). Analyst at Everys. Her parents are both doctors and she refuses their money. She is sharp, stubborn and funny, and she does not take orders from anyone she doesn't respect.
- **MICHAEL McCLAREN** (37). Famous film director and Donald's best friend. Loud, charming and always joking, and secretly hiding a woman named Sophia.
- **GEOFFREY DUROSE** (37). Heir of Durose Holdings and Donald's best friend. Quiet, a financial genius, and secretly hiding a file about his late father.
- **ROSS** (45). Emma's mentor. Warm, and hiding something terrible.
- **DEBBY** (38). Ross's girlfriend.
- **MARCUS VANE** (52). Old-money tycoon. Smiling and patient, with a grudge older than anyone knows.
- **MAMA IRWIN** (64). Donald's mother. Fragile since the night her husband died.
- **ADAEZE** (30). Donald's PA, the only person who tells him the truth.
- **TOBI** (26). Emma's best friend at work. Speaks Pidgin and has no filter.
- **MALLAM ISA** (60s). Suya seller near the old Ebute-Metta railway yard. He has fed Donald since Donald was a boy.
- **JAMES REARDON** (31). Emma's older brother, a musician the family pushed out.
- **DR. & MRS. REARDON**. Emma's parents, both doctors.
- **YOUNG DONALD, YOUNG MICHAEL, YOUNG GEOFFREY** (12–14). **MR. IRWIN**, Donald's father. **INSPECTOR BELLO**. **NARRATOR**.

## THE THREE SECRETS RUNNING AT THE SAME TIME

| Brother | What he's hiding | What gives him away |
|---|---|---|
| Donald | He's in love with Emma | Two plates of jollof, a woman's shoe, smiling at his phone |
| Michael | He's in love with Sophia, a writer suing his studio | A contact saved as "Script Supervisor", late-night whispering |
| Geoffrey | A hidden file about his late father and a dead man called Elias Hart | Leaving early, lying about "board meetings", a woman called Olivia |

## RECURRING DETAILS TO KEEP CONSISTENT

- **The ₦3,000 share certificate.** "Three Boys Ventures", their first company, is framed in three pieces, one piece each. Whoever falls in love first must hand his piece to the other two on the rooftop and confess.
- **"Still sworn?"** The three ask it every Rooftop Night, and every year they say "Still sworn." This year all three are lying.
- **Mallam Isa's suya.** Donald ate it as a boy, he brings it to Emma, and it saves her life.
- **Page eleven.** Emma's margin note starts everything. It becomes her secret code to Donald.
- **The tracker.** Donald's controlling flaw. Emma finds it, deletes it, and he has to find her without it.

## SCENE 1 — STILL SWORN
**EXT. ROOFTOP, AJEGUNLE — NIGHT — PRESENT DAY**
*(~0:00–1:00)*

*The rusty rooftop of an old face-me-I-face-you building. It is cleaned up now, with fairy lights, three leather chairs and a small table with three glasses. Below, generators hum. Far away, Victoria Island glitters. Three powerful men in expensive clothes sit where they once sat as hungry boys: DONALD IRWIN, MICHAEL McCLAREN and GEOFFREY DUROSE.*

**MICHAEL:** *(raising his glass)* Twenty-three years, gentlemen. Twenty-three years, and not one woman has caught us.

**GEOFFREY:** Not one.

**DONALD:** Not one.

*They clink. Then, by tradition, Michael asks it.*

**MICHAEL:** Still sworn?

**GEOFFREY:** *(a tiny pause)* Still sworn.

**DONALD:** *(without blinking)* Still sworn.

**MICHAEL:** Still sworn!

*They drink. Donald's phone vibrates in his pocket and he doesn't check it. Michael's phone vibrates and he flips it face down. Geoffrey glances at a folded paper in his jacket and buttons the jacket.*

**NARRATOR:** Three best friends. Three promises. And that night, on the rooftop where it all began, three lies. *(beat)* To understand how the most powerful men in Lagos ended up lying to each other, you have to go back to the night they made the promise.

## SCENE 2 — PREAMBLE: THREE BOYS, ONE VOW
**EXT. SAME ROOFTOP, AJEGUNLE — NIGHT — TWENTY-THREE YEARS AGO**
*(~1:00–2:29)*

*Rusty zinc, no lights, no chairs. YOUNG DONALD (14), YOUNG MICHAEL (14) and YOUNG GEOFFREY (14) share one bottle of Fanta. In front of them on a flattened carton is a handwritten "share certificate": THREE BOYS VENTURES, CAPITAL: ₦3,000.*

**YOUNG MICHAEL:** Three thousand naira. We're officially businessmen.

**YOUNG GEOFFREY:** We're officially boys who sold recharge cards for four months.

**YOUNG DONALD:** It's a start. *(He tears the certificate carefully into three pieces and hands one to each of them.)* One for each of us. Nobody can ever say this company belongs to one person.

**YOUNG MICHAEL:** So what are the rules, Chairman?

**YOUNG DONALD:** One. We become successful. Two. We take care of our families. Three. We never come back to this. *(He looks at the zinc and the darkness.)* Never.

**YOUNG MICHAEL:** And four. No girls. Girls make men stupid. My uncle sold his shop for a girl in Ibadan. Now he sells pure water.

**YOUNG GEOFFREY:** *(laughing)* Fine. No girls. And whoever falls in love first?

**YOUNG DONALD:** He comes back to this rooftop, hands his piece of the company to the other two, and confesses. Out loud. Like a fool.

**YOUNG DONALD:** And five. We protect each other. Whatever it costs.

**YOUNG MICHAEL:** Deal! Because it won't be me.

**YOUNG GEOFFREY:** It won't be me.

**YOUNG DONALD:** *(very serious)* It will never be me.

*Hands stacked together.*

**ALL THREE:** Sworn.

*Young Geoffrey looks down at his piece of paper, suddenly quiet.*

**YOUNG GEOFFREY:** My father says we're moving next month. To Ikoyi. He says "something happened" and now we're rich. *(beat)* He won't tell me what happened.

*Michael and Donald exchange a look. Nobody knows what to say.*

## SCENE 3 — PREAMBLE: THE NIGHT THAT MADE DONALD
**INT. ONE-ROOM APARTMENT, AJEGUNLE — NIGHT — TWO YEARS EARLIER**
*(~2:29–3:35)*

*A kerosene lamp. YOUNG DONALD (12) does homework on the floor. His MOTHER, young and beautiful, stirs a pot of stew. MR. IRWIN's empty chair waits at the small table.*

**MOTHER:** Your papa promised meat today. It's payday. The company pays at night, he said.

**YOUNG DONALD:** Papa said when I'm big, I'll have a company that pays in the day. So nobody walks home in the dark.

*A hard knock. Two POLICEMEN stand at the door, hats in their hands. The spoon falls.*

**POLICEMAN:** Madam… your husband. By the bus stop. Robbers. They took everything he was carrying.

*His mother screams and folds to the floor. Young Donald doesn't cry. He stares at the empty chair. The lamp flickers.*

**YOUNG DONALD:** *(whispering)* Nobody will ever take from me again. Never again will I be powerless. Never.

**NARRATOR:** That night, a boy stopped crying. Over the years, he also stopped trusting, stopped resting and stopped letting anything happen that he didn't control. *(beat)* It made him rich. It also made him very, very hard to love.

## SCENE 4 — PREAMBLE: BUILDING THE EMPIRE
**MONTAGE — LAGOS — TWENTY YEARS IN TWO MINUTES**
*(~3:35–4:44)*

*Quick cuts. Fifteen-year-old Donald sells recharge cards in Lagos traffic, sweating. Michael films a wedding with a borrowed camera. Geoffrey counts their money by candlelight in a notebook.*

**YOUNG MICHAEL:** *(holding up a rusty yellow danfo bus key)* We bought a bus! An actual bus!

**YOUNG GEOFFREY:** We bought a bus that doesn't have brakes.

*The bus breaks down on Third Mainland Bridge in the rain. The three young men push it, laughing and crying at the same time.*

**NARRATOR:** They failed together. They recovered together. When one rose, he pulled the others up.

*Twenty-two-year-old Donald signs a lease for a tiny logistics office. Michael's first film premieres in a half-empty cinema, and Donald and Geoffrey sit in the front row clapping like a stadium. Geoffrey, now in a suit, takes over his father's company, Durose Holdings, and looks lonely in a huge office.*

*Then the EVERYS CONGLOMERATE tower rises over Victoria Island. Logos appear one after another: EVERYS PORTS. EVERYS TELECOM. EVERYPAY. EVERYS EKO ATLANTIC.*

**NARRATOR:** By thirty-seven, Donald Irwin owned ports, phone networks, a fintech that moved half of Lagos's money and a skyline in Eko Atlantic. He kept every promise he made on that rooftop. *(beat)* Until a girl with a red pen walked into his life.

## SCENE 5 — THE MAN WHO CONTROLS EVERYTHING
**INT. EVERYS TOWER — BOARDROOM, 40TH FLOOR — DAY**
*(~4:44–5:43)*

*Glass walls over the lagoon. Twelve executives sit in silence. DONALD stands at the head of the table and doesn't sit, because sitting is for people who have time.*

**DONALD:** The Halbrook Shipping acquisition. Where are we?

**EXECUTIVE:** Sir, the numbers are… mostly stable—

**DONALD:** "Mostly" is a word people use when they've stopped counting. *(He swipes his tablet. Every phone in the room buzzes at once.)* I've moved all your meetings to 6 a.m. tomorrow. All of them. We'll count together.

*Groans are swallowed. He walks out. ADAEZE, his PA, matches his pace with practiced speed.*

**ADAEZE:** Sir, your mother called. Twice. And you've scheduled lunch for 12:07 again. Nobody eats lunch at 12:07.

**DONALD:** I do.

**ADAEZE:** Sir, with respect, you schedule your sleep.

**DONALD:** Sleep is the only meeting nobody else attends. *(beat)* Who runs Finance Operations while Ross is on leave?

**ADAEZE:** *(checking)* A Miss Emma Reardon. Three months in the company.

**DONALD:** Three months, running Halbrook's accounts during an acquisition. *(cold)* Send me her report. Tonight.

## SCENE 6 — MAMA
**INT. MAMA IRWIN'S HOUSE, IKEJA GRA — EVENING**
*(~5:43–6:36)*

*A comfortable, quiet home Donald bought for her. MAMA IRWIN (64), gentle and a little fragile, sits on a sofa shelling egusi seeds. Donald sits beside her and takes his shoes off. Here, and only here, he looks like somebody's son.*

**MAMA IRWIN:** You came late again.

**DONALD:** I came, Mama. That's the important part.

**MAMA IRWIN:** Your cousin Ngozi has three children. Three. Even the gateman's son is getting married.

**DONALD:** *(smiling)* Mama, not today.

**MAMA IRWIN:** When then? When I'm in the ground? *(She takes his hand.)* Donald. I watched you build walls since you were twelve. Walls keep out thieves. But they also keep out wives.

**DONALD:** I don't need a wife, Mama. I need control. Love is just… losing control with extra steps.

**MAMA IRWIN:** *(laughing softly)* Your father said the same thing. *(beat)* Before he met me.

*Donald laughs, but something flickers in his eyes. His phone buzzes. It's Emma Reardon's report, arriving.*

## SCENE 7 — THE DAUGHTER WHO SAID NO
**INT. REARDON FAMILY HOME, IKOYI — DINING ROOM — NIGHT**
*(~6:36–7:24)*

*Elegant. Both parents still wear their hospital lanyards. EMMA pushes jollof rice around her plate.*

**DR. REARDON:** Emma, one phone call and you're in medical school by January. I'll pay for everything.

**EMMA:** Daddy, I don't want your money. I have a job.

**MRS. REARDON:** You count other people's money for a man who doesn't know your name.

**EMMA:** *(putting down her fork)* Then one day he'll learn it.

**DR. REARDON:** This is how it started with your brother. A dream, a guitar—

**EMMA:** James. His name is James. Six years, and you still can't say it.

*Silence. Emma kisses her mother's cheek and takes her bag.*

**EMMA:** I'm not running away, Mummy. I'm walking. There's a difference.

*She leaves. Her father looks at the empty chair where his son used to sit.*

## SCENE 8 — THE MENTOR'S GIFT
**INT. EVERYS — FINANCE OPERATIONS FLOOR, 14TH FLOOR — MORNING**
*(~7:24–8:12)*

*ROSS (45), kind and fatherly, packs a box. Emma watches, nervous. TOBI hovers with tea.*

**ROSS:** You're the sharpest person on this floor. You'll be fine.

**EMMA:** Ross, I've been here three months. Halbrook alone is a monster.

**ROSS:** *(handing her a heavy silver fountain pen)* My first boss gave me this. Sign your first big win with it. And remember the only rule I ever taught you.

**EMMA:** *(smiling)* "Numbers don't lie. People do."

*His phone buzzes. The screen shows one letter: "D." He snatches it, and his face goes grey.*

**ROSS:** Debby. Family wahala. *(He lifts the box.)* Emma, if anything about Halbrook ever looks strange… *(He stops himself.)* Never mind. Old man's worries.

*The lift doors close on his sad eyes.*

**TOBI:** That man is hiding something.

**EMMA:** Tobi, everybody is hiding something.

**TOBI:** Yes, but his own is sweating.

## SCENE 9 — THE EMAIL
**INT. EVERYS — EMMA'S DESK — NIGHT**
*(~8:12–8:36)*

*11:47 p.m. Files pile up, red deadline alerts glow and cold coffee sits on the desk. Emma rubs her eyes. Tobi is half asleep on a chair.*

**TOBI:** Emma, go home. Even the generator has gone home.

*The computer pings. NEW EMAIL. FROM: Donald Irwin, CEO.*

**ON SCREEN:** *"Be in my office by 10 a.m."*

*Nothing else.*

**TOBI:** *(suddenly wide awake)* Ehn. Emma. Write your will. Leave me your shoes.

## SCENE 10 — PAGE ELEVEN
**INT. DONALD'S OFFICE, 40TH FLOOR — 9:58 AM**
*(~8:36–10:00)*

*Cold, huge, perfect. Donald reads her report, which is already bleeding red ink. He doesn't look up.*

**DONALD:** Sit.

*She sits. Ten seconds of pages turning.*

**DONALD:** Miss Reardon. Did you write this, or did it happen to you?

**EMMA:** I wrote it, sir.

**DONALD:** Then you should apologise to it. Your forecast is optimistic, your summary is soft, and you missed two deadlines.

**EMMA:** My department lost its manager three weeks ago and nobody replaced him. I'm doing two jobs on one salary.

*He looks up. Their eyes meet for the first time.*

**DONALD:** Is that an excuse?

**EMMA:** It's a fact. You like facts, don't you? I've read your interviews.

**DONALD:** *(dangerously quiet)* Careful.

**EMMA:** With respect, sir, you've torn me apart without asking one question about the numbers. If you had, you'd know page eleven is the most important page in that report. And you skipped it.

*Nobody has spoken to him like this in ten years. Slowly, he turns to page eleven.*

**DONALD:** Get out, Miss Reardon.

*She stands with her chin high and walks out. The door clicks shut. Donald reads page eleven. In the margin, in small, neat handwriting:*

**ON PAPER:** *"Halbrook — 0.3% missing every month. Same day. Same amount. Who?"*

*He reads it three times. Then, for the first time in years, Donald Irwin smiles at something that isn't a profit.*

**NARRATOR:** He had read ten thousand reports in his life. This was the first one he would remember.

## SCENE 11 — ASKING FOR A FRIEND
**INT. DONALD'S CAR, THIRD MAINLAND BRIDGE / INT. MICHAEL'S FILM SET — NIGHT — INTERCUT**
*(~10:00–10:57)*

*Donald sits in the back of his car, staring at page eleven again. He calls Michael. Michael answers on a busy film set, with lights and crew everywhere.*

**MICHAEL:** Chairman! You never call after 9 p.m. Who died?

**DONALD:** Nobody died. Question. Theoretical.

**MICHAEL:** I love theoretical.

**DONALD:** If a man can't stop thinking about… a report.

**MICHAEL:** A report.

**DONALD:** A report. Written by a… person. And the man reads it three times. Not for the numbers.

*A long pause on Michael's end.*

**MICHAEL:** Donald. Is this "report" wearing heels?

**DONALD:** *(instantly)* Goodnight, Michael.

**MICHAEL:** Donald! DONALD! Are you breaking the—

*Donald hangs up. On set, Michael laughs and turns around. A WOMAN'S VOICE calls from behind a trailer: "Michael, are you coming or not?" His smile disappears. He looks around to check that nobody heard.*

**MICHAEL:** *(hissing toward the trailer)* Sophia! I said wait in the car!

**NARRATOR:** Donald thought he was the only one with something to hide. *(beat)* He wasn't.

## SCENE 12 — QUIETLY
**INT. EVERYS — FINANCE FLOOR — 8:30 PM**
*(~10:57–11:42)*

*The floor is empty. Emma packs up. Expensive footsteps approach. Donald stands there with her report.*

**EMMA:** If you've come to fire me, you could have sent another one-line email.

**DONALD:** Page eleven. Explain it.

**EMMA:** Every month, Halbrook loses point three percent. Too small for an audit. Fourteen months running. Same day, same amount. Numbers don't lie.

**DONALD:** *(quietly)* People do.

*She blinks. He finished Ross's sentence.*

**DONALD:** Investigate it. Quietly. You report to nobody but me. Not your colleagues. Not your manager. Nobody.

**EMMA:** Why me?

**DONALD:** Because you looked at that number and didn't look away. *(turning to go)* And your report was still bad.

**EMMA:** Your email was worse!

*He keeps walking and doesn't let her see him smile.*

## SCENE 13 — MALLAM ISA'S SUYA
**INT. EVERYS — SMALL CONFERENCE ROOM — MIDNIGHT — ONE WEEK LATER**
*(~11:42–12:53)*

*Papers everywhere. Emma is barefoot and drawing arrows on a whiteboard. Donald enters with a parcel wrapped in old newspaper, and the smell of suya fills the room.*

**EMMA:** You brought suya? Billionaires eat suya?

**DONALD:** Not this suya. This is Mallam Isa's. Ebute-Metta, by the old railway yard. Best in Lagos. *(He sits and unwraps it.)* When I was fifteen, he gave me suya on credit for a whole year. Never wrote it down.

**EMMA:** Did you pay him back?

**DONALD:** I bought him a house. He still sells suya under the same umbrella. Says the trains are good for business. *(He holds out a stick.)* Eat.

*They eat. For a moment, they are just two people at midnight.*

**EMMA:** The money goes to a vendor called Crestline Logistics. No trucks. No staff. No office. And the payments are approved inside Everys by someone with senior access.

**DONALD:** *(stops chewing)* How senior?

**EMMA:** Senior enough that if I say a name before I'm sure, I'm finished.

**DONALD:** You have pepper on your lip.

*She wipes it, embarrassed. He laughs, a real laugh, rusty from lack of use.*

**EMMA:** You laugh?

**DONALD:** Tell nobody. I'll deny it under oath.

## SCENE 14 — THE MAN IN THE WHITE SUIT
**INT. MARCUS VANE'S MANSION, BANANA ISLAND — STUDY — NIGHT**
*(~12:53–13:33)*

*Dark wood, a chessboard and an old framed photo of a trucking company sign: VANE HAULAGE, EST. 1979. MARCUS VANE (52), silver-haired and in a silk robe, makes a call on a burner phone.*

**MARCUS:** She found Crestline.

**DISTORTED VOICE:** I'll send her a warning. She'll stop.

**MARCUS:** Warnings are for children. *(He looks at the old photo.)* Twenty years, I've waited. Halbrook bleeds, Everys's share price falls, and when it's cheap enough, I buy the boy who bought my father. *(beat)* Don't fail me. Remember who holds Debby's debts.

*He hangs up and moves a white knight across the board.*

**MARCUS:** Donald Irwin has no weakness. No wife. No children. No heart. *(He smiles.)* Yet.

## SCENE 15 — ROOFTOP NIGHT
**EXT. AJEGUNLE ROOFTOP — NIGHT — (THE COLD OPEN, CONTINUED)**
*(~13:33–14:34)*

*We are back in Scene 1, right after "Still sworn." The glasses go down.*

**MICHAEL:** *(leaning in)* So, Donald. Who's the report?

**DONALD:** There's no report.

**MICHAEL:** You called me at 9 p.m. to ask about a report "not for the numbers."

**DONALD:** It was a work question.

**MICHAEL:** Geoffrey, he's blushing. Look at his ears.

*Donald's phone buzzes. He glances at it. "E. Reardon: Found something. Call me." He puts it away too fast.*

**DONALD:** *(changing the subject, aggressive)* What about you, Michael? Who's "Script Supervisor"? She calls you eleven times a day.

*Michael freezes.*

**MICHAEL:** She… supervises scripts.

**DONALD:** At 2 a.m.?

**MICHAEL:** Scripts don't sleep, Donald!

*They both turn to Geoffrey, who is already standing and buttoning his jacket.*

**GEOFFREY:** I have an early board meeting.

**MICHAEL:** It's Saturday.

**GEOFFREY:** *(already leaving)* It's an early Saturday board meeting.

*He's gone. Michael and Donald stare at each other, each knowing the other is lying and neither able to say it.*

**NARRATOR:** That's the problem with three liars on one rooftop. Nobody can point a finger without three pointing back.

## SCENE 16 — THE OTHER SECRET
**INT. DUROSE HOLDINGS — JACOB DUROSE'S OLD OFFICE — SAME NIGHT**
*(~14:34–15:10)*

**MEANWHILE:** *Same night. Across the lagoon.*

*Dust sheets cover the furniture. Geoffrey enters alone with a torch. He unlocks a drawer in his late father's desk with a small brass key. Inside is one old photograph. Two young men stand in front of a tiny office: JACOB DUROSE and another man. On the back, in faded ink: "Jacob & Elias. Partners forever. 1998."*

**GEOFFREY:** *(whispering)* Partners? Papa, you told me you built it alone.

*His phone lights up with an unknown number. A woman's voice comes through when he answers.*

**OLIVIA (V.O.):** You found it, didn't you, Mr. Durose? Meet me tomorrow. Alone.

*Click. Geoffrey stares at the photograph. Every brother has a secret now.*

**INT. EMMA'S CAR — EVERYS CAR PARK — NIGHT**

**ON SCREEN:** *"STOP LOOKING INTO HALBROOK OR ELSE."*

**EMMA:** *(whispering)* Who are you?

## SCENE 18 — "YOU'RE MY RESPONSIBILITY"
**INT. DONALD'S OFFICE — NEXT MORNING**
*(~15:29–17:08)*

*Emma walks in without knocking. Adaeze fails to stop her. Emma puts the phone on his desk. As Donald reads it, a second message arrives with PHOTOGRAPHS: Emma at her door, Emma at the supermarket, Emma asleep in traffic.*

**ON SCREEN:** *"WE SEE YOU, EMMA."*

*Donald's knuckles go white.*

**DONALD:** *(intercom)* Adaeze. Two guards on Miss Reardon. Around the clock. A driver. Armoured car. And move her into one of our serviced apartments in Eko Atlantic, tonight.

**EMMA:** Excuse me? No. Absolutely not.

**DONALD:** It's not a request.

**EMMA:** I didn't refuse my father's money so another man could put me in a cage!

**DONALD:** It's not a cage. It's a—

**EMMA:** A cage with a nicer view is still a cage, Donald!

*It's the first time she has used his first name. They both hear it.*

**DONALD:** *(quieter)* Someone stood outside your door, Emma.

**EMMA:** Then let me decide how scared to be. One guard. My car. My apartment.

**DONALD:** Two guards.

**EMMA:** One.

**DONALD:** …Two, and I'll stop talking about the apartment.

**EMMA:** You negotiate like a criminal.

**DONALD:** I learned from them.

*She leaves. Donald stares at the door, then presses the intercom again, quietly.*

**DONALD:** Adaeze. Send someone from IT. Install location tracking on her company phone. Silently.

**ADAEZE:** *(after a pause)* Sir… she didn't agree to that.

**DONALD:** She'll thank me.

**ADAEZE:** *(softly)* Sir, nobody has ever thanked anybody for that.

*He doesn't answer. Adaeze sighs and makes the call.*

**NARRATOR:** It was the kind of decision Donald had made a thousand times: quiet, fast and for her own good. He didn't know yet that it would cost him the one thing he couldn't buy back.

## SCENE 19 — THE MIRROR
**INT. DONALD'S MANSION, LEKKI — MASTER BATHROOM — NIGHT**
*(~17:08–17:57)*

*Donald stands at the mirror in a white vest and practises, like a man rehearsing a lie for court.*

**DONALD:** I am not in love. *(beat)* She is an employee. *(beat)* A very irritating employee. *(beat)* With very nice handwriting.

*He groans and leans on the sink.*

*He walks to his bedside, where an old faded photo of MR. IRWIN, smiling in a driver's uniform, stands in a cheap frame among the luxury.*

**DONALD:** Papa. You died because you trusted the night. I built a whole life so I'd never trust anything I can't control. *(beat)* And now there's a woman I can't control, and I can't stop thinking about her. *(beat)* What do I do?

*The photo, of course, says nothing. Donald switches off the light. In the dark, his phone lights up: "E. Reardon: Goodnight, Mr. Criminal." He smiles at the ceiling like a teenager.*

## SCENE 20 — TWO PLATES OF JOLLOF
**INT. DONALD'S MANSION — KITCHEN — NIGHT**
*(~17:57–19:12)*

*Emma and Donald have papers spread over the kitchen island, with two plates of jollof and two glasses of zobo. She's laughing at something he said. The intercom buzzes. SECURITY (V.O.): "Sir, Mr. McClaren is at the gate. He says he's already inside."*

*The front door bangs open.*

**MICHAEL (O.S.):** CHAIRMAN! I'm hungry and my chef has abandoned me!

**DONALD:** *(panicking for the first time in his life)* Pantry. Now.

**EMMA:** I'm sorry, the WHAT?

*He pushes her gently into the walk-in pantry and shuts the door just as Michael strolls in.*

**MICHAEL:** Ah! Jollof! *(He sees two plates.)* Two plates?

**DONALD:** I'm hungry twice.

**MICHAEL:** *(narrowing his eyes, picking up a fork)* Since when do you eat dinner? You schedule dinner. *(He spots a woman's heel lying under the island. He picks it up slowly.)* And since when do you wear a size thirty-eight heel?

**DONALD:** That's Adaeze's.

**MICHAEL:** Adaeze wears flat shoes. She told me she'd rather die than run after you in heels.

*Michael's phone rings. He glances at the screen, which says "Script Supervisor", and declines it fast. It rings again. He answers in a hiss, turning away.*

**MICHAEL:** *(whispering)* Sophia, I said I'll call you back! …No, I'm not hiding you, I'm just… I'm at Donald's! …Yes, I'll bring the script!

*He hangs up and turns around. Donald is standing right behind him with his arms folded.*

**DONALD:** Sophia.

*Michael lifts the size thirty-eight heel.*

**MICHAEL:** Adaeze.

*A long, silent standoff. Two men, two secrets, no way out. Then, together:*

**MICHAEL & DONALD:** Goodnight.

*Michael leaves, taking the plate of jollof with him. Donald opens the pantry. Emma is sitting on a bag of rice, crying with laughter.*

## SCENE 21 — THE PANTRY
