# How Hospitals Treat You at Every Level of Wealth: Video Prompts (20 key beats)

All clips are 5 seconds and image-to-video. Each one uses the matching beat image from image-prompts.md as its start frame.

Style, used in every prompt: flat 2D cartoon webcomic animation, bold clean black outlines, flat cel shading, desaturated slate-blue and gray palette with muted warm accents, limited subtle motion, no morphing, characters stay on-model, 16:9.

Protagonist: a young man with an oversized round head, short dark-brown side-parted hair, thin rectangular glasses, large half-lidded unimpressed eyes and a small flat mouth, always deadpan.

1. Beat 1. "Level one, zero dollars. No insurance. No savings."
   The protagonist, in a charcoal hoodie, stands in a bare studio apartment holding an open, empty wallet. Slow push-in from medium to medium-close. He blinks once, slowly, and lowers the wallet. Dust drifts in the cool fluorescent light, which flickers faintly. Mood: bleak and quiet.

2. Beat 8. "under a light that flickers every few seconds."
   A low-angle shot looks up at a buzzing fluorescent tube above an emergency waiting room. The light stutters off and on twice. Below it, the protagonist's tired face, in a charcoal hoodie, dims and brightens with each flicker. Static camera with a very slight drift. Mood: oppressive and draining.

3. Beat 11. "That small pause on the keyboard tells you everything. You've just been sorted."
   Extreme close-up of a tired nurse's fingers in light-blue scrubs typing on an old keyboard. The fingers stop and hover, frozen. Behind them, a monitor dropdown slides open and highlights "SELF-PAY". Slow rack focus from the fingers to the screen. Mood: quietly devastating.

4. Beat 15. "Then someone who arrived after you, holding a card in a leather wallet."
   Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the protagonist, in a charcoal hoodie, seated in a plastic chair. A well-dressed man in a camel overcoat walks past him, flips open a leather wallet and shows a card. A smiling nurse waves the man through the triage doors, which swing shut. The protagonist's head turns slowly to follow. Mood: unfair and stinging.

5. Beat 26. "Three weeks later, the envelope arrives. $38,000. Itemized. $19 for a single pill."
   Close-up over the protagonist's shoulder. He wears a charcoal hoodie and sits at a dim apartment table, unfolding a hospital bill. The paper opens to reveal "TOTAL DUE: $38,000". The camera tilts down the itemized lines and stops on "Acetaminophen 1 tab - $19.00". His thumb tightens on the paper. Mood: shocking and absurd.

6. Beat 29. "$400 a month for eight years."
   Medium shot of the protagonist, in a charcoal hoodie, in a small dim room. Bill envelopes drop from above one by one and stack into a tower, rising quickly until it looms over him. He looks up slowly, deadpan. Slow camera pull-back to show the scale. Mood: crushing.

7. Beat 71. "The people in the waiting room all look tired in the same way."
   A slow sideways tracking shot along a row of plastic chairs in a cool-lit clinic waiting room. Patients of different ages share the same heavy-lidded, exhausted expression, and each one blinks slowly as the camera passes. The protagonist, in a charcoal hoodie, sits in the middle of the row. Mood: collective weariness.

8. Beat 96. "you recognize the pause on the keyboard. You can't fix it."
   An intake window with scratched plexiglass. In the foreground, a nurse's fingers freeze above the keyboard. Behind the glass, the protagonist, in a charcoal crewneck sweater, stands beside his small gray-haired mother in a faded cardigan. His jaw tightens and his eyes drop. Slow rack focus from the fingers to his face. Mood: painful recognition.

9. Beat 120. "and a coffee machine that grinds the beans."
   Close-up of a polished espresso machine in a calm boutique clinic lobby. Beans grind, then coffee streams into a ceramic cup and steam curls up. The protagonist's hand, in a charcoal quarter-zip sleeve, reaches in and lifts the cup. Soft warm daylight. Gentle push-in. Mood: indulgent and quiet.

10. Beat 153. "You just know your name moved to the top of a list"
    Close-up of a monitor showing a long waiting list of grayed-out patient names. One name lifts out of the middle, glows gold and slides smoothly up past hundreds of others to the top row. Slow tilt up following it. Warm amber glow. Mood: privileged and unsettling.

11. Beat 163. "Within an hour, a doctor who speaks your language knocks on the door."
    A warm-lit hotel corridor abroad at night. A friendly local doctor with a leather medical bag walks into frame, stops at a room door and knocks twice. A wall clock reads 12:50. Static camera at eye level. Mood: relief and rescue.

12. Beat 170. "Level six, $10 million. The hospital has another floor."
    Close-up of a hospital elevator panel. The protagonist's finger, in a dark tailored jacket sleeve, presses a hidden unlabeled button above the top floor. The button lights up gold and the panel's reflection shimmers. Slight push-in. Warm golden light. Mood: secret and exclusive.

13. Beat 195. "On your way out, the elevator stops at the wrong floor."
    Inside a private wood-paneled elevator with warm light. The protagonist, in a dark tailored jacket, stands calmly. The floor indicator ticks down and stops at "ER - 1" with a soft chime. His eyes flick up toward it. Static camera. Mood: unexpected.

14. Beat 198. "Nobody upstairs ever mentions that floor. It's directly beneath your suite."
    A cutaway cross-section of a hospital building. The camera slowly tilts down from a warm, golden private suite, where the protagonist in a dark tailored jacket stands at the window, to the crowded emergency room directly below, with blue plastic chairs under a flickering fluorescent light. Mood: stark and uncomfortable.

15. Beat 207. "Your name is carved in stone above the entrance"
    A low-angle shot of a grand granite hospital facade in golden afternoon light. A slow upward tilt reveals large carved letters above glass entry doors. Sunlight glints across the engraving and soft lens dust drifts. Mood: monumental.

16. Beat 221. "The room nods. You remember a number. $38,000. You raise your hand."
    A windowless boardroom under pendant lights. Executives in suits nod in unison around a long table. In the foreground, the protagonist, in a dark tailored jacket, slowly raises his hand. A faint ghost of an old "$38,000" bill flickers in the reflection of his glasses. Slow push-in on him. Mood: quiet defiance.

17. Beat 240. "you feel exactly what you felt in that plastic chair."
    A double exposure. The protagonist, in a dark tailored jacket, sits hunched on the edge of a luxurious bed in a dark bedroom lit by blue moonlight. His younger self, in a charcoal hoodie, slowly fades in over him in the same pose on a blue plastic emergency-room chair under a flickering light, until the two overlap. Mood: haunting and full-circle.

18. Beat 247. "Somewhere right now, someone is sitting in a plastic chair"
    A crowded emergency waiting room under a cool, flickering fluorescent light. A tired young woman with curly black hair in a faded maroon hoodie sits alone in a blue plastic chair, arms wrapped around her stomach. She shifts slightly and winces. Slow push-in from a wide shot. Mood: quiet and cyclical.

19. Beat 262. "Same building. Same doctors. Same body, breaking the same way."
    A full cross-section of a hospital building at night. The luxury suite at the top glows warm gold and the crowded emergency room at the bottom glows cold blue. The same doctor figure walks through a room on both floors at the same time. Slow vertical pan from the top floor to the bottom. Mood: haunting symmetry.

20. Beat 264. "It decides what your sickness is worth."
    Close-up of a hospital wristband lying on a blue plastic waiting-room chair, with a small paper price tag attached. The fluorescent light above flickers one last time, then the frame slowly fades to black. Static camera. Mood: cold and final.
