# THE SWORN BACHELORS — PART TWO: MICHAEL × SOPHIA
## "The Ruthless Director Who Never Forgave Betrayal Fell for the Woman Who Betrayed Him"

*A Nigerian romance drama in 30 scenes. About 25 minutes. All dialogue, no narrator. The only text that isn't spoken is short visual notes for the AI scenes.*

---

## HOW THIS PART CONNECTS TO PART ONE

Part Two happens **at the same time** as Part One. While Donald is hiding Emma, Michael is hiding Sophia, and the scenes that overlap are shown again from Michael's side.

| Part One moment | What Michael was really doing |
|---|---|
| Scene 13: Donald calls Michael "asking for a friend" | Sophia was waiting behind Michael's trailer |
| Scene 17: Rooftop Night, "Who's Script Supervisor?" | Sophia had just told Michael she loved him |
| Scene 22: the jollof, the shoe, "Sophia." / "Adaeze." | Michael races home to tell Sophia that Donald is hiding a woman |
| Scene 36: "Not now, Michael." | He was about to confess. Sophia was calling about their last-chance date |
| Scene 45: "You promised you'd tell them tonight." | He breaks Sophia's heart one more time, then fixes everything |

## CAST

- **MICHAEL McCLAREN** (37). Nigeria's most powerful film director and owner of McClaren Studios. Charming in public and ruthless on set. Since he joined his studio to his brothers' money, he has had more to lose and less mercy. His rule: *"Betray me once and you're dead to me."*
- **SOPHIA BENNETT** (31). Screenwriter from Badagry, a fisherman's daughter. She is proud and fearless, and allergic to his ambition. She wrote *The Last Horizon* about her father, who went to sea and never came back.
- **DONALD IRWIN** (37). Michael's brother from Part One. Controlling, suspicious, and secretly in love with Emma.
- **GEOFFREY DUROSE** (37). Michael's brother. Quiet and watchful, and hiding a file about his father.
- **KOLA BADMUS** (40). Michael's executive producer and right-hand man for fifteen years, since the days of filming weddings in Enugu.
- **MAMA BENNETT** (60). Sophia's mother, a fisherman's widow.
- **DAPO** (28). Nigeria's most popular actor.
- **BISI**. Assistant director.
- **MR. HARGREAVES**. Head of a foreign streaming platform, seen on a video call.
- **VICTOR ADEKUNLE**. CEO of Apex Studios, Michael's biggest rival. Heard only on the phone.
- **WAITER**.

## RECURRING DETAILS TO KEEP CONSISTENT

- **"CUT!"** Michael's power word. He shouts it at everyone, and he can never say it to Sophia.
- **The Last Horizon.** Sophia's father never came back from the sea. Michael rewrote the ending so the father comes home rich. The film can only be honest when he restores the true ending.
- **"Script Supervisor".** The name Sophia is saved under in Michael's phone, so his brothers won't see her real name.
- **The empty chair.** Sophia waits for Michael at a restaurant twice. The second time, she leaves the chair empty for good.
- **"Betray me once and you're dead to me."** Michael has never forgiven anyone, until her.

## SCENE 1 — "YES. I DID IT."
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — NIGHT — RAIN ON THE WINDOWS**
*(~0:00–0:35)*

*MICHAEL McCLAREN stands with a printed email shaking in his hand. Every blog in Lagos is on his wall of screens: "McCLAREN STOLE A DEAD FISHERMAN'S STORY." SOPHIA BENNETT stands across the desk, soaked and not backing down.*

**MICHAEL:** These emails came from MY laptop. At 1 a.m. In MY office. *(quietly)* Only one person was here at 1 a.m., Sophia.

**SOPHIA:** Yes. I did it.

**MICHAEL:** *(as if she slapped him)* Do you know what I do to people who betray me?

**SOPHIA:** You ruin them. Everybody in Lagos knows.

**MICHAEL:** Then why are you still standing in my office?

**SOPHIA:** *(stepping closer)* Because you haven't ruined me yet, Michael. *(beat)* Why not?

*Thunder. Michael opens his mouth, but nothing comes out.*

**MICHAEL:** *(barely a whisper)* …I don't know.

**TITLE CARD:** *FOUR MONTHS EARLIER*

## SCENE 2 — "CUT."
**EXT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — FILM SET OF "THE LAST HORIZON" — BADAGRY BEACH — DAY**
*(~0:35–1:30)*

*A huge beach set with cameras, a crane, a hundred crew and an old wooden fishing boat. Michael sits in his director's chair in dark glasses. DAPO, the superstar actor, strolls in with his entourage twenty minutes late.*

**DAPO:** Sorry, Oga Michael. Traffic.

**MICHAEL:** *(not looking up)* Bisi. What time did we call Dapo?

**BISI:** Six a.m., sir.

**MICHAEL:** And what time is it?

**BISI:** Six twenty-one, sir.

**MICHAEL:** *(standing, removing the glasses)* A hundred people were standing in the sun for twenty-one minutes. A hundred people who also have traffic. *(to Dapo)* You're fired.

**DAPO:** *(laughing)* You can't fire me. I'm Dapo.

**MICHAEL:** And tomorrow, you'll be the actor who used to be Dapo. Security.

*Gasps. KOLA BADMUS, Michael's producer, hurries over.*

**KOLA:** *(whispering)* Michael, he has eleven million followers. The investors—

**MICHAEL:** The investors are my brothers, Kola. I'll handle my brothers. *(loud, to the crew)* Recast by Monday! *(He sits.)* And Kola, the screenplay. Did the writer sign the changes?

**KOLA:** She refused. She's in your office right now.

**MICHAEL:** Then she can wait.

**KOLA:** Sir… she's been waiting since five.

## SCENE 3 — THREE BROTHERS, NO TRUST
**INT. THREE BOYS VENTURES — PRIVATE BOARDROOM, IKOYI — DAY**
*(~1:30–2:31)*

*A long table, three chairs, three closed laptops. DONALD sits at the head as always, with GEOFFREY on the right. Michael walks in late and sits with his feet on the table.*

**DONALD:** The Last Horizon. Your budget has tripled, Michael. Six billion naira. I want the breakdown.

**MICHAEL:** No.

**DONALD:** We merged so we could stop hiding things from each other.

**MICHAEL:** Then you go first. Geoffrey moved money last month. Who did he move it to?

*Geoffrey adjusts his glasses and says nothing.*

**DONALD:** Geoffrey?

**GEOFFREY:** *(calm)* It's handled.

**MICHAEL:** *(throwing up his hands)* You see? Everybody has a secret except me, and I'm the one being audited!

**DONALD:** You fired Dapo this morning. It's on every blog. That's eleven million followers who won't buy a ticket.

**MICHAEL:** I've built my career on firing people who waste my time, Donald. That's what you two paid for.

**GEOFFREY:** *(quietly)* We paid for a brother. Lately we're getting a stranger.

*Silence. Michael stands and buttons his jacket.*

**MICHAEL:** I have a writer to deal with.

**DONALD:** "Deal with"?

**MICHAEL:** She's suing my studio. I'll crush her by lunch.

## SCENE 4 — THE WOMAN WHO WASN'T IMPRESSED
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — DAY**
*(~2:31–3:50)*

*Posters of his blockbuster films line the walls, with awards on shelves. Sophia sits in HIS chair behind HIS desk, reading her own screenplay, which has his red pen all over it. Michael walks in and stops.*

**MICHAEL:** You're in my chair.

**SOPHIA:** You're in my story.

*He blinks. Nobody talks to him like this.*

**MICHAEL:** Miss Bennett, I'm a busy man—

**SOPHIA:** I know. I've been watching you be busy for six hours. *(She holds up the script.)* Page ninety-two. In my story, the fisherman goes to sea and never comes back. His wife stands on the beach every evening until the sun disappears. That's the last horizon. *(beat)* In YOUR version, he comes back with a boat full of gold and buys a mansion in Lekki.

**MICHAEL:** People don't pay to cry, Miss Bennett. They pay to hope.

**SOPHIA:** My father didn't come back with gold. He didn't come back at all. *(She stands.)* You can't make his death a happy ending because it sells better.

**MICHAEL:** *(smiling, smooth)* Name your price. Fifty million. A hundred. You'll never write another word in your life.

**SOPHIA:** *(walking right up to him)* That's the problem with men like you. You think everything has a price because everything you love does.

*She drops the script on his chest and walks to the door.*

**SOPHIA:** My lawyer will call your lawyer. Have a nice day, Mr. Director.

*The door closes. Michael stands frozen, still holding the script against his chest. Kola peeks in.*

**KOLA:** Should I call legal?

**MICHAEL:** *(still staring at the door)* No.

**KOLA:** Sir?

**MICHAEL:** Call her back. *(beat)* Hire her.

## SCENE 5 — THE FISHERMAN'S DAUGHTER
**INT. MAMA BENNETT'S SMALL HOUSE, BADAGRY — EVENING**
*(~3:50–4:36)*

*A tiny, neat sitting room. An old photo of a smiling fisherman hangs on the wall. MAMA BENNETT fries fish. Sophia waves a letter.*

**SOPHIA:** He wants to hire me, Mama! Co-writer on set. Every day. With HIM.

**MAMA BENNETT:** The man who stole your story?

**SOPHIA:** The most arrogant man in Nigeria. He fired an actor for being twenty minutes late.

**MAMA BENNETT:** *(turning the fish)* Your father was never late for the sea. Not one day. *(beat)* He was only late for us.

*Sophia goes quiet. She looks at the photo.*

**SOPHIA:** That's exactly why I can't stand him. He's married to his work, like Papa was married to the sea.

**MAMA BENNETT:** Then go and take your story back from him. *(beat)* And don't fall into his sea, my daughter.

**SOPHIA:** *(laughing)* Mama. Please. Never.

## SCENE 6 — HE FORGOT TO SAY "ACTION"
**EXT. THE LAST HORIZON SET — BADAGRY BEACH — DAY**
*(~4:36–5:04)*

*The cameras are rolling and the crew is ready. Michael sits in his chair, and Sophia sits on a crate beside him with a notebook. The new lead actor waits in the boat.*

**BISI:** Sir, we're ready.

*Michael is watching Sophia, who is mouthing the lines along with the actor and wiping a tear.*

**BISI:** Sir? …Sir! Everyone is waiting for "Action."

**MICHAEL:** *(snapping back)* Cut!

**BISI:** Sir, we haven't started.

**MICHAEL:** Then… action! Action. Yes. Action.

*Sophia raises an eyebrow. Kola, behind them, notices everything and keeps his face blank.*

**SOPHIA:** *(whispering)* The great Michael McClaren forgot how to start a scene?

**MICHAEL:** *(whispering back)* The great Michael McClaren was thinking about the ending.

**SOPHIA:** My ending?

**MICHAEL:** *(after a beat)* …I haven't decided whose ending yet.

## SCENE 7 — THE MAN WHO NEVER FORGIVES
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — EDITING SUITE — 2 AM**
*(~5:04–6:09)*

*Only the screens light the dark room. Michael and Sophia sit close, watching rough footage of the fishing boat sailing into the sunset. Takeaway jollof containers are on the desk.*

**SOPHIA:** Why are you like this? Everyone on that set is afraid of you.

**MICHAEL:** Good. Afraid people don't steal.

**SOPHIA:** Who stole from you?

*A long silence. He pauses the footage.*

**MICHAEL:** My first film. Enugu. I was twenty-two. My producer, Chuka, my best friend in that city, took the master tape to Lagos and released it under his own name. *(beat)* Then my fiancée left me for a man with a yacht.

**SOPHIA:** And?

**MICHAEL:** And I spent five years buying every company Chuka worked for and firing him from each one. *(He smiles, but there's no warmth in it.)* He sells phones in Alaba now. *(beat)* I never forgive, Sophia. Not once. Not anybody.

**SOPHIA:** *(softly)* That's not strength, Michael. That's a scar you're proud of.

*He turns to look at her. They're very close. Neither moves away.*

**MICHAEL:** You're the only person who talks to me like that.

**SOPHIA:** You're the only person who needs it this badly.

*He leans in. She leans in. The door bangs open.*

**KOLA:** Sir, the investors moved the call to— *(He stops.)* …Sorry. Wrong time.

*Kola backs out. His face is blank, but he looks at Sophia a second too long.*

## SCENE 8 — "ASKING FOR A FRIEND"
**INT. FILM SET — BEHIND A TRAILER — NIGHT**
*(~6:09–7:04)*

*Michael is on the phone, grinning. Sophia waits beside the trailer, holding his jacket. (This is Part One, Scene 13, seen from Michael's side.)*

**DONALD (ON PHONE):** If a man can't stop thinking about… a report. Written by a person.

**MICHAEL:** Donald. Is this "report" wearing heels?

**DONALD (ON PHONE):** Goodnight, Michael.

**MICHAEL:** *(laughing into the dead line)* Donald! DONALD! Are you breaking the—

**SOPHIA:** Michael, are you coming or not?

*Michael spins around in panic and hisses at her.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia! I said wait in the car!

**SOPHIA:** *(arms folded)* Why? Who was that?

**MICHAEL:** My brother.

**SOPHIA:** And your brother can't know I exist?

**MICHAEL:** *(lowering his voice)* Sophia. Seven years ago, on a rooftop, my brothers and I swore that none of us would ever fall in love. Whoever breaks it first has to confess like a fool and give up his share of our first company.

**SOPHIA:** *(slowly)* So I'm a secret.

**MICHAEL:** You're not a secret. You're… a strategy.

**SOPHIA:** *(handing him his jacket hard)* Then strategise your own way home.

## SCENE 9 — THE FIRST EMPTY CHAIR
**INT. CANDLELIT RESTAURANT, VICTORIA ISLAND — NIGHT**
*(~7:04–7:20)*

*A beautiful table by the window. Sophia sits in a red dress, her phone face up beside a small cake with "Happy Birthday, Sophia" written on it. The chair opposite is empty. 8:00. 8:40. 9:30. A WAITER hovers.*

**WAITER:** Madam, should we… still wait?

**SOPHIA:** *(forcing a smile)* He's a director. He's always late. Just one more glass of water.

## SCENE 10 — THE DEAL
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — CONFERENCE ROOM — SAME NIGHT — INTERCUT**
*(~7:20–7:46)*

*Michael is on a giant video call with MR. HARGREAVES of a foreign streaming platform. His phone buzzes over and over on the table with "Script Supervisor." He turns it face down.*

**MR. HARGREAVES (ON SCREEN):** Michael, we're prepared to take The Last Horizon global. Two hundred countries. But we need the happy ending. Families don't subscribe to grief.

**MICHAEL:** *(not even hesitating)* The happy ending stays. You have my word.

**MR. HARGREAVES (ON SCREEN):** And the writer? I hear she's difficult.

**MICHAEL:** *(after a tiny pause)* The writer is handled.

*The phone buzzes again. He doesn't turn it over. Kola, sitting beside him, sees the screen and says nothing.*

## SCENE 11 — "IT WAS MY BIRTHDAY"
**EXT. RESTAURANT — VICTORIA ISLAND — 11:15 PM**
*(~7:46–8:43)*

*Sophia walks out holding the uneaten cake. Michael's car screeches to the kerb. He jumps out with his tie loose, glowing with excitement.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia! I'm sorry, I'm sorry! But listen. Two hundred countries! The Last Horizon is going GLOBAL! Do you know what this means?

**SOPHIA:** *(very calm)* Do you know what today means?

**MICHAEL:** It means everything we— *(He sees the cake.)* "Happy Birthday, Sophia." *(The excitement drains out of him.)* …Oh, God.

**SOPHIA:** Three hours, Michael. And when you finally came, the first thing out of your mouth was a deal.

**MICHAEL:** Sophia, I'll make it up to you. Tomorrow, I'll fly you anywhere. Dubai. Paris—

**SOPHIA:** I don't want Paris! I want a man who remembers I exist when there's no camera on me!

*She pushes the cake into his hands.*

**SOPHIA:** My father missed every birthday I ever had because the sea was always more important. I swore I'd never love a man like that. *(Her voice breaks.)* And look at me.

*She gets into a taxi. Michael stands on the pavement holding a birthday cake, the most powerful director in Nigeria and completely powerless.*

## SCENE 12 — "STILL SWORN"
**EXT. AJEGUNLE ROOFTOP — ROOFTOP NIGHT — (PART ONE, SCENE 17)**
*(~8:43–9:26)*

*Fairy lights and three leather chairs. Michael's phone keeps lighting up in his pocket with "Script Supervisor." He flips it face down.*

**MICHAEL:** Still sworn?

**GEOFFREY:** *(a tiny pause)* Still sworn.

**DONALD:** Still sworn.

**MICHAEL:** *(too loud)* Still sworn!

**DONALD:** *(leaning in, aggressive)* Who's "Script Supervisor", Michael? She calls you eleven times a day.

**MICHAEL:** She… supervises scripts.

**DONALD:** At 2 a.m.?

**MICHAEL:** Scripts don't sleep, Donald! And who's YOUR "report"? You blush when your phone buzzes!

**DONALD:** That's business.

**MICHAEL:** Business! Everything is "business" with you two! Geoffrey moves billions and says "it's handled"! You hide your phone like a teenager! And I'm the one on trial?

**GEOFFREY:** *(standing, buttoning his jacket)* I have an early board meeting.

**MICHAEL:** It's SATURDAY!

*Geoffrey is gone. Donald and Michael stare at each other, two liars with nowhere left to go.*

**DONALD:** *(quietly)* Rule four, Michael.

**MICHAEL:** *(just as quietly)* Rule four, Donald.

## SCENE 13 — THE SHOE
**INT. MICHAEL'S G-WAGON — NIGHT — DRIVING (AFTER PART ONE, SCENE 22)**
*(~9:26–10:18)*

*Michael drives with one hand, eating jollof from Donald's plate. A woman's size thirty-eight heel sits on the passenger seat. He's on speakerphone with Sophia, laughing hard.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia, listen! DONALD IRWIN is hiding a woman in his kitchen! I have her SHOE!

**SOPHIA (ON PHONE):** *(laughing despite herself)* The serious one? The one who schedules his lunch?

**MICHAEL:** 12:07, every day! And tonight there were two plates of jollof!

**SOPHIA (ON PHONE):** So tell Geoffrey. Win your little rooftop game.

**MICHAEL:** *(the laugh fading)* I can't.

**SOPHIA (ON PHONE):** Why not?

**MICHAEL:** Because he heard me say your name. Donald knows about you, and I know about her. If I expose him, he exposes me.

**SOPHIA (ON PHONE):** *(quietly)* So we're both somebody's secret. *(beat)* Are you still angry I left on my birthday?

**MICHAEL:** I'm angry I made you leave.

*A soft silence on the line.*

**SOPHIA (ON PHONE):** That's the first nice thing you've ever said without a camera nearby.

## SCENE 14 — THE RULES
**INT. MICHAEL'S TRAILER — FILM SET — NIGHT**
*(~10:18–10:52)*

*Michael paces. Sophia sits on the sofa, unimpressed.*

**MICHAEL:** Tomorrow, Donald and Geoffrey are visiting the set. Investor visit. So for one day, we're not… whatever we are.

**SOPHIA:** What are we pretending to be?

**MICHAEL:** Enemies. You're the writer suing me. You hate me. Be cold. Be rude. Insult me if you have to.

**SOPHIA:** *(a slow, dangerous smile)* Insult you?

**MICHAEL:** …Not too much.

**SOPHIA:** Michael, I've been rehearsing for this role since the day I met you.

## SCENE 15 — THE WORST ACTOR IN NIGERIA
**EXT. THE LAST HORIZON SET — DAY**
*(~10:52–11:33)*

*Donald and Geoffrey walk onto the set in suits, with Kola guiding them. Sophia is at the monitor. Michael meets them, too cheerful.*

**MICHAEL:** Brothers! Welcome! This is… *(He gestures at Sophia stiffly.)* …that writer. The one suing us.

**SOPHIA:** *(ice cold, not looking up)* Mr. McClaren. Your continuity is a disaster, your actors sound like adverts and your shirt is an insult to fabric.

**DONALD:** *(eyebrows rising)* Ah.

**MICHAEL:** *(overacting)* HOW DARE YOU, madam! *(He steps back dramatically, trips over a camera cable and nearly falls into the monitor.)* …I am FINE. Totally fine.

**SOPHIA:** *(trying not to laugh)* You see? He can't even walk on his own set.

**GEOFFREY:** *(quietly, to Donald)* He's never tripped on a set in fifteen years.

**DONALD:** *(watching Michael closely)* No. He hasn't.

*Sophia hands Michael a script page. Their fingers touch. He jumps as if it burned him.*

**MICHAEL:** Thank you, Sophia, my lo— my… lawyer. My lawyer will contact you.

*Long silence. Donald folds his arms.*

**DONALD:** "Script Supervisor."

**MICHAEL:** *(sweating)* Don't.

## SCENE 16 — THE BROTHERS EXPLODE
**INT. MICHAEL'S TRAILER — MOMENTS LATER**
*(~11:33–12:41)*

*Donald shuts the door. Geoffrey leans against the wall. The three brothers are crammed into a small trailer.*

**DONALD:** Your budget is six billion naira over, you've fired the biggest actor in Nigeria, the insurers are asking questions, and you're on set flirting with the woman who's SUING YOU?

**MICHAEL:** I am not flirting!

**DONALD:** You called her "my love."

**MICHAEL:** I called her "my lawyer"!

**DONALD:** You said "my lo—" and then you invented a lawyer!

**MICHAEL:** *(exploding)* And YOU? You want to talk about rule four? Halbrook is bleeding money, you're hiding a size thirty-eight heel in your kitchen, and you've got the nerve to stand in my trailer and—

*He stops himself. Too late. Donald goes very still.*

**GEOFFREY:** *(slowly)* What heel?

*Donald and Michael look at each other. Silence.*

**DONALD:** *(cold)* Nothing. *(to Michael)* We'll discuss your budget in the boardroom. Like businessmen.

**MICHAEL:** Like brothers, you mean? Oh no, we stopped doing that.

**GEOFFREY:** *(opening the door)* I'm leaving before someone says something they can't take back.

**MICHAEL:** You never say ANYTHING, Geoffrey! That's your whole personality!

*Geoffrey stops in the doorway without turning around.*

**GEOFFREY:** *(quietly)* Some of us are protecting more than a film, Michael.

*He leaves. Donald follows. Michael kicks the trailer wall, hard.*

## SCENE 17 — THE EMAIL SHE WASN'T SUPPOSED TO SEE
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — 1 AM**
*(~12:41–13:05)*

*Michael has given Sophia his laptop and password so she can finish the rewrites overnight while he's at the investor dinner. She's alone in the dark, typing. A new email notification slides in. It's FROM: Michael McClaren, TO: Investors, SUBJECT: The Writer.*

**SOPHIA:** *(reading aloud, her voice slowly dying)* "The writer is irrelevant. Once filming ends, the story belongs to the studio. Her name will be removed from the credits. Let her sue. She can't afford it." *(beat)* "— M."

*She reads it again. Her hand covers her mouth. She looks at the photo of her father on her phone, then back at the screen.*

**SOPHIA:** *(through tears)* "Irrelevant."

*Her jaw sets. She opens the studio's internal email folder labelled "LAST HORIZON — CHANGES", selects everything, attaches her original screenplay and types an address: gist@lagosgistqueen.ng.*

*Her finger hovers over SEND. A long pause.*

**SOPHIA:** You taught me this, Michael. Betray me once…

*She presses SEND.*

## SCENE 18 — "DEAD TO ME"
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — THE NEXT NIGHT**
*(~13:05–14:11)*

*We're back in Scene 1, continuing the moment. Rain, screens and the blogs.*

**MICHAEL:** *(barely a whisper)* …I don't know.

**SOPHIA:** *(holding up her phone to show the email)* "The writer is irrelevant." Did you think I'd never see it?

**MICHAEL:** *(reading it, confused)* I never wrote this.

**SOPHIA:** It's from your account, Michael!

**MICHAEL:** I was at the investor dinner! I didn't touch my phone all night! *(He slams the phone down.)* But YOU went through my laptop. My private files. I gave you my password, Sophia. I've never given ANYONE my password.

**SOPHIA:** And I've never given anyone my father's story! You stole it first!

*Kola enters with two security guards. His face is grave.*

**KOLA:** Sir. Mr. Hargreaves has pulled the deal. Two other investors too. And Mr. Irwin is on line one. He's… shouting.

*Michael looks at Sophia for a long, terrible moment. Then the ruthless director comes back. His voice goes flat and cold.*

**MICHAEL:** Kola. Ban her from every McClaren set. Freeze her payments. Sue her for two billion naira.

**SOPHIA:** *(stunned)* Michael—

**MICHAEL:** You know my rule. *(He turns his back to her.)* Betray me once, and you're dead to me.

*Security steps forward. Sophia walks out on her own and stops at the door.*

**SOPHIA:** *(without turning around)* My father died at sea, Michael. You're dying on land. You just don't know it yet.

*She's gone. Behind Michael's back, Kola allows himself the smallest smile.*

## SCENE 19 — HE CAN'T DELETE HER
**INT. EDITING SUITE — 3 AM**
*(~14:11–14:31)*

*Michael sits alone in the dark, watching footage of Sophia at the table read, laughing, crying over her father's scene and correcting him. His finger hovers over DELETE ALL.*

**KOLA:** *(in the doorway)* Delete it, sir. She's poison.

*Michael doesn't move.*

**KOLA:** Sir, you've destroyed men for less. Chuka. Dapo. A hundred others. Delete her.

**MICHAEL:** *(very quietly)* Leave me, Kola.

**KOLA:** Sir—

**MICHAEL:** *(roaring)* LEAVE ME!

*Kola leaves. Michael presses PLAY again. On screen, Sophia says, "Every evening, my mother stood on the beach until the horizon swallowed the sun." He mouths the words along with her.*

**MICHAEL:** *(whispering to the screen)* Why can't I hate you?

## SCENE 20 — "I STILL LOVE HIM"
**INT. MAMA BENNETT'S HOUSE, BADAGRY — NIGHT**
*(~14:31–15:13)*

*Sophia sits on the floor with her head in her mother's lap.*

**SOPHIA:** I betrayed him, Mama. I sent the emails. I wanted to hurt him the way he hurt me.

**MAMA BENNETT:** *(stroking her hair)* Hurt people throw stones, my daughter. Even good people.

**SOPHIA:** He's suing me for two billion naira.

**MAMA BENNETT:** And?

**SOPHIA:** *(her voice cracking)* And I still love him. *(beat)* What kind of fool still loves the man who's destroying her?

**MAMA BENNETT:** The same kind who stood on that beach every evening for twenty years, waiting for a boat. *(She smiles sadly.)* It runs in our family.

*Sophia's phone buzzes. It's a news alert: "BREAKING: Confidential Last Horizon footage leaked online. Insurance cancelled. McClaren Studios in crisis."*

**SOPHIA:** *(sitting up, confused)* Footage? I didn't send any footage.

## SCENE 21 — THE SABOTAGE GETS WORSE
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — CONFERENCE ROOM — DAY**
*(~15:13–15:57)*

*Chaos. Michael, Kola, Bisi and lawyers are all there. Screens show the leaked footage everywhere.*

**BISI:** The camera crane malfunctioned this morning, sir. It nearly fell on the crew.

**KOLA:** And the leaked footage came from a login with Miss Bennett's credentials. There's also… *(He slides a paper across.)* …a transfer. Five million naira from a studio account, to a man named Femi Bennett. Her cousin.

**MICHAEL:** *(staring at the paper)* She's banned. She has no access.

**KOLA:** Her credentials do, sir. Maybe she had a partner. *(beat)* Sir, I'm sorry. She fooled all of us.

*Michael picks up the paper. His hand is shaking.*

**MICHAEL:** *(to himself)* The emails, I understand. But the crane? People could have died.

**KOLA:** Hurt women do crazy things, sir.

*Michael looks up at Kola for a long beat. Something flickers, and then it's gone.*

## SCENE 22 — 3 A.M., IN THE RAIN
**EXT. MAMA BENNETT'S GATE, BADAGRY — 3 AM — RAIN**
*(~15:57–17:01)*

*Michael stands at the gate, soaked, without an umbrella or a driver. Sophia opens the door and freezes.*

**SOPHIA:** You came to serve me more court papers? At 3 a.m.?

**MICHAEL:** Did you leak the footage? *(beat)* Did you touch the crane?

**SOPHIA:** *(walking into the rain to face him)* No. I sent the emails. Only the emails. And I'm sorry. I'm sorry I did it. But I would NEVER put your crew in danger. Never.

*He searches her face.*

**MICHAEL:** I've destroyed people for less than what you did. I know exactly how to ruin you. I have the lawyers, the money, the blogs. *(His voice breaks.)* So why am I standing in the rain outside your mother's gate at 3 a.m.?

**SOPHIA:** *(softly)* Because you're not the man you pretend to be.

**MICHAEL:** I don't know how to forgive, Sophia. I've never done it. Not once. I don't even know what it feels like.

**SOPHIA:** *(touching his wet face)* It feels like this. Like standing in the rain when you could be dry.

*He kisses her desperately. Then he pulls away, shaking, and walks back to his car without a word. She stands in the rain, confused and heartbroken.*

---

## SCENE 23 — "NOT NOW, MICHAEL"
**INT. EVERYS — BACKSTAGE — DAY — (PART ONE, SCENE 36)**
*(~17:01–17:30)*

*Donald is pressed against the wall, wrecked. Emma has been kidnapped. Michael and Geoffrey are with him. Michael's phone buzzes with a message from "Script Supervisor": "Tonight, 8pm. Same restaurant. Last chance, Michael. If you don't come, I'm leaving Lagos."*

**DONALD:** *(broken)* I love her. I broke the vow. Take my share. Just help me find her. Please.

**MICHAEL:** *(grabbing his face)* You idiot. You think we didn't know?

**GEOFFREY:** We protect each other. Whatever it costs.

*Michael looks at his phone, then at Donald, then back at the phone.*

**MICHAEL:** And Donald, since we're confessing, I also have to tell you—

**GEOFFREY:** *(sharply)* Not now, Michael.

*Michael closes his mouth. He looks at Sophia's message one last time, puts the phone away and grabs his car keys.*

**MICHAEL:** *(to Donald)* My car. Let's go get her.

## SCENE 24 — THE SECOND EMPTY CHAIR
**INT. THE SAME RESTAURANT, VICTORIA ISLAND — NIGHT**
*(~17:30–17:43)*

*The same table and the same window. Sophia waits, without a red dress this time, just a travel bag under the table. 8:00. 9:00. 10:30. 11:45. The candle burns down to nothing.*

**WAITER:** Madam, we're closing.

**SOPHIA:** *(quietly)* I know. *(She stands and looks at the empty chair.)* He'll always choose something bigger than me.

*She types on her phone: "Goodbye, Michael." Send. She leaves the chair empty and walks out with her bag.*

## SCENE 25 — "WHEN IS IT ME?"
**EXT. MAMA BENNETT'S HOUSE, BADAGRY — DAWN**
*(~17:43–18:33)*

*A taxi waits, loaded with bags. Michael's car screeches up. He jumps out still wearing last night's clothes, dirty and exhausted, with a cut on his hand.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia! Wait! I couldn't come last night! My brother… Donald's… someone he loves was taken. We were in a railway yard in Ebute-Metta until 4 a.m.

**SOPHIA:** *(turning, surprised, then softening)* Is she alright?

**MICHAEL:** She's alive. She's safe.

**SOPHIA:** *(quietly)* I'm glad. *(beat)* Then you did the right thing.

**MICHAEL:** So you'll stay?

**SOPHIA:** No.

**MICHAEL:** *(stunned)* Sophia—

**SOPHIA:** Last night it was your brother. And I understand. But before that it was the deal, before that the film, and before that the investors. And next time it will be your vow. *(beat)* There's always something bigger, Michael. When is it ME?

*He has no answer. She gets into the taxi.*

**SOPHIA:** And you're still suing me for two billion naira.

*The taxi drives away. Michael stands in the dust, alone.*

---

## SCENE 26 — "SOMEONE SITTING CLOSE TO YOU"
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — DAY**
*(~18:33–19:33)*

*Michael sits at his desk with Sophia's goodbye text open. Geoffrey enters quietly and closes the door.*

**GEOFFREY:** I've been looking into your sabotage. Quietly. Like I do everything.

**MICHAEL:** *(not looking up)* It's Sophia. Her credentials, her cousin, the transfer.

**GEOFFREY:** Her credentials were copied from inside McClaren Studios. From a computer on this floor. *(He sets down a printout.)* The footage leaked from a studio terminal at a time when Sophia was in Badagry. I checked her phone records. She hasn't left her mother's house in a week.

*Michael slowly looks up.*

**GEOFFREY:** And the email she saw, "the writer is irrelevant"? It was sent from your account at 12:58 a.m. on the night of the investor dinner. *(beat)* Michael, you were sitting next to me at that dinner. Your phone was in your pocket the whole night. I watched it.

**MICHAEL:** *(standing slowly)* Then who…

**GEOFFREY:** *(quietly)* The leak isn't outside, Michael. *(He looks at the empty chair beside Michael's desk, the one Kola always sits in.)* It's someone sitting close to you.

## SCENE 27 — THE THIRD BROTHER
**INT. McCLAREN STUDIOS — MICHAEL'S OFFICE — NIGHT**
*(~19:33–20:53)*

*A trap. Michael sits casually. Kola enters with two coffees, just like every night for fifteen years.*

**MICHAEL:** Kola. The master copy of the film. The only one. I'm moving it to the vault at Geoffrey's bank tomorrow at 6 a.m. Nobody else knows.

**KOLA:** Good idea, sir. Safer.

*He leaves. A moment later, Geoffrey's laptop on the side table lights up: "Intercepted call — K. Badmus → V. Adekunle (Apex Studios)."*

**KOLA (ON RECORDING):** Victor, the master moves at six. Get your people ready. And after this, McClaren is finished. You promised me my own studio.

*Michael closes his eyes. Then he calls out quietly.*

**MICHAEL:** Kola. Come back in.

*Kola returns and sees the laptop. His face drops.*

**MICHAEL:** Fifteen years. Enugu. The weddings. You carried my cameras when I couldn't afford a bus. *(His voice is shaking.)* Why?

**KOLA:** *(bitter, all the politeness gone)* Fifteen years, and you never once put my name on a poster. "A Michael McClaren film." Always Michael. Victor offered me my own studio. My OWN name. *(beat)* You have two brothers already, Michael. Donald and Geoffrey. You never had room for a third.

*Silence. That one landed.*

**MICHAEL:** You faked the email. You framed her. You let me destroy the woman I—

**KOLA:** *(smirking)* You did that yourself, sir. I only gave you a reason. You were always so ready to believe people betray you.

*Michael stands. Every ruthless instinct in him rises. He walks right up to Kola, fists clenched. Then he stops.*

**MICHAEL:** *(quietly)* The old Michael would spend five years ruining you. *(He opens the door. Two POLICE OFFICERS wait outside.)* But I've got somewhere more important to be.

## SCENE 28 — THE LAST LIE ON THE ROOFTOP
**EXT. AJEGUNLE ROOFTOP — ROOFTOP NIGHT — (PART ONE, SCENE 45)**
*(~20:53–21:54)*

*Fairy lights. Donald has just confessed and handed over his piece of the certificate. Everyone is laughing. Emma waits by the stairs. Michael's phone buzzes with a message from "Script Supervisor ❤️": "Where are you? You promised you'd tell them tonight."*

**MICHAEL:** *(raising his glass)* Well. One down. *(turning to Geoffrey)* Still sworn?

**GEOFFREY:** *(pause)* …Still sworn.

**MICHAEL:** *(too loud, his heart breaking as he says it)* STILL SWORN!

*Under the table, he turns his phone off. Donald watches him over his glass and says nothing.*

*LATER. Michael's apartment, 1 a.m. Sophia sits on his sofa in the dark. She's back, and his heart leaps.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia! You came back. The lawsuit's gone, I cancelled it, I've been trying to tell you—

**SOPHIA:** *(quietly)* I know. Geoffrey called me. He told me about Kola. *(beat)* And then I waited for you to tell your brothers about me. Tonight. Like you promised.

**MICHAEL:** I was going to. I swear. But Donald had just confessed, and everyone was laughing, and I—

**SOPHIA:** You said "still sworn." *(Her eyes fill with tears.)* I forgave you for the lawsuit. I forgave you for my birthday. I even forgave you for my father's ending. But I can't keep being the woman you're ashamed to be seen with.

*She puts his spare key on the table.*

**SOPHIA:** You're a brilliant director, Michael. You've controlled every scene of your life. *(beat)* Except the one where you choose me.

*She walks out. This time he doesn't chase her. He sits in the dark and looks at the framed piece of the old ₦3,000 certificate on his wall.*

---

## SCENE 29 — THE PREMIERE
**INT. LAGOS GRAND CINEMA — PREMIERE OF "THE LAST HORIZON" — NIGHT**
*(~21:54–23:32)*

*Red carpet, flashing cameras and a packed hall of a thousand people. Donald and Emma sit in the front row, and Geoffrey sits beside them. In the very back row, half hidden, is Sophia, with Mama Bennett holding her hand tightly so she can't leave. Michael walks onto the stage. The crowd goes quiet.*

**MICHAEL:** Good evening. Tonight was supposed to be a story about a fisherman who comes home rich. That's the version I sold to the world. *(beat)* It was a lie.

*Murmurs in the hall.*

**MICHAEL:** This film was written by Sophia Bennett. *(The screen behind him reads: "WRITTEN BY SOPHIA BENNETT.")* Her father went to sea and never came back. Every evening, her mother stood on the beach until the horizon swallowed the sun. I changed that ending because I thought sadness wouldn't sell. *(beat)* Tonight, you'll see the real ending.

*In the back row, Mama Bennett squeezes Sophia's hand. Sophia is crying.*

**MICHAEL:** Sophia once betrayed me. And I was ready to destroy her for it, because that's who I was. A man who never forgave anybody. *(beat)* But I betrayed her first. I took her father's story and sold it, and I hid her like a secret because of a promise I made with my brothers on a rooftop in Ajegunle.

*In the front row, Donald and Geoffrey slowly turn to look at each other.*

**MICHAEL:** So tonight, in front of a thousand people, two brothers and every camera in Lagos, I'm breaking that promise. *(He looks straight at the back row.)* I love Sophia Bennett. I've loved her since she sat in my chair and told me I was in her story. *(beat)* Sophia. You asked me, "When is it me?" *(His voice breaks.)* It's you. Tonight, it's you, in front of everyone.

*Silence. Then a woman stands up in the back row. Every head and every camera turns. Sophia walks slowly down the long aisle and stops at the foot of the stage.*

**SOPHIA:** *(tears and a small smile)* You're late again, Mr. Director.

**MICHAEL:** *(kneeling at the edge of the stage)* I know. But for once, I'm early for something that matters.

*She reaches up. He takes her hand and helps her onto the stage, and they kiss in front of a thousand people. The hall explodes with applause. In the front row, Emma claps hardest. Donald laughs and shakes his head. Geoffrey takes off his glasses and wipes them, slowly.*

**DONALD:** *(to Geoffrey, over the noise)* Script Supervisor.

**GEOFFREY:** *(almost smiling)* I've known for months.

## SCENE 30 — TWO DOWN
**EXT. AJEGUNLE ROOFTOP — NIGHT — ONE WEEK LATER**
*(~23:32 — END)*

*The three leather chairs and fairy lights. Michael stands before his brothers holding his framed piece of the old ₦3,000 certificate. Sophia and Emma sit on the steps, laughing and sharing suya.*

**MICHAEL:** By the rules of this rooftop. I, Michael McClaren, confess. I fell in love. I broke the vow. *(He places his piece on the table.)* And by the way, Donald, I found your woman's shoe first. I just want that on record.

**DONALD:** *(laughing)* And I heard "Sophia" first.

**MICHAEL:** *(serious now)* And brothers… no more secrets. Not about women, not about money, not about anything. My budget, my sabotage, my mistakes. Everything goes on the table. That's how we started, and that's how we finish.

**DONALD:** *(putting his hand on Michael's shoulder)* Agreed. No more secrets.

*They both turn to Geoffrey. He stands at the edge of the rooftop with his back to them, looking at the lights of Ikoyi, where his father's mansion still stands.*

**MICHAEL:** Geoffrey?

**DONALD:** You heard him. No more secrets.

*Long pause.*

**GEOFFREY:** *(without turning)* Still sworn.

**MICHAEL:** Geoffrey…

**GEOFFREY:** *(turning, forcing a calm smile)* I said, still sworn.

*His phone buzzes. He glances at it, and for the first time in his life, Geoffrey Durose's hand trembles. Michael and Donald see it.*

**DONALD:** *(quietly)* Geoffrey. What's wrong?

*Geoffrey answers the phone and puts it to his ear. A woman's voice comes through, calm and cold.*

**OLIVIA (ON PHONE):** Mr. Durose. My name is Olivia Hart. My father died helping build the fortune you inherited. *(beat)* And I think your father killed him.

*Geoffrey slowly lowers the phone. Michael and Donald stare at him.*

**MICHAEL:** Geoffrey. Who was that?

**GEOFFREY:** *(barely a whisper)* …The end of my family.

**TITLE CARD:** *THE SWORN BACHELORS. PART THREE: GEOFFREY × OLIVIA. "THE SECRET OF DUROSE."*

**END.**
