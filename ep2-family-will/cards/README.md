# Episode 2 · ON SCREEN cards (14-minute version)

Animated info cards (MP4, 1920×1080, 30 fps, no sound), with the characters from each scene. The `.jpg` files are only still previews; use the `.mp4` files.

## How to place them

The card goes **between two lines of narration**, and the voice **pauses** while the card plays.

```
…the voice says the "after" line  →  CARD (voice paused, sound effect)  →  the voice continues with the "before" line
```

1. Play to the end of the "after" line.
2. Split the voiceover there and drag everything after it to the right, leaving a gap the length of the card.
3. Put the card in the gap and add a sound effect (tick for clocks, buzz for phones, cha-ching for money, a thud for PAID IN FULL).
4. Keep the background music running underneath.

The 35 cards add about 3.2 minutes of pauses. To stay close to 14 minutes, speed the longer cards up to 1.5× or skip a few year cards.
