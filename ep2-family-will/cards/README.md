41 animated info cards (MP4 videos, 1920×1080, 30 fps, no sound), with the characters from each scene. The `.jpg` files are only still previews; use the `.mp4` files.

## How to place them

Same method as Episode 1: the card goes **between two lines of narration**, and the voice **pauses** while the card plays.

```
…the voice says the "after" line  →  CARD (voice paused, sound effect)  →  the voice continues with the "before" line
```

1. Play to the end of the "after" line.
2. Split the voiceover there and drag everything after it to the right, leaving a gap the length of the card.
3. Put the card in the gap and add a sound effect (tick for clocks, buzz for phones, cha-ching for money, a stamp thud for PAID IN FULL).
4. Keep the background music running underneath.

The 41 cards add about 3 minutes 45 seconds of pauses, so the video lands at about 43 minutes. To stay closer to 40, speed the longer cards up to 1.5×.

Timecodes are approximate (2.6 words per second) until the voiceover is recorded; go by the lines.
