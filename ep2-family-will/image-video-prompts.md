# Episode 2 · Image and Video Prompts

**POV: Your Family Cut You Off Because You Were "Broke"... Then They Saw Your Will**

173 shots · **147 images (85% of runtime)** · **26 video clips (15% of runtime)** · cold open 58% video · 35 on-screen cards · narration ≈ 14:15

## How to use this book

1. **Make the 9 reference images first** (section 1). Every shot lists which ones to upload.
2. **IMAGE shots:** generate the image; in the edit, give every still a slow zoom or pan.
3. **VIDEO shots:** generate the start-frame image, then animate it with the video prompt. Generate the clip length shown and trim to the narration.
4. **Cards:** when a shot says **THEN PLAY CARD**, put that card from `episode2-on-screen-cards.zip` right after the shot, with the voice paused (35 cards in total).
5. **Timecodes** assume 2.6 words per second plus the script's pauses. Once the voiceover is recorded, slide each shot to its line.
6. **Captions:** keep word-by-word captions in the bottom 15% of the frame (every prompt keeps that area clear).

**Negative prompt (add to every image if your tool supports it):** no photorealism, no 3D render, no anime style, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no garbled text, no watermark, no logo, no speech bubbles, no captions baked into the image

**Color rule:** the present-day Tuesday is cold blue with phone glow; the family photos you're excluded from are bright and warm; the past is faded sepia; the ending warms to gold.

## Google Flow and Grok: uploading references

1. Make the 9 reference images first (section 1) and save them with the names shown.
2. For each shot, attach exactly the images on its UPLOAD FIRST line, then paste the prompt. In Google Flow, add them as ingredients or reference images; in Grok Imagine, upload them with the prompt.
3. Attach only what's listed. Extra references can mix up faces.
4. IMAGE shots: generate, pick the best, and save it with its shot number (shot-012.png).
5. VIDEO shots: make the start-frame image first, then animate it from that first frame with the video prompt.
6. Shots longer than 8 seconds: in the edit, split the still into two moves (a slow push-in, then a cut to a tighter crop of a detail) so the picture changes about every 4 seconds.

## Policy check (Google Flow and Grok)

- Every character is fictional: no real people, celebrities or public figures. Only upload images you created yourself (the REF and LOC sheets).
- Dad's heart attack is never shown: only a spilled grocery bag and distant ambulance lights. Hospital scenes are a garage, a lobby and a bill. No injury, blood or medical procedure on screen.
- Danny's nephew appears only in everyday family moments (a birthday, a photo, the back steps). He is never used as a reference upload. If a tool is strict about children, frame him from behind or at a distance.
- No brand names or logos: cars, apps and stores are generic. Danny's "SHINE SQUAD" logo is his own made-up business.
- No violence, weapons, nudity, sexual content, drugs, gambling imagery or hate content. The gambling line is only spoken by Mom, never shown.
- No prompt imitates a named artist, studio or franchise. 'Somber Journal style' is your own channel.
- If a generation is blocked, rewrite that one line more neutrally. Never work around a block by misspelling words.
