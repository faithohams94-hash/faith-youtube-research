# ỌMỌ ÌYÁ MI | My Mother's Child | Nollywood Family Drama

**Format:** Feature-length YouTube Nollywood drama (target runtime 75 to 90 min)
**Language:** English with Yoruba dialogue (English subtitles)
**Genre:** Family betrayal, revenge, redemption
**Logline:** When a jealous sister poisons a happy marriage with lies, a wife is thrown out with nothing. Years later she owns the mansion her husband lost, and the secret her mother left behind exposes everything.

# COLD OPEN (0:00 to 4:00)

**EXT. ADEBISI MANSION GATE. NIGHT. HEAVY RAIN.**

Black screen. Rain. A gate slams.

ADUNNI stands outside in a soaked wrapper, holding one cloth bag. The mansion glows behind her through the gate bars.

**TUNDE** *(from the doorway, voice cracking)*
Take your things and go.

**ADUNNI**
Tunde, I have never touched another man. Please look at me.

**TUNDE**
I looked at those pictures every night for a week. Do not make me say it again.

**ADUNNI**
Who gave you those pictures?

Tunde does not answer. He looks away. The security man closes the gate.

Adunni grips the bars.

**ADUNNI** *(quietly)*
Someone who hates me did this.

A car pulls up. A rear window lowers. **MORENIKE** looks out with a concerned face.

**MORENIKE**
Adunni! My sister, come, come inside. Let me take you home.

Adunni cries into her sister's arms. Over her shoulder, Morenike's face changes. The concern drops. For one second she smiles.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:** *ỌMỌ ÌYÁ MI*

# ACT ONE: THE PERFECT MARRIAGE (4:00 to 28:00)

### SCENE 1: THE ADEBISI MANSION. MORNING.

A bright, expensive house. Adunni in a simple wrapper arranges breakfast. She hums. Tunde comes down in a suit, kisses her forehead.

**TUNDE**
You are up before the cook again.

**ADUNNI**
A wife should know her husband's food.

**TUNDE**
You are not a servant here. You are the queen.

They laugh. Chief Mrs. Adebisi watches from the stairs, softly amused.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Tunde, leave the girl alone. Her soup is better than anyone's.

*(Establish: this home is warm. Adunni is loved. This is what Morenike will destroy.)*

### SCENE 2: MORENIKE'S RENTED FLAT. SAME MORNING.

Cramped, hot, a broken fan. BANKOLE lies on the sofa scrolling his phone. Morenike stirs a thin pot of stew.

**BANKOLE**
Where is the money?

**MORENIKE**
Which money? I paid the landlord last week with my own salary.

**BANKOLE**
You talk too much for a woman who earns so little.

She says nothing. She grips the spoon until her knuckles go pale.

### SCENE 3: MORENIKE VISITS THE MANSION. DAY.

Morenike arrives with a small bag of oranges. Adunni runs out and hugs her.

**ADUNNI**
Sister! You didn't say you were coming.

**MORENIKE** *(brightly)*
Can't a sister visit?

Tunde hands Morenike an envelope.

**TUNDE**
For your trouble, Sister. Adunni told me about the rent.

Morenike thanks him warmly. When she turns away, her expression is stone. *Every kindness is an insult to her.*

### SCENE 4: THE BIRTHDAY CAR. EVENING.

The garden. Balloons. Staff clapping. Adunni stands blindfolded while Tunde guides her.

**TUNDE**
Open your eyes.

A white SUV with a red ribbon. Adunni gasps and covers her mouth.

**ADUNNI**
Tunde! What did I do to deserve you?

**TUNDE**
You married me.

She hugs him as the staff cheers. Morenike stands near the gate clapping slowly. Her smile does not reach her eyes.

### SCENE 5: THE BREAKING POINT. NIGHT.

Morenike arrives home. Bankole is waiting.

**BANKOLE**
Did your rich sister give you something?

**MORENIKE**
Not everything is about money.

**BANKOLE**
Everything is about money. Look at her husband. He buys her cars. What have you ever bought me?

He kicks the table over. Morenike stares at the broken plate on the floor. She does not cry.

**MORENIKE** *(low, to herself)*
If I cannot have happiness, she will not keep hers.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

*(Act One end. Cliffhanger: the audience knows what is coming and the sisters do not.)*

# ACT TWO: THE LIES (28:00 to 58:00)

### SCENE 6: THE FIRST VISIT. DAY.

Morenike arrives at Chief Mrs. Adebisi's sitting room with a gift of kola nut. She looks worried.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Morenike, what brings you here so early?

**MORENIKE**
Mama, I do not know how to say this. Adunni is my sister and I love her, but I am afraid for this family.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Speak.

**MORENIKE**
She has been saying things about you when you are not around. That you are old-fashioned, that you control Tunde.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI** *(coldly)*
I do not believe it. That girl cooks for me with her own hands.

**MORENIKE**
Maybe I misheard. Please forgive me. I only came out of love.

She leaves. The old woman sits very still. *The seed is planted.*

### SCENE 7: THE SECOND VISIT. A WEEK LATER.

**MORENIKE**
Mama, it is about the money. Adunni has been sending cash to someone. A man.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Be careful, Morenike. That is a serious accusation.

**MORENIKE**
I am not accusing. I only tell you what I saw.

She plays a voice note on her phone. A woman's voice, edited and cut, says: *"Tunde does not need to know."*

### SCENE 8: THE FORGERY. NIGHT.

A tiny photo studio. FEMI the photographer works at a laptop. Morenike counts out cash on the desk.

**FEMI**
Madam, this is risky. You want her face on another woman's body?

**MORENIKE**
I want a photograph of her and a man at a restaurant. Make it look real.

**FEMI**
And if someone checks?

**MORENIKE**
Nobody will check. People believe what they already fear.

She slides the money across. Femi pockets it, uneasy.

### SCENE 9: THE FAKE MESSAGES. DAY.

Morenike sits in a café, typing on a second phone. We see the screen: a conversation labeled "Kunle," full of affectionate messages between two people.

She takes screenshots. She whispers each message aloud as she types.

### SCENE 10: THE CONFRONTATION IN THE LIVING ROOM. EVENING.

Chief Mrs. Adebisi hands Tunde a folder. He opens it.

**TUNDE**
What is this?

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
I did not want to believe it. Look at the pictures.

Tunde flips through. His face changes by degrees: confusion, then pain, then fury.

**TUNDE**
Where did you get these?

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
It was brought to me by someone who loves this family.

Adunni walks in with a tray of tea. She stops.

**ADUNNI**
What is wrong?

Tunde holds up one photo. Adunni stares.

**ADUNNI**
That is not me.

**TUNDE**
Then who is it?

**ADUNNI**
I do not know! But it is not me.

**TUNDE**
The messages say otherwise.

**ADUNNI**
What messages? Show me!

He will not. Her voice breaks.

**ADUNNI**
Tunde, I would never.

**TUNDE** *(quietly)*
I want to believe you. God knows I do.

**ADUNNI**
Then believe me.

He turns away.

**TUNDE**
Leave.

*(This flows straight into the cold open at the gate. Mid-point cliffhanger: the audience sees the eviction again, now knowing everything.)*

### SCENE 11: THE COMFORT. NIGHT.

Morenike's flat. Adunni sits on the floor, weeping. Morenike brings her tea and strokes her hair.

**MORENIKE**
Cry, my sister. Men are like this. They believe the first thing they hear.

**ADUNNI**
But why would anyone do this to me?

**MORENIKE**
The world is wicked. Sleep. Tomorrow we will think.

Adunni sleeps. Morenike watches her for a long moment. Then she goes to the kitchen and drinks water alone, almost smiling.

### SCENE 12: THE FLASHBACK. MAMA ABIKE'S WILL.

**INT. COKER CHAMBERS. DAY (YEARS EARLIER).**

Mama Abike, frail, signs papers with BARRISTER COKER. The two girls wait in the corridor.

**BARRISTER COKER**
Madam, are you sure you want to leave the workshop to Adunni alone?

**MAMA ABIKE**
Morenike has never loved the cloth. Adunni weaves with her heart. When I am gone, she will need something that cannot be taken from her.

**BARRISTER COKER**
And if Morenike asks?

**MAMA ABIKE**
Then tell her nothing until Adunni is ready. A mother knows her children. One of them will need protecting.

She seals the envelope.

# ACT THREE: THE WORKSHOP AND THE COMEBACK (58:00 to 82:00)

### SCENE 13: THE OLD WORKSHOP. DAWN.

A dusty weaving house. Looms under cloth sheets. BABA SULE sweeps the floor. Adunni stands at the door with her bag.

**BABA SULE**
Adunni? Mama Abike's daughter?

**ADUNNI**
Baba, I have nowhere else to go.

**BABA SULE**
Nowhere? This is your house. Your mother left it to you.

**ADUNNI**
What?

He leads her to a locked cabinet, opens it, and hands her a sealed letter.

**BABA SULE**
Barrister Coker left this for you. He said you would come when you needed it.

She opens it. Inside is the deed and a note in her mother's handwriting.

**MAMA ABIKE** *(V.O.)*
*My Adunni. If you are reading this, someone has hurt you. Do not be bitter. Be useful. The cloth will carry you.*

Adunni weeps, then wipes her eyes.

**ADUNNI**
Baba, teach me everything.

### SCENE 14: THE REBUILDING. MONTAGE.

- Adunni sweeping, dyeing, learning the looms
- SADE joins as her first assistant
- Late nights sketching new patterns
- A first small order from a church choir
- A first big order from an Abuja designer
- A showroom with her mother's photo on the wall

**TITLE OVER IMAGE:** *Seven years later*

### SCENE 15: THE FALL OF THE ADEBISIS. DAY.

Tunde at his desk. Lawyers, files. His phone rings.

**LAWYER (V.O.)**
Sir, the bank is calling in the loans.

**TUNDE**
How much time?

**LAWYER (V.O.)**
Thirty days.

Tunde puts his head in his hands. Across the city, Chief Mrs. Adebisi sits in the empty living room. The paintings are already gone.

### SCENE 16: THE AUCTION. DAY.

A hall. The Adebisi mansion is on a projector. A crowd of buyers.

**AUCTIONEER**
Lot 14. The Adebisi property. Opening at three hundred million naira.

Paddles go up. A woman in an elegant gele at the back raises hers. We see only her hands and a single ring.

**AUCTIONEER**
Sold!

### SCENE 17: MORENIKE FINDS OUT. DAY.

Morenike, thinner, her marriage still unhappy, walks into a luxury boutique with a friend. The sign reads **ADUNNI HOUSE OF ASO OKE.**

She freezes at the window. Through the glass: Adunni, glowing, receives a celebrity client.

**MORENIKE** *(whispering)*
How?

She marches in.

**MORENIKE**
Adunni.

**ADUNNI** *(calm)*
Sister. I did not expect you.

**MORENIKE**
This is Mama's workshop. You told me you were broke.

**ADUNNI**
You never asked what Mama left me.

**MORENIKE**
You hid it from me!

**ADUNNI**
You never asked, Morenike. You only came when you wanted something.

Morenike storms out. In the street she calls Bankole, voice shaking.

**MORENIKE**
She has everything. Everything!

### SCENE 18: THE NEW LANDLORD. DAY.

The Adebisis stand outside their mansion with cardboard boxes. A new sign reads **PRIVATE PROPERTY.** A black car pulls up. The window lowers.

**ADUNNI**
Good afternoon, Chief.

Chief Mrs. Adebisi stares.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Adunni?

**ADUNNI**
You may stay until your new place is ready. I am not cruel.

Tunde steps forward, speechless.

**TUNDE**
You bought our house?

**ADUNNI**
I bought a house, Tunde. Nothing more.

# THE TRUTH COMES OUT (74:00 to 82:00)

### SCENE 19: FEMI'S CONFESSION. DAY.

Chief Mrs. Adebisi, now humbled, visits a small studio. Femi sits behind his desk, older and nervous.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
Young man, seven years ago, did you make a photograph for a woman?

**FEMI**
Ma, I...

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
I am an old woman. I have nothing left to threaten you with. I only want the truth.

Femi breaks. He opens a drawer and hands her an old flash drive.

**FEMI**
I saved everything, Ma. The original photos, the messages. I never forgot. She paid me. Her name is Morenike.

### SCENE 20: THE FAMILY GATHERING. EVENING.

The big living room of the mansion. Everyone sits: Tunde, Chief Mrs. Adebisi, Adunni, Morenike, Bankole. Adunni has invited them all.

**ADUNNI**
Thank you for coming. I have something to show you.

The projector lights the wall. Femi's original photos. The raw images. The fake messages, with timestamps.

Morenike's face drains.

**MORENIKE**
This is a lie.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI** *(standing)*
Sit down, Morenike. For seven years I wondered who poisoned my son's marriage. I will not wonder anymore.

**TUNDE** *(shaking)*
Morenike. Why?

Morenike looks at each face. No escape.

### SCENE 21: THE BREAKDOWN.

**MORENIKE**
You want to know why? Because she had everything! Because everything came easily to her! A husband who loved her, a car, a mansion. And me? I had a man who spat on me, and she never even noticed.

**ADUNNI**
I did not know, Morenike.

**MORENIKE**
You never asked!

**ADUNNI** *(softly)*
You never told me.

Silence. Morenike sinks to the floor. The anger leaves her.

**MORENIKE** *(sobbing)*
I am sorry. I am so sorry. I did not think it would go this far. I only wanted to feel what you feel.

Chief Mrs. Adebisi turns to Adunni. This proud woman kneels.

**CHIEF MRS. ADEBISI**
My daughter, forgive an old fool.

**ADUNNI** *(lifting her)*
Mama, please. I forgave you long ago.

### SCENE 22: TUNDE'S PLEA.

Tunde approaches, eyes wet.

**TUNDE**
Adunni, come back. We can start again. I was blind, I was a coward.

**ADUNNI**
You were. And you were also my husband.

**TUNDE**
Then...

**ADUNNI**
I forgive you, Tunde. But I will not return.

**TUNDE**
Why?

**ADUNNI**
Because when I needed you to trust me, you chose a photograph over my face. I built a life without you. I like the woman it made.

He nods slowly. He understands.

### SCENE 23: THE SISTERS. FINAL SCENE.

**EXT. GARDEN. SUNSET.**

Adunni sits on a bench. Morenike approaches, hesitant.

**MORENIKE**
Will you ever speak to me again?

**ADUNNI**
You are my sister. I will not stop being your sister.

**MORENIKE**
But?

**ADUNNI**
But trust is not something I can hand back. It has to be earned. Slowly.

**MORENIKE**
I will earn it.

**ADUNNI**
Start by leaving Bankole. You deserve better than what you accepted.

Morenike cries. Adunni takes her hand.

**ADUNNI**
Mama used to say we are ọmọ ìyá, children of one mother. Whatever we do to each other, that does not change.

They sit together in silence as the sun goes down.

**FINAL IMAGE:** Adunni walks into her showroom. Her mother's photograph hangs on the wall. She touches the frame.

**ADUNNI** *(softly)*
Mama, I did it.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:** *ỌMỌ ÌYÁ MI. My Mother's Child.*

# PRODUCTION NOTES

**Pacing for retention:**
- Hook within 10 seconds (the gate scene)
- A turn every 5 to 7 minutes
- Place mid-roll ads at 28:00, 48:00 and 66:00, each right after a cliffhanger

**Music:**
- Soft talking drum and shekere for the happy scenes
- Low strings and sparse piano for Morenike's scenes
- Gospel-tinged choir for the final garden scene

**Visual language:**
- Warm gold light for the mansion in Act One
- Cold blue for Morenike's flat and the eviction
- Rich indigo and aso oke patterns for the workshop and the comeback

**Casting tip for the thumbnail:** The thumbnail face matters most. Cast Morenike with an expressive, slightly smiling face that reads as innocent at first glance.

**Possible Part 2 (for a series):** Morenike's journey to redemption, and Adunni's fashion house going international.
