# Master Thumbnail Prompt V2 (country economies and geopolitics)

Built from the 5 click drivers in The Invisible Hand's most popular thumbnails (`channel-research/the-invisible-hand/thumbnails/analysis.md`), adapted to our 2D stickman style.

There are two parts.
- PART A: paste it into a chat with your title to get 5 thumbnail concepts and image prompts.
- PART B: a fill-in template for writing a single image prompt by hand.

**Tip:** AI image generators often misspell text. Generate the image **without text**, then add the words in Canva or Photoshop using the text spec the prompt gives you.

---

## PART A: Title → 5 thumbnails

```
You are the thumbnail designer for a faceless YouTube channel about countries' economies and geopolitics: debt crises, collapses and economic miracles gone wrong. Videos are animated with 2D stickman characters, maps and charts. Create 5 high-CTR thumbnail concepts for the video below.

VIDEO TITLE: [PASTE EXACT TITLE]
COUNTRY / REGION: [COUNTRY OR REGION]
KEY FACT OR NUMBER (optional): [e.g. "$51 billion debt", "inflation 85%"]
SCRIPT HOOK (optional): [PASTE THE FIRST 100 WORDS OF THE SCRIPT]

THE 5 CLICK RULES (every concept must follow all 5)
1. THE MAP IS THE MAIN IMAGE: the base is a 3D shaded relief map (textured mountains, cream or sand land, teal-blue sea) or a dark night-style world map. The subject country or region is large, centered or slightly off-center, and recognisable by its shape at phone size.
2. RED MARKS THE COUNTRY IN TROUBLE, EVERYTHING ELSE CALM: only the subject country (or the hot spots) is red. All other land and sea stays cool and muted. Colour budget: cool blue/teal + danger red + ONE yellow highlight. No other bright colours.
3. 1–4 WORDS, A VERDICT OR A MONEY FIGURE: never the topic. Use a verdict word (BANKRUPT, SCAM, TRAP, GAMBLE, BROKE, COLLAPSE, NEXT?, THIS ISN'T NORMAL, POOR) or a money figure ($1 TRILLION, €500 BILLION, 85% INFLATION). Heavy bold all-caps sans-serif, white letters on solid black rectangular boxes. The single most shocking word is yellow on black (optionally underlined). Never repeat the title's words.
4. ONE GRAPHIC THAT TELLS THE STORY: exactly one of these: yellow arrows pointing in or out (money flowing), red network arcs with glowing nodes (a hidden system), cracks across the country (breaking), fire over the country (destruction), a red dashed border (divide or split), chains or a trap (debt trap), a falling red chart line, stickman citizens fleeing or queuing.
5. THE THUMBNAIL ANSWERS THE TITLE: the title asks or explains, and the thumbnail gives a shocking answer or verdict. Together they create a question the viewer must click to resolve.

STICKMAN STYLE (when a character is used)
- Clean 2D stickman figures: round white head, thick black outline, simple body, very expressive face (worried brows, sweat drop, head in hands, shocked open mouth).
- LEADER: a stickman in a dark suit and tie with a small national flag pin, and a solid red bar across the eyes (channel signature). Never a realistic face or likeness of a real person.
- CITIZEN: a stickman in plain clothes holding a prop (an empty wallet, a sack of worthless cash, a "closed" bank sign).
- Characters stand ON or BESIDE the map and take up at most 30–40% of the frame. The map stays the main image.

FIVE LAYOUTS (use each one once across the 5 concepts)
A. MAP NETWORK: a world map with red arcs and glowing red nodes connecting countries, and text in the center. Best for global or big-question videos.
B. RED COUNTRY: one country in red on a 3D map, with cracks or fire, or yellow arrows pointing in or out, and text over or beside the country. Best for collapse videos.
C. SPLIT DIVIDE: two regions, one red and one blue, separated by a red dashed border, with one verdict word on the red side. Best for comparison or "left behind" videos.
D. DARK GLOW: a dark world map, the subject countries glowing red-pink, yellow trade arcs, and white text across the bottom-left. Best for warning or "this isn't normal" videos.
E. LEADER + BURNING COUNTRY: the stickman leader with the red eye bar on the left (chest-up, worried), the country map on fire or cracking on the right, and text on the right over the map. Best for miracle-gone-wrong or gamble videos.

SAFE ZONES
- Keep the bottom-right corner (about 25% wide × 20% tall) completely clear of text and key details, because the duration badge covers it on mobile.
- Leave the top-left corner free for the channel logo.
- Text must not cover the subject country's recognisable outline.
- Must read clearly at phone size (test at 25% zoom).

STYLE (put it inside every image prompt)
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720, 3D shaded relief map with realistic terrain texture, teal-blue ocean, cream land, subject country in saturated red, 2D flat stickman characters with thick black outlines, high contrast, cinematic lighting, clean composition, no text, bottom-right corner empty.

OUTPUT FORMAT (for each of the 5)
## [#]. [Layout letter + concept name]
- Thumbnail text: the exact words, with the yellow word marked in **bold**
- Text position: e.g. "center, two lines" / "right side over the map"
- Story graphic: which one and why
- Title + thumbnail gap: one line on the question the pair creates
- Image prompt (no text): one fully standalone paragraph for an image generator covering map type, subject country, colours, story graphic, stickman (if any), lighting, style and safe zones

After the 5 concepts:
- "Recommended for A/B test:" the 2 strongest concepts and why, in one line each
- "Text alternatives:" 5 more 1–4 word verdict options for this title
```

## PART B: Single image template (no text, add the text in Canva)

```
YouTube thumbnail background, 16:9, 1280x720. A [3D shaded relief map with teal-blue ocean and cream land / dark night-style world map] centered on [COUNTRY/REGION]. [COUNTRY] is filled in saturated red; all other land and sea is cool and muted.
Story graphic: [yellow arrows pointing into/out of COUNTRY / red network arcs with glowing nodes / deep cracks across COUNTRY / fire across COUNTRY / red dashed border between COUNTRY and NEIGHBOR / heavy chains around COUNTRY / falling red chart line].
[Optional: a 2D flat stickman leader in a dark suit with a flag pin and a solid red bar across his eyes, chest-up on the left, worried expression / small stickman citizens on the map holding empty wallets.]
High contrast, cinematic lighting, clean composition, no text, empty space in the [center / right side] for a text box, bottom-right corner empty.
```

### Text spec for Canva
- Font: Anton, Bebas Neue Bold or Montserrat Black, in ALL CAPS
- White text on solid black rectangle boxes, with a little padding around the letters
- One word in yellow (#FFE600) on black, optionally with a yellow underline
- 1–4 words, on two lines at most, with each line at least 1/8 of the thumbnail height
- Channel logo small in the top-left. Nothing in the bottom-right.
