| Plan beat | Where it lands |

| **Act 1: set the contradiction** | Scenes 2–8: the heiress of a ₦-billion tower takes a ₦38,500 cleaning job in her own building. |

| **Climax: the reveal at the worst moment** | Scenes 26–28: midnight on the night of his engagement. "Happy birthday, Madam Chairman." The forty cleaners walk out. |
| **Ending: payoff + lesson** | Scenes 29–32: Mama Ngozi comes in through the front door, the mother apologises, the two cups come back to floor 31, and the lesson card closes the film. |

- ✓ The contradiction is clear in one line: *she mopped his floors; she owned the building.*
- ✓ Every scene has an **Establish** shot and an **Aftermath** beat, and ends on a **Fade**.
- ✓ Romance stays at hugs and leaning on shoulders, the same rule as the Sworn Bachelors scripts.

## 05 · Visual notes for production

- **Open on scenery.** The first frame is the aerial Lagos skyline with the gold tower. Put the title in gold text over that shot and "Written by [name]" in small white text. No intro.
- **Every scene follows the plan's rhythm:** establishing scenery, then the moment, then the aftermath, then a fade. The script marks each beat with **ESTABLISH** and **AFTERMATH** labels.
- **Character lock:** make one reference image for each main character from the CAST looks in `script.md`, and attach it to every shot.
- **Props to keep identical:** the crown necklace, the red-taped mop, the two cups (paper, then china in Scenes 30–31), Tobi's silver glasses, and the rooftop clock.
- **Glitch checklist before upload:** hands and fingers, the same face in every shot, no morphing cups or phones, readable text (the banner, the brass plaque, the CCTV timestamp, the fee notice), lip sync and eye lines, and the same outfit and lighting within each scene.
- **YouTube:** turn on the altered or synthetic content label at upload. The original script and the writer credit help keep the film inside the inauthentic-content policy.
