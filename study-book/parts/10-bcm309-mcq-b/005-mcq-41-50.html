41. Cause secondary male hypogonadism:
(a) cryptorchidism (b) eunuchidism (c) orchitis (d) Kallmann syndrome (e) all of the above
Answer: (d) Kallmann syndrome — Secondary (hypogonadotropic) hypogonadism = the fault is in the hypothalamus/pituitary. Kallmann syndrome = GnRH deficiency + anosmia. Cryptorchidism and orchitis damage the testis itself = primary hypogonadism; eunuchoidism is the clinical result of hypogonadism, not a cause.
42. Causes male hypergonadism:
(a) leydig cell tumour (b) testicular teratoma (c) hemochromatosis (d) all of the above (e) both a and b are correct
Answer: (e) Both a and b — Interstitial (Leydig) cell tumours secrete excess testosterone; some testicular teratomas/germ-cell tumours secrete hormones (e.g. hCG) that drive androgen production. Haemochromatosis (iron deposition in pituitary/testis) causes hypogonadism.

Oestrogens, progesterone and the ovarian cycle (items 43–50)

43. Most potent estrogen is:
(a) estrone (b) estriol (c) progesterone (d) estradiol (e) None of the above
Answer: (d) Estradiol (17β-oestradiol, E2) — Potency: oestradiol > oestrone (E1) > oestriol (E3). Progesterone is a progestogen, not an oestrogen.
44. Produced more during pregnancy from the placenta:
(a) estradiol (b) estriol (c) cholesterol (d) cortisol
Answer: (b) Estriol (E3) — The placenta makes oestriol from fetal adrenal DHEA-S (16-hydroxylated in fetal liver) — the "feto-placental unit". Maternal oestriol levels are therefore an index of fetal well-being.
45. Which of the following is false:
(a) estrogens are formed from aromatization of the B ring of androgens (b) three hydroxylation steps are involved in synthesis of estrogen (c) estradiol is formed directly from testosterone (d) estrone and estradiol are interconvertible (e) androstenedione is an intermediate in the synthesis of estrogen
Answer: (a) — Aromatase (CYP19) aromatises the A ring of androgens (losing C-19), not the B ring. The aromatase reaction uses three successive hydroxylations (NADPH + O2); testosterone → oestradiol and androstenedione → oestrone; E1 ⇌ E2 via 17β-HSD.
46. Roles of estrogen:
(a) induce growth of female genital organs (b) makes the body smooth (c) growth of mammary duct system (d) increased libido (e) all of the above
Answer: (e) All of the above — Oestrogens develop the uterus, tubes and vagina, give soft, smooth skin and female fat distribution, cause growth of the breast duct system (progesterone develops the lobules/alveoli), and contribute to libido.
47. Medical use of estrogen include the following except:
(a) oral contraceptives (b) treatment of prostate cancer (c) hormone replacement therapy in post-menopausal women (d) induction of ovulation (e) treatment of bulimia
Answer: (e) Treatment of bulimia — Bulimia is an eating disorder; oestrogen has no role. Uses: combined pill, HRT, androgen-dependent prostate cancer (older use). Note: (d) is also questionable — ovulation is induced by anti-oestrogens (clomiphene) or gonadotropins, not by oestrogen itself — but the lecturer's obvious "odd one out" is (e).
48. Which of the following can cause ovarian hyperactivity except:
(a) cancer of the uterus (b) cancer of the breast (c) granulosa cell tumours (d) female eunuchoidism (e) all of the above
Answer: (d) Female eunuchoidism — Eunuchoidism is ovarian under-function (hypogonadism). Granulosa (theca) cell tumours secrete excess oestrogen. (The item mixes cause and effect — uterine/breast cancers are associated with, rather than causes of, hyperoestrogenism — but (d) is clearly the intended odd one out.)
49. All the following are functions of progesterone except:
(a) increased endometrial vascularization (b) increased secretory activities of the uterus and fallopian tube (c) increased contraction of the myometrium (d) decrease in maternal immunity during pregnancy (e) inhibits milk production during pregnancy
Answer: (c) Increased contraction of the myometrium — Progesterone ("pro-gestation") quietens the myometrium so the pregnancy is not expelled; it makes the endometrium secretory and vascular, and with oestrogen inhibits lactation until delivery.
50. Ovarian menstrual cycle include the following except:
(a) HCG phase (b) Ovulation (c) follicular phase (d) luteal phase (e) all of the above
Answer: (a) HCG phase — The ovarian cycle = follicular phase → ovulation → luteal phase. hCG is a placental hormone that rescues the corpus luteum only if pregnancy occurs; there is no "HCG phase".
