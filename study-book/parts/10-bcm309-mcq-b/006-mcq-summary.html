Memory trick "Salt, Sugar, Sex — the deeper you go the sweeter it gets": glomerulosa (salt, aldosterone), fasciculata (sugar, cortisol), reticularis (sex, androgens). Carbon count "27 → 21 → 19 → 18": cholesterol → corticoids/progesterone → androgens → oestrogens. RAAS in order "Liver makes it, Kidney cuts it, Lung converts it, Adrenal answers it".
Traps the lecturer set in this paper Insulin A chain = 21 (not 30). ADH is hypothalamic (not adrenal). FSH is pituitary (not gonadal). PTH is not a thyroid hormone. Adrenaline dilates bronchi. Catecholamines increase gluconeogenesis. Rate-limiting step of steroidogenesis = desmolase (not 17-hydroxylase). Cortisol gives eosinopenia. Aromatase acts on ring A. Progesterone relaxes the myometrium.
