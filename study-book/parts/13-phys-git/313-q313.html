Differentiate between pancreatic lipase and lipoprotein lipase (6mks).

Revision tracker: Read Drew diagram Wrote from memory
What the examiner wants A comparison table covering source, site of action, substrate, products, activator/cofactor, regulation, function and deficiency. Key contrasts: colipase and bile salts versus apo C-II and heparin/insulin; dietary TG in the lumen versus TG of chylomicrons and VLDL in plasma.

Definitions

Pancreatic lipase is the main fat-digesting enzyme of pancreatic juice. In the lumen of the small intestine it hydrolyses emulsified dietary triglycerides.

Lipoprotein lipase (LPL) is an enzyme bound to the luminal surface of capillary endothelium in extrahepatic tissues. It hydrolyses the triglycerides carried in circulating chylomicrons and VLDL, so the tissues can take up the fatty acids. It is also called the "clearing factor".

Similarities (one line earns a mark)

Both are lipases that hydrolyse ester bonds of triglycerides to release fatty acids, and both need a protein cofactor: colipase for one, apo C-II for the other.

Conclusion

Pancreatic lipase digests dietary fat in the gut, while lipoprotein lipase delivers absorbed and liver-made fat to the tissues from the blood.

Understand it (plain English) Think of fat being shipped twice. First it must be unpacked in the gut so it can cross the intestinal wall; pancreatic lipase does that. The enterocyte repacks it into chylomicron "parcels" and sends them into the blood. At the tissues, LPL on the capillary wall unpacks the parcels again so the fatty acids can enter fat and muscle cells.
Memory trick "Pancreatic needs Colipase, Lipoprotein needs C-II", so both have a "C" helper. LPL = Lining of capillaries, Loves insulin.
How to write this in the exam Give two one-line definitions, then a table of at least 6 rows (6 marks), then the two-panel sketch if time allows. Put "colipase", "apo C-II", "heparan sulphate", "steatorrhoea" and "type I hyperlipoproteinaemia" in the table.
Mistakes that cost marks Saying LPL acts in the intestine or is secreted by the pancreas. Giving glycerol as the main product of pancreatic lipase (it gives 2-monoacylglycerol). Confusing LPL with hormone-sensitive lipase, which works inside adipocytes on stored fat.
