Physiology — Gastrointestinal Physiology (PIO 216)

Deglutition, GIT innervation and electrophysiology, saliva, gastric secretion and mucosal defence, bile and enterohepatic circulation, digestion and absorption of fat, protein and sugar, GI hormones, defecation, secretory immunity. Based on K. Sembulingam, Essentials of Medical Physiology (Digestive System). Bile salts, fat digestion, electrophysiology, mucosal defence and deglutition each come up in every version of the paper, so learn those first.
