With appropriate examples and interactions, define Androgens (4mks).

Revision tracker: Read Drew diagram Wrote from memory
What the examiner wants A precise definition (steroid, masculinising), 3–4 named examples with their source, and the key "interactions": conversion to DHT and oestradiol, LH/FSH control with negative feedback, synergism with FSH (spermatogenesis) and GH (pubertal growth).

Definition

Androgens are steroid hormones (C-19 steroids derived from cholesterol) that produce masculinising effects — development of male reproductive organs and male secondary sexual characters — and have a protein-anabolic action.

Examples (with source)

  1. Testosterone — main androgen; from Leydig (interstitial) cells of the testis (also small amounts from ovary and adrenal).
  2. Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) — most potent; formed from testosterone in target tissues (prostate, skin, external genitalia) by 5α-reductase.
  3. Androstenedione — testis, adrenal cortex, ovary.
  4. Dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) and its sulphate — mainly adrenal cortex (zona reticularis); weak.

Interactions

  1. Control by pituitary (feedback): LH (ICSH) stimulates Leydig cells → testosterone; testosterone inhibits GnRH and LH (negative feedback). FSH is inhibited by inhibin from Sertoli cells.
  2. Synergism with FSH: FSH + testosterone together are needed for spermatogenesis (testosterone acts via Sertoli cells, bound to androgen-binding protein).
  3. Synergism with GH: testosterone + GH produce the pubertal growth spurt and then cause epiphyseal fusion.
  4. Conversion: testosterone → DHT (5α-reductase, amplifies action) and → oestradiol (aromatase in adipose tissue and brain) — so androgens are also precursors of oestrogens.
Understand it (plain English) "Andro" = man, "gen" = produce. Any steroid that "makes male" is an androgen. Testosterone is the main one, but tissues can sharpen it into DHT (stronger) or soften it into oestradiol.
Memory trick "T-DAD" — Testosterone, DHT, Androstenedione, DHEA.
How to write this in the exam 4 marks ≈ 5 minutes: 1 mark definition, 1 mark examples, 2 marks interactions. The little diagram gets the interactions mark quickly.
Mistakes that cost marks Defining androgens as "male hormones" only (women also produce them). Forgetting that they are steroids. Writing FSH as the stimulator of Leydig cells (it is LH).
