Write on the following Genetic terms: (a) Genome (b) Allele (c) Sex Chromosome.

Revision tracker: Read Drew diagram Wrote from memory
What the examiner wants A precise definition for each term, then 4–6 expanding points with numbers (genome size, gene count), types (dominant/recessive/codominant/multiple alleles), and for sex chromosomes: X vs Y structure, SRY, Barr body/Lyon hypothesis, sex determination and abnormalities. One labelled diagram.

(a) Genome

Definition: The genome is the complete set of genetic material (DNA) of an organism, including all its genes and the non-coding DNA, present in one (haploid) set of chromosomes.

  1. Human nuclear genome — about 3.2 × 109 base pairs per haploid set, distributed on 23 chromosomes (22 autosomes + X or Y); a diploid cell has two copies (~6.4 × 109 bp).
  2. It contains roughly 20,000 protein-coding genes; coding sequences (exons) form only about 1.5% of the genome. The rest is introns, regulatory sequences and repetitive DNA (satellite DNA, LINEs, SINEs such as Alu).
  3. Mitochondrial genome — a separate small circular DNA of 16,569 bp with 37 genes; inherited only from the mother.
  4. The Human Genome Project (completed 2003) sequenced the entire human genome; it is the basis of gene mapping, diagnosis and pharmacogenomics.
  5. Related terms: genomics (study of the whole genome), genotype (genetic constitution at a particular locus).

(b) Allele

Definition: An allele (allelomorph) is one of the alternative forms of a gene that occupy the same locus on a pair of homologous chromosomes.

  1. Each person has two alleles for every autosomal gene — one maternal and one paternal.
  2. Homozygous — both alleles identical (AA or aa); heterozygous — different alleles (Aa); hemizygous — only one allele present (X-linked genes in males).
  3. Dominant allele is expressed in the heterozygote; recessive only in homozygous state; codominant alleles are both expressed (IAIB → blood group AB; HbA and HbS in sickle cell trait).
  4. Multiple alleles — more than two alleles exist in the population for one locus, though an individual carries only two (ABO: IA, IB, i; β-globin: HbA, HbS, HbC).
  5. Alleles arise by mutation; a variant present in >1% of the population is a polymorphism.
  6. Alleles segregate at gamete formation (Mendel's law of segregation).

(c) Sex chromosome

Definition: Sex chromosomes (allosomes) are the pair of chromosomes that determine the sex of an individual — XX in the female and XY in the male. They form the 23rd pair; the other 22 pairs are autosomes.

  1. Sex determination: the female produces only X-bearing ova; the male produces X- and Y-bearing sperms in equal numbers. Fertilisation by an X sperm → XX (female); by a Y sperm → XY (male). So the father determines the sex of the child; the ratio is 1 : 1.
  2. SRY gene product (testis-determining factor) makes the indifferent gonad develop into a testis; in its absence an ovary develops.
  3. Pseudoautosomal regions (PAR) at the tips of X and Y pair and cross over during male meiosis.
  4. Lyon hypothesis (X-inactivation): in females one X in each somatic cell is randomly inactivated early in embryonic life; it appears as the Barr body (sex chromatin) at the nuclear margin. Number of Barr bodies = number of X − 1. Seen as a "drumstick" in neutrophils.
  5. Abnormalities: Turner syndrome (45,X — female, no Barr body), Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY — male, one Barr body), triple X (47,XXX), 47,XYY.
  6. Genes on the X give X-linked inheritance (haemophilia, Duchenne dystrophy, G6PD deficiency); Y-linked (holandric) traits pass father → son.

Conclusion

The genome is the whole genetic material; an allele is one version of a gene at a locus; the sex chromosomes (XX/XY) determine sex through the SRY gene and are the site of X-linked inheritance.

Understand it (plain English) Genome = the whole instruction library. Gene = one recipe in it. Allele = a version of that recipe (the "HbA" recipe vs the "HbS" recipe for β-globin). You hold two versions of each recipe, one from Mum and one from Dad. The sex chromosomes are the one pair that is not identical in males: the small Y carries the SRY "switch" that says "make testes".
Memory trick "Allele = Alternative, same Address (locus)". Barr bodies: "X minus one". "Dad Decides" the sex (X or Y sperm).
How to write this in the exam Three sub-headings (a), (b), (c). Each: bold definition first, then numbered points. Give numbers (3.2 billion bp, ~20,000 genes, 46 chromosomes) — numbers show mastery. Add the X/Y comparison table and one diagram; 12–15 minutes total.
