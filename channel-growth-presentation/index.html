01 · Thumbnail · Brightness

Drag it. See the click.

Dull · how ours read nowBright · the target

On a phone a thumbnail is about the size of a thumb. Dark images disappear in the feed; bright faces and bold text get the tap.

Our thumbnail recipe

Lift exposure 15–20% Warm golden light Faces lit from the front Clear emotion on faces 2–5 words of text Dark outline on text Gold · white · red only

We can A/B test up to 3 thumbnails per video with YouTube Studio's Test & Compare.

04 · Storyline

How a 1M-view story is built

What separates a million-view script from an average one: it starts inside the conflict and never lets the tension rest.

0:00–0:30

Cold open on the conflict

Drop the viewer into the problem. No slow build-up.

Act 1

Set the contradiction

Who they are vs. who they seem: the heir posing as a driver.

Act 2

Escalate in rounds

Each scene raises the stakes and has an aftermath.

Climax

The reveal

The secret comes out at the worst possible moment.

Ending

Payoff + lesson

Consequences land. The viewer feels it.

05 · Visuals · Every scene

Every scene follows one rhythm

More context, less confusion. The viewer always knows where we are, what just happened, and what it cost.

1 · Establish

Scenery introduces the scene

A city, a house, a hospital: show where we are before anyone speaks.

2 · The scene

The moment itself

Clean, cinematic shots with the emotion clear on every face.

She stares at the message long after he leaves.
3 · Aftermath

Show the consequence

An aftermath action after every scene: the reaction, the silence, the decision.

4 · Fade

Fade to the next scene

Every scene ends on a fade, so cuts never feel abrupt.

05 · Visuals · Quality control

AI glitches: caught before upload

✓
Hands and fingersCount them. Redo any extra or melted fingers.
✓
Same face, every shotLock each character's look with a reference image.
✓
No morphing objectsCups, phones and jewellery stay solid.
✓
Readable signs and screensNo gibberish text in the background.
✓
Lip sync and eye linesMouths match the voice; people look at each other.
✓
Consistent settingSame room, same outfit, same lighting within a scene.

Why it matters for monetization

Since July 2025 YouTube's inauthentic content policy blocks mass-produced, repetitive videos from earning. Original scripts, a writer credit and clean visuals keep us on the right side. Realistic AI scenes also need YouTube's altered or synthetic content label at upload.
