# Master Image Prompt (Quiet Equity niche)

There are two parts.
- PART A: paste it into a chat with your script to get standalone image prompts for every beat.
- PART B: a fill-in template for writing a single image prompt by hand.

---

## PART A: Script → image prompts

```
You are the visual director for a faceless YouTube channel that tells cinematic second-person money stories. Turn the script below into image prompts for EVERY beat, without skipping a single word.

BEAT RULES
- Split the script in order into beats of 13 words or fewer (3–5 seconds of narration each).
- Split long sentences at natural pauses. Never merge text across level changes.
- The beats together must reproduce the script word for word.
- Number every beat.

LOCKED VISUAL STYLE (put it inside EVERY prompt)
Flat 2D cartoon webcomic illustration, bold clean black outlines, flat cel shading, desaturated slate-blue and gray palette with muted olive, rust, mustard and maroon accents, detailed lived-in background, semi-chibi characters with oversized heads, layered foreground, mid-ground and background, 16:9 frame with the bottom 15% kept clear of important detail (for captions).

LOCKED PROTAGONIST (describe him fully in EVERY prompt he appears in)
A young man with an oversized round head, short dark-brown side-parted hair, thin rectangular glasses, large half-lidded unimpressed eyes and a small flat mouth, always deadpan. He is always the darkest value in the frame.
Outfit by wealth tier:
- Broke or low: plain charcoal hoodie
- Middle: charcoal crewneck sweater
- Upper-middle: charcoal quarter-zip pullover
- Rich and above: dark tailored jacket over a charcoal tee
Flashbacks to his poor past show him in the charcoal hoodie, thinner and more tired.

LIGHTING BY TIER (it warms as wealth rises)
- Lowest: harsh cool fluorescent light with a faint flicker, blue-gray shadows
- Low: flat cool fluorescent light, washed-out tones
- Middle: soft clean daylight
- Upper-middle: soft warm daylight
- Rich: warm natural light with amber accents
- Very rich: warm hotel-like lamp pools against cool blue windows
- Top: rich golden light with deep soft shadows
Flashbacks: desaturated, cold.

COMPOSITION RULES
- Mostly eye-level medium and medium-wide shots, with the protagonist off-center in the foreground and separated from the crowd.
- Supporting characters are semi-realistic cartoon adults of mixed ethnicities with EXPRESSIVE faces (laughing, angry, tired), contrasting his deadpan.
- Use close-up inserts for phones, bills, screens and receipts, with short legible text and exact numbers from the script.
- Use one-point perspective interiors, story props in view, and occasional split-screen or cutaway cross-sections for rich-versus-poor contrast.
- Metaphor beats (abstract lines) get a literal visual metaphor, such as a tower of envelopes or price tags floating over objects.
- Outro "Somewhere right now, someone…" beats use a NEW stranger in the same settings as Level 1.

STANDALONE RULE
Each prompt must fully describe the subject, environment, action, camera, lighting and mood, name the visual style, and give every named character a short appearance note (e.g. "the nurse is a tired middle-aged woman in light-blue scrubs"). Never write "same as before" or rely on another prompt.

OUTPUT FORMAT (for every beat)
Beat [#] (~[seconds]s)
[Script Segment Text]: "<exact words>"
Image Prompt: <one standalone paragraph: scene + characters + style + camera + lighting + mood>
Camera Angle: …
Lighting: …
Mood: …
Action: …

If the output is long, continue in batches until the last beat. Never summarize or skip beats.

SCRIPT:
[PASTE FULL SCRIPT HERE]
```

```
[Character notes for anyone else in the frame.]

Camera: [SHOT + ANGLE]. Lighting: [TIER LIGHTING]. Mood: [2–3 WORDS].
```
