# Ink Explainer Style Guide (stick-figure explainer look)

A visual style kit modeled on the references in `references/`. Videos in this style move between two kinds of shot:

1. **Story scenes:** a round-headed stick figure living through the topic (a caveman on the savanna, a modern person on a city street).
2. **Diagram inserts:** clean, flat infographic cards that explain one idea (a world map with dots, a labeled body pose, one object with a warning arrow).

A typical pattern: about 60–70% story scenes and 30–40% diagram inserts, cutting to a diagram whenever the narration states a fact, number, place or body mechanic.

---

## 1. The character (the "Ink" figure)

| Trait | Lock it as |
|---|---|
| Head | Large perfect white circle, about 1/3 of body height, thick black outline |
| Body | Single thin black line torso, stick arms and legs, small round dot or circle hands |
| Face | Minimal: two small black dot eyes, simple line eyebrows, small line or open mouth. Emotion comes from eyebrows and mouth only |
| Default expression | Neutral and blank (`scene-caveman-savanna.jpg`) |
| Other expressions | Worried (tilted brows, wavy grimace, sweat), exhausted (droopy eyes, slumped neck), surprised (raised brows, round "o" mouth) |
| Posture | Carries the emotion: upright and still = neutral, hunched and dragging = struggle, mid-stride with motion lines = urgency |

**Costume by era (one item max, the body stays a stick):**
- Prehistoric: messy shaggy brown hair, ragged brown fur tunic with a belt, wooden spear with a stone tip
- Modern: no hair or a simple tuft, no clothes (plain stick body), optional prop (phone, bag, coffee)
- Other eras: one costume piece only (a hat, a toga, a helmet) so the silhouette stays a stick figure

> Do **not** copy the other channel's bottom-right logo. Keep the bottom-right corner clear and put **your own** channel bug there in editing.

---

## 2. Story scene look

- **Line:** bold, slightly wobbly hand-inked black outlines, like marker on paper
- **Fill:** flat colors, no gradients on characters; soft flat shading only in skies and ground
- **Backgrounds:** simple and wide with low detail. One horizon line, a few props (dead tree, sign, buildings)
- **Palette per mood:**
  - Calm or neutral: pale sky blue `#AFD3DC` over warm tan grass `#C99A5B`
  - Hardship or drought: storm gray sky `#8A8D96` over dusty brown cracked earth `#9C7A5E`
  - Modern or city: light cyan sky `#CDEFF7`, cream and light-gray blocky buildings `#F3EBCB` / `#CFCFCF`, gray pavement
- **Composition:** figure centered or slightly left, full body, eye-level wide shot, lots of empty space. Prop on the opposite third (tree, sign, spear)
- **Motion cues** are drawn in: short curved lines by swinging arms, dust puffs at feet, sweat drops

## 3. Diagram insert look

- **Background:** clean off-white `#FAFAF7` or warm cream `#F2ECE0`
- **Line:** smooth, even, thick black vector outline (cleaner than the story scenes)
- **Colors:** muted olive green `#8A9A5B`, brick red `#B5434A` for highlights, charcoal `#3C3F44` for text
- **Text:** ONE short label in bold all-caps rounded sans-serif (Montserrat or Nunito Black style), e.g. `GLOBAL HELP`, `CAUTION`, `UPRIGHT`, `GRAVITY`
- **Label placement:** top-right corner, top-center in a dark rounded pill, or next to an arrow pointing at the key detail
- **Arrows:** thick charcoal or red straight arrows with solid triangle heads
- **Human figures in diagrams** are semi-realistic flat vector people (like `diagram-labeled-figure.webp`), NOT the stick figure. Use them for anatomy, posture and body mechanics
- **Maps:** olive-green land with black coastline and dots in brick red for locations

## 4. Shot menu

| Narration type | Shot to use |
|---|---|
| Story moment, emotion | Story scene: stick figure, full body, simple background |
| "Back then..." / ancient past | Story scene: prehistoric stick figure |
| "Today..." / "You..." | Story scene: modern stick figure in a city |
| A place, spread, "around the world" | Diagram: map with red dots |
| A danger, a myth, "be careful" | Diagram: one object + arrow + `CAUTION` / `MYTH` label |
| Body, posture, biology | Diagram: vector person + arrow + one-word label |
| A number or stat | Diagram: big bold number on cream with one icon |
| Comparison | Diagram: split frame, two icons, `THEN` vs `NOW` labels |

## 5. Technical

- 16:9, 1920×1080 (or 2000×1125 as in the references)
- Keep the bottom 12% clear for captions and the bottom-right corner clear for your logo
- One idea per image. If you need two labels, use two images
- Hold each image 3–5 seconds and cut on the narration's beat
